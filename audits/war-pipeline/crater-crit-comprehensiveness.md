## SHOULD-ADD (deepening, not gaps that distort)

1. **Confederate countermining / suspicion (near MUST-ADD).** The draft's MEANWHILE says
   the South Carolinians "had no idea" powder was under them. In fact the Confederates
   *suspected* a mine and dug listening shafts/countermines that failed to find Pleasants's
   gallery (it ran deeper than they probed). Including this (a) is more accurate than
   "no idea," and (b) heightens the drama and the engineering achievement. One clause would
   fix it. **SHOULD-ADD (verify the countermine detail before printing).**

2. **Cemetery Hill / Jerusalem Plank Road as the named objective.** The draft refers to
   "the high ground behind the line" and "the road into Petersburg" but never names
   **Cemetery Hill** or the **Jerusalem Plank Road** (both in fact-pack §4). Naming the
   actual objective makes "the breakthrough thrown away" concrete and matches the maps.
   **SHOULD-ADD.**

3. **Beauregard.** P.G.T. Beauregard commanded the Petersburg defenses under Lee and is in
   the pack (§2) but absent from the draft. Not essential — Mahone/Johnson carry the
   Confederate tactical story — but a one-word nod would be more complete. **SHOULD-ADD (low
   priority).**

4. **The grimmest McClellan detail is omitted by choice.** The "let me kill one more" /
   pocket-knife throat-cutting line is documented but not used. This is a legitimate
   editorial restraint (avoiding spectacle), not a gap — **no action**; flagged only so the
   integrator knows the omission is deliberate and defensible.

## Fact-pack blind spots worth noting (independent of the draft)

- **The pack's "no idea / most precisely doomed" framing** glosses the real Confederate
  countermining effort — see SHOULD-ADD #1. The pack should not have implied total
  obliviousness.
- **Engaged-strength contradiction** (pack §2 ~8,500/~6,100 headline vs ABT-CAL/EV
  ~16,500/~9,500 wider sector): the draft correctly *avoided* printing an engaged headline
  and leaned on the well-agreed casualties. Good call; the pack's contradiction is sidestepped
  rather than inherited. No action needed in the draft.
- **Lines-distance contradiction** in the pack (§1.5 ~130 yd vs §4 ~100 yd) propagated into
  the draft as a printed "130 yards." Flagged in the fact-check gate as MUST-FIX.

# Fig 3 — Secession map replacement

Replaced the faint, hard-to-read Charles Paullin (1932) secession map with a
clean, high-contrast color-coded version that reads clearly at phone size.

**Installed file:** `public/war-img/improvising-a-war-fig3-secession-map.jpg`
(true format: PNG, 1280×786, saved under the .jpg name so the existing `<img>`
path keeps working; browsers sniff the real type).

**Source:** rasterized PNG render (1280px) of `File:US_Secession_map_1861.svg`
on Wikimedia Commons.

## Caption (house voice)
The country splits apart, 1860 to 1861. Dark red states seceded before Fort Sumter (the lower South), bright red went after (the upper South), the blue states stayed in the Union, and the four loyal slave states (Kentucky, Maryland, Missouri, Delaware) sit in yellow. Gray is unsettled territory.

## MUST-FIX

1. **"roughly one in four of the enlisted men aboard ship were Black" — WRONG for the West Gulf Blockading Squadron specifically (§5 / "what-it-won").** The draft attributes the 1-in-4 figure to *Farragut's own* West Gulf Blockading Squadron. NARA's "Black Men in Navy Blue" **Table 2** gives the West Gulf squadron at **20 percent (one in five)** for the second quarter of 1864 — the LOWEST of the representative squadrons. The "one quarter" figure is the **Navy-wide** rule-of-thumb, not the West Gulf number. (The article explicitly notes the Black proportion in Farragut's squadron "fell well short" of the Mississippi and North Atlantic squadrons.) The fact-pack itself seeded this error (§8 asserts "roughly 1 in 4" for the West Gulf squadron). **Fix:** either (a) change the West Gulf figure to "roughly one in five," or (b) keep "one in four" but attribute it to the Navy as a whole, not to Farragut's squadron. The Navy-wide ~18,000 men / ~20 percent figure in the same sentence IS correct and verified — only the squadron-specific 1-in-4 claim is wrong.

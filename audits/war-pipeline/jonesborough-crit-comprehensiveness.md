## SHOULD-ADD (enrichment, not required)

1. **A one-line locator for the reader who's lost in the geography.** The prose names
   the Flint River (west), the Macon & Western (through town), Lovejoy's (south),
   McDonough Road (north out of Atlanta) — but never plants the single orienting
   sentence "Atlanta is ~20 miles north; everything here happens south of it." §1
   implies it; one explicit compass sentence (or the map doing this job) would help.
   SHOULD-ADD.

2. **What capture *meant* for Govan's men.** The draft lands the image of a brigade
   "scooped up off the field and marched to the rear" but stops there. A half-line
   on the prisoners' fate (POW camps / later exchange — Govan himself was exchanged)
   would close the human loop the draft opens. The pack flags Govan's exchange as
   [VERIFY]; safe to add only if confirmed. SHOULD-ADD (low priority).

3. **A civilian/Atlanta-population beat.** The section is entirely army-and-railroad
   plus the (excellent) self-emancipation thread. The fall of Atlanta also meant
   Sherman's subsequent expulsion of the civilian population — a notable human
   consequence one rung beyond this battle. It is arguably out of scope for a
   battle section (it belongs to the post-fall occupation), so this is a genuine
   SHOULD-ADD-or-defer, not a gap. The self-emancipating thousands already carry
   the human weight; do not let an Atlanta-civilian beat dilute Hook A.

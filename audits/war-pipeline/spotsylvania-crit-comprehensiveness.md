## SHOULD-ADD (enrichment, none essential)

1. **Burnside's IX Corps on the Fredericksburg Road (May 10).** The fact pack §2/§4 notes Burnside
   advanced down the Fredericksburg Road and "found a gap in Lee's line May 10 but entrenched instead
   of exploiting it." The draft never mentions Burnside or the IX Corps at all. This is a defensible
   selective cut — Burnside's inaction was a non-event — but it is a notable lost-opportunity beat
   (a *second* near-breakthrough squandered the same day as Upton's). **SHOULD-ADD** one line if room,
   because "two cracks in Lee's line on May 10, both wasted for lack of follow-through" sharpens the
   campaign's recurring theme (Grant could break in but not pour through). Optional.

2. **The Po River / "Battle of the Po" (May 10).** Pack §4: Hancock's II Corps crossed the Po on
   May 10 (the western-flank probe) and Barlow's rearguard was roughly handled withdrawing back across
   it. The draft omits the Po entirely. Again defensible as selective — the Po action was minor — but
   it is the one named sub-engagement the draft drops, and it explains why Hancock's corps was available
   and positioned for the May 12 apex assault. **SHOULD-ADD** only if the author wants the western
   flank on the map; not essential to the narrative spine.

3. **"North of the James River" qualifier on the 23rd USCT first-combat claim.** (Also flagged in the
   fact gate.) The precise, well-attested superlative is "first directed combat between USCT and the
   Army of Northern Virginia **north of the James River**." The draft states it without the qualifier.
   For comprehensiveness/precision, **SHOULD-ADD** the qualifier so the claim is exactly the one the
   marker makes. Also optional: print the place-name "**Alrich Farm**" once for searchability/anchoring.

## Blind spots in the FACT PACK itself (panel brief asked for these)

1. **The pack pre-judged the 23rd USCT location as a mismatch** ("the draft places... but the fact
   pack says Alrich Farm"). In fact the pack's own §8 *equates* Alrich Farm with the Catharpin/Orange
   Plank intersection. The "mismatch" was illusory. The pack could have stated explicitly that Alrich
   Farm = that crossroads, to forestall exactly the confusion the panel brief inherited. Minor doc hygiene.
2. **Burnside (IX Corps) and the Po River action are in the pack but flagged as low-priority**; the
   pack correctly carries them as available-if-wanted. No defect, but the pack does not signal that
   the May 10 *double* near-breakthrough (Upton + Burnside's gap) is a thematic pairing — that's a
   missed framing cue for the author. Minor.
3. **The 23rd USCT roster** in the pack §8 omits "the city of Fredericksburg," which ABT's article
   includes. The draft inherited the omission. (Cross-listed in the fact gate as SHOULD-FIX.)
4. **No map-beat guidance gap** — the pack §4 map orientation is thorough and self-consistent; no
   comprehensiveness blind spot there.

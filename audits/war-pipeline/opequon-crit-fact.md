## G. "Whirling through Winchester" — the contested attribution (special scrutiny)

The draft PARAPHRASES it and explicitly tells the reader the sources can't agree who
said/wrote it (Sheridan vs. chief of staff; telegram to Grant vs. Washington). It is
NOT printed in quotation marks attributed to a named speaker. **This is exactly
correct.** My research confirms the attribution is genuinely inconsistent: Warfare
History Network calls it "Sheridan's memorable phrase," while the telegram wording
("We have just sent them whirling through Winchester…") is variously credited to
Sheridan's staff/chief of staff in a dispatch to Washington, and Sheridan's own
telegram to Grant uses different words ("charged and carried every position…"). The
draft's handling — signature *image* of the winning side, attribution openly flagged
as unsettled — is the safe and honest path. **VERIFIED handling. No fix.**

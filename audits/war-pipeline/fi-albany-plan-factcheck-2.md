# Fact-Check 2 (focused re-check) — "The Albany Congress & the Plan of Union"

Scope: only the material ADDED or CHANGED in the revision (the 1753 Mohawk-break opening, the
Kayaderosseras grievance, the "150 Iroquois" attendance figure, the Wyoming Valley currency).
Independently web-verified — not trusting the draft or the pack.

## OVERALL: PASS

All four checked items are CONFIRMED. No ❌ remains. The two required corrections — "about 150
Iroquois delegates" and "in 1753" (not "June 1753") — were both applied in the final draft.

## A. The 1753 Mohawk-break material — ✅ CONFIRMED

- **"in 1753," NOT "June 1753"** — ✅ CONFIRMED applied. Draft line 13 reads "In 1753 the Mohawk
  leader Theyanoguin… led a delegation of 17 Mohawk leaders past Albany and straight to Governor
  George Clinton in New York City." The cited source (Wikipedia — Covenant Chain) dates the break
  to **1753 only, no month**. (A loose web snippet says "June 1753," but it is not in the cited
  sources; the draft correctly omits the month.) Delegation of 17 (Hendrick + 16) confirmed.
  Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Covenant_Chain ;
  https://www.newyorkalmanack.com/2022/01/albany-colonial-wars-in-northeast/
- **The "Covenant Chain is broken" walkout to Clinton in NYC** — ✅ CONFIRMED. They went directly
  to Governor George Clinton and the NY provincial council and declared the chain broken.
- **Verbatim pull-quote** — ✅ CONFIRMED wording and attribution. The quote "...the Covenant Chain
  is broken between you and us. So brother you are not to expect to hear of me any more, and
  Brother we desire to hear no more of you." matches the passage attributed to Chief Hendrick to
  Governor Clinton, 1753 (Wikipedia — Covenant Chain, via Howard Zinn). Provenance is a mediated
  18th-c. transcription quoted through a modern secondary work; the draft's "as recorded" framing
  elsewhere covers it. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Covenant_Chain
- **Causal link (break → Board of Trade ordered the congress to repair the chain)** — ✅ CONFIRMED.
  Wikipedia states plainly "The Albany Congress was called to help repair the chain"; New York
  Almanack confirms the Board of Trade ordered the colonial governors to meet the Haudenosaunee in
  response. Draft line 17 matches.
- **Identity = Theyanoguin (c.1692–1755), NOT the 1710 London Hendrick** — ✅ CONFIRMED. Wikipedia
  explicitly separates Hendrick Theyanoguin (c.1691/92–1755, Canajoharie, died at Lake George) from
  the older Hendrick Tejonihokarawa (c.1660–c.1735), the Four-Mohawk-Kings/1710-London man. The
  draft uses "born around 1692," "Mohawk leader," and never gives him the London visit.
  Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hendrick_Theyanoguin

## B. The Kayaderosseras Patent — ✅ CONFIRMED; draft correctly hedges, does NOT over-commit

- The "small farms deed ballooned into hundreds of thousands of acres of Mohawk land" framing is
  supported: original Mohawk claim = the sale was for "three small farms," with the patent
  multiplying boundaries to roughly five-by-fourteen-by-eight-by-twenty miles, "upwards of 800,000
  acres" per New York Almanack (Wikipedia gives 406,000). The draft says "a few small farms… had,
  on paper, ballooned into a claim of hundreds of thousands of acres" — accurate and inside both
  estimates. ✅ No over-commit.
- **Draft does NOT pin an exact grant year** — ✅ CONFIRMED (line 19 says only "a land grant in the
  Saratoga region"). Grant year is contested (1701 Wikipedia / 1708 Almanack), so omitting it is
  correct.
- Saratoga region + denounced as fraudulent + still raised at the 1754 congress — ✅ all supported.
  Source: https://www.newyorkalmanack.com/2018/07/kayaderosseras-patent-settlement-a-short-history/ ;
  https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kayaderossera_patent

## C. Attendance figure "about 150 Iroquois delegates" — ✅ CONFIRMED applied

- Draft line 39: "About 150 Iroquois delegates came to Albany." The unsupported "~200 Native
  people" is gone, and no invented "several hundred camped" phrasing was added. "About 150 Iroquois"
  is the source-supported headline figure.
  Source: https://www.newyorkalmanack.com/2022/01/the-albany-congress-of-1754-native-people-colonists-the-monarchy/ ;
  https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Albany_Congress

## D. Wyoming Valley deed price "£2,000 (New York currency)" — ✅ CONFIRMED

- Draft line 76: "for about £2,000 (New York currency)." Confirmed: "The consideration for the deed
  was 2,000 pounds in New York money" (Connecticut Sons of the American Revolution / Susquehannah
  Company sources); £2,000 figure corroborated by Connecticut History and the Pitt deed record. The
  "(British pounds)" reading is corrected. ✅
  Source: https://connecticuthistory.org/the-susquehanna-settlers/ ;
  https://historicpittsburgh.org/collection/iroquois-land-deed

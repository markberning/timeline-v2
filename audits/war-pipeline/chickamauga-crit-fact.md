## ❌ MUST-FIX

**1. "Bragg abandoned Chattanooga on September 9, 1863" — WRONG DATE (off by one).**
- Draft §the-maneuver: *"Bragg abandoned Chattanooga on September 9, 1863, and
  pulled south."*
- Correct: **Bragg EVACUATED Chattanooga on September 8, 1863**; the Union army
  **OCCUPIED it on September 9**. The draft has merged two events onto the wrong
  date. The abandonment is the 8th.
- Fix: change to "abandoned Chattanooga on September 8, 1863" (or "evacuated…on
  September 8; the Union marched in the next day").
- Source: Wikipedia *Battle of Chickamauga* / *Chattanooga campaign* ("On
  September 8…Bragg evacuated Chattanooga…The Union army occupied Chattanooga on
  September 9").

That is the only hard factual error found. Everything else verifies, or is a
should-fix / confirmation below.

## ✅ CONFIRMED (all [VERIFY] flags resolved)

- **Strength ~60,000 US / ~65,000 CS** — CONFIRMED (WP/ABT/NGE). The 125,000 figure

- **Casualties** ~16,170 US / ~18,454 CS, combined ~34,624; second-bloodiest battle
  after Gettysburg; Confederates won the field but lost more men — ALL CONFIRMED
  (WP/ABT). Cozzens day-1 estimate (6,000–9,000 CS / ~7,000 US for Sep 19 alone),
  correctly labeled single-day — CONFIRMED.
- **Wood's order**, exact wording — CONFIRMED. Full verbatim per WP: *"The general
  commanding directs that you close up on Reynolds as fast as possible, and support
  him."* Draft uses the gist *"close up on Reynolds as fast as possible, and support
  him"* — accurate. The "Wood recently reprimanded for slow obedience" framing is
  supported (he had been rebuked earlier that morning for a delayed move) — OK.
- **Polk's attack ~9:30 a.m.; Longstreet's column ~11:10 a.m.; eight brigades, five
  lines deep** — CONFIRMED (WP).
- **Thomas as Virginian / former slaveholder who chose the Union** — CONFIRMED and
  the draft's framing is fair. Born Southampton County VA into a slaveholding
  planter family (15 enslaved people by 1829); chose the Union; relatives disowned
  him. Draft's "a Virginian and a former slaveholder…who chose the Union over his
  native South" is accurate. (Sources: Encyclopedia Virginia, WP *George Henry
  Thomas*.)
- **Ben Hardin Helm relation to Lincoln** — CONFIRMED exactly. Helm married **Emilie
  Todd, half-sister of Mary Todd Lincoln**, making him **Lincoln's brother-in-law**;
  killed (mortally wounded) at Chickamauga; Lincoln mourned him personally ("I never
  saw Mr. Lincoln more moved"). Draft wording correct. (WP *Benjamin Hardin Helm*.)
- **Deshler and Preston Smith killed** — CONFIRMED (WP/ABT/History).
- **"Roughly ten Confederate generals killed or wounded"** — CONFIRMED. ABT/WP:
  ten Confederate generals killed or wounded. "Roughly ten" is accurate.
- **"Rock of Chickamauga" provenance** — CONFIRMED handled correctly. Garfield's
  battlefield dispatch ("Thomas standing like a rock…"); NO newspaper use of "Rock
  of Chickamauga" before 1869 (Civil War Monitor); the 1870 memorial oration
  presumes the phrase is familiar. The draft does NOT assert spot-coinage and uses
  the sourced 1870 line *"He was, indeed, the 'Rock of Chickamauga.'"* — verbatim
  match. The folklore caution is correctly applied. GOOD.
- **"River of death" etymology** — CONFIRMED handled correctly. Draft frames it as
  folklore, not a literal Cherokee translation, and points to the Chickasaw
  *chokma* ("be good") derivation. Matches WP. (Minor: WP attributes *chokma* to
  Chickasaw, which the draft does; good.)
- **Distance ~12 miles** — ACCEPTABLE. WP/ABT loosely say "~20 mi" (to Bragg's
  LaFayette camp); the battlefield itself is most-cited at ~10–12 mi south of
  Chattanooga (NPS-area sources). Draft's "~12 miles" / "Twelve miles to the
  northwest" is defensible. Pin the same number across prose + cards.
- **Tullahoma campaign June 24 – July 3, 1863** — CONFIRMED (WP *Tullahoma
  campaign*).
- **Wilder's Lightning Brigade, Spencer repeating rifles, Alexander's Bridge** —

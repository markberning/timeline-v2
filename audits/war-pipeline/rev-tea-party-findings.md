# CRITIC FINDINGS — "The Tea Party & the Intolerable Acts" (all 4 critics: PASS-WITH-FIXES)

## FACT-CHECK
- **BLOCKER:** Wedderburn's "true incendiary" and "first mover and prime conductor of this whole contrivance" are FUSED into one quoted string with "and" as if a single verbatim utterance. They were separate rhetorical moments. Separate into two attributed phrases or paraphrase one.
- **SHOULD-FIX (pack error):** Virginia: Dunmore dissolved the House of Burgesses **May 26, 1774**; the Raleigh Tavern meeting + congress proposal was **May 27**. Draft collapses both under 27 May. Separate the two days (or blur: "in late May").
- **SHOULD-FIX (pack error):** Charleston: the ship *London* ARRIVED **December 2, 1773**; **December 3** was the town meeting. Fix the date or decouple.
- **SHOULD-FIX:** Samuel Adams reported-speech paraphrase: use "could do nothing **further** to save the country" (attested wording), not "more."
- **NIT:** Adams pull-quote capitalizes "There" implying sentence start; original reads "Then there was no other Alternative…" — prepend "Then" or use ellipsis.

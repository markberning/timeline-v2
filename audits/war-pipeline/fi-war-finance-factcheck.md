## ❌ WRONG / MISQUOTED

### 1. Franklin Examination quote — opening clause is altered, presented as verbatim
**Draft (line 130):**
> "Before 1763, there was no objection to the right of [Parliament] laying duties to regulate commerce; but a right to lay internal taxes was never supposed to be in parliament, as we are not represented there." — Benjamin Franklin, 1766

**Problem:** The opening clause is not Franklin's wording. The verbatim text of the 1766 Examination reads:
> "I had **never heard any objection** to the right of laying duties to regulate commerce; but a right to lay internal taxes was never supposed to be in parliament, as we are not represented there."

The draft's "Before 1763, there was no objection to the right of [Parliament] laying duties..." is a paraphrase silently dressed as a verbatim quotation. The bracketed "[Parliament]" and the invented "Before 1763, there was no objection" replace Franklin's actual "I had never heard any objection." The substance (external duties accepted, internal taxes rejected for want of representation) is accurate, but a quotation-marked line must match the source.
**Note:** This error originates in the FACT PACK (§8, line 120), which carries the same altered wording marked "VERBATIM." The fact pack is wrong here too.
**Source:** [Examination of Benjamin Franklin before the House of Commons, Feb. 13, 1766](https://www.revolutionary-war-and-beyond.com/examination-of-benjamin-franklin-before-the-house-of-commons-february-13-1766.html); [Founders Online](https://founders.archives.gov/documents/Franklin/01-13-02-0035)
**Fix:** Either quote Franklin verbatim ("I had never heard any objection to the right of laying duties to regulate commerce; but a right to lay internal taxes was never supposed to be in parliament, as we are not represented there.") or remove the quotation marks and present it as a paraphrase.

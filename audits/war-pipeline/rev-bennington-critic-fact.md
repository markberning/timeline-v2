## SHOULD-FIX (3)

1. **"exactly two months after Bennington" — off by a day (pack's own slip).**
   Draft line 208: "On October 17, 1777, exactly two months after Bennington, Burgoyne
   surrendered his army at Saratoga." Aug 16 → Oct 17 is two months **and one day**.
   The pack (§1, §6) says "exactly 2 months," so the draft traced it faithfully — but
   it's a date-arithmetic error, and the draft's own Locator 1 caption already has the
   correct rendering ("Two months later, almost to the day"), so the page currently
   contradicts itself.
   **Fix:** in the section-3 prose use "two months later, almost to the day" (or "two
   months and a day after Bennington"); note the pack error for the pack's maintainer.

2. **Quoted Molly-Stark variant not byte-exact to the pack.**
   Draft line 185: 'The polished versions came later and multiplied ("tonight Molly
   Stark sleeps a widow!" among them)'. The pack's verified variants are "…or **this
   night** Molly Stark sleeps a widow!", "to-night they must be ours or Mollie Stark's
   a widow", and "Tonight the American flag floats from yonder hill, or Molly Stark
   sleeps a widow." The draft's wording inside quotation marks matches none of them
   exactly. Low stakes (the page's whole point is that variants multiply), but
   anything inside quote marks must be pack-exact.
   **Fix:** quote a pack variant verbatim — e.g. ("this night Molly Stark sleeps a
   widow!" among them) — or drop the quote marks and paraphrase.

3. **Troiani caption "(2000s)" — the decade is not solid.**
   Draft lines 93–94 caption the Troiani painting as "A modern (2000s) painting." The
   pack instructed exactly that, but external dating is murky: Bridgeman Images and
   HistoryNet list a Don Troiani "Battle of Bennington" as a **1986** oil on canvas,
   and the Commons file's 2004 date may be file metadata rather than the painting's
   creation year. "Modern, not period art" is unambiguously true; the decade is not.
   **Fix:** caption as "A modern painting, not period art…" without the decade (also
   flag to the pack: §8.4's "(2000s)" should be softened).

## NIT (4)

1. **Breymann escape fraction inconsistent across surfaces.** His commander card
   (line 44) says "escaped in the dark with perhaps two-thirds of his men"; the
   section-2 prose (line 193) says "two-thirds to three-quarters" (the pack range).
   The card silently takes the low end. Align the card to "two-thirds to
   three-quarters" or "perhaps two-thirds or more."
2. **"North of Bennington, at Manchester" (MEANWHILE, line 170).** The direction is
   not in the pack (the pack never places Manchester relative to Bennington).
   Web-verifiable as true (Manchester VT is ~20 mi north), so not a blocker, but it
   is technically an out-of-pack assertion; either keep (it's correct) or trim to
   "At Manchester, in the Grants."
3. **"The dragoons stood the whole weight of the attack for the full two hours"
   (line 191).** The pack gives the 2-hour action (Stark's quote) and the dragoons
   holding until ammunition gave out; "the whole weight … the full two hours" is a
   mild intensification of "every post collapsed inward onto the redoubt." Acceptable
   dramatization; could soften to "stood at the center of it for two hours."
4. **Locator 2: the second-fight dot shares Sancoick's exact coordinate** (both
   42.95, −73.35). The pack says to place the second-fight marker *between* the mill
   and the hilltop; nudging it slightly east (e.g. ~42.945, −73.34) would keep the
   two dots from stacking and better match the instruction. Both are `approx`-tagged,
   so this is cartographic, not factual.

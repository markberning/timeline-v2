## Perspective / coverage assessment
- **Both sides represented:** Union (Banks, Lee, Ransom, Emory, A.J. Smith), Confederate (Taylor, Mouton, Polignac, Walker, Green). Side-tags present on first mention per section.
- **The enslaved are present, not just "cotton."** The draft repeatedly returns the cotton to the people who grew it and pairs it with the Corps d'Afrique freedmen-in-blue — a genuine perspective beyond the two white armies. Strong.
- **The mechanism is taught, not just narrated** (the road → strung-out column → local superiority). This is exactly the "explain mechanisms" house rule.

## SHOULD-ADD (upside only, none blocking)
1. **Sherman's loan / the clock.** The factpack notes A.J. Smith's veterans were on loan from Sherman only until the end of April — a deadline that pressured the whole operation and helps explain why Banks couldn't recover after a "mere" tactical setback. The draft mentions A.J. Smith but not the borrowed-clock. One clause would deepen the "why the campaign was so brittle" point. SHOULD-ADD.
2. **Which Union corps bled.** The factpack notes the XIII Corps suffered most and the XIX Corps relatively little (which is *why* Emory's fresh XIX Corps division could backstop). The draft has Emory's fresh division but doesn't draw the contrast that the wrecked element and the intact backstop were different corps. A single phrase would sharpen the "fresh line" logic. SHOULD-ADD.
3. **Taylor himself as a slaveholding planter.** Available and on-theme (see Framing); would tighten the cause hook on the Confederate side, mirroring Mouton. SHOULD-ADD.

## Fact-pack blind spots (noted, not the draft's fault)
- The fact pack under-specifies the **USCT combat footprint** (84th/92nd at Pleasant Hill, not Mansfield). The draft wisely did NOT claim USCT combat at Mansfield, so no error resulted — but a future integrator should keep "served in the campaign," not "fought at Mansfield."
- The fact pack's "Moss plantation," "Keachi," "Walker's Greyhounds," "Polecat" were all [VERIFY]; the draft omitted them. The web actually confirms "Prince Polecat" and "Walker's Greyhounds" — minor color the integrator could add later, but the omission is the safe call and not a comprehensiveness gap.

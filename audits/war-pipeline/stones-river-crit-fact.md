## ❌ MUST-FIX

1. **Garesché's rank — draft says "Colonel," he was a LIEUTENANT COLONEL.**
   Draft §the-dawn-blow and ledger #9/#13 tag "Colonel Julius P. Garesché (North)." Wikipedia (the fact pack's own keyed WP source) states he "was promoted to the rank of lieutenant colonel and assistant adjutant general on July 17, 1862" and labels him "Lieutenant Colonel Julius Peter Garesché" at his death on Dec 31, 1862. Britannica/secondary sources concur ("lieutenant colonel in the regular army"). The fact pack itself mis-listed him as Colonel; the draft inherited the error.
   FIX: change "Colonel Julius P. Garesché (North)" → "Lieutenant Colonel Julius P. Garesché (North)."
   Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Julius_Peter_Garesch%C3%A9

2. **Lincoln quote — one extra comma vs. the verbatim Collected Works text** (the draft presents this as `[VERIFIED — full text]`, so it must be exact).
   Draft §the-cost-and-the-meaning prints: "…you gave us a hard earned victory, **which,** had there been a defeat instead, the nation could scarcely have lived over."
   The authoritative Basler *Collected Works of Abraham Lincoln* (vol. 6, Lincoln to Rosecrans, Aug 31, 1863) reads: "…you gave us a hard earned **victory which,** had there been a defeat instead, the nation could scarcely have lived over." — i.e. **no comma after "victory."**
   FIX: delete the comma after "victory" so the verbatim quote matches the source it is attributed to. (Everything else in the quote — wording, attribution, Aug 31 1863 date — is correct and verbatim.)
   Source: Collected Works of Abraham Lincoln, vol. 6 (UMich quod.lib.umich.edu/l/lincoln/lincoln6).

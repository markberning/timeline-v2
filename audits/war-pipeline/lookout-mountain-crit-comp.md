## MUST-ADD (essential gaps — should not ship without adjudication)

### MUST-ADD 1 — The total lunar eclipse that screened the Confederate withdrawal
On the night of Nov 24→25 a **total lunar eclipse** blacked out a brilliant full moon
during the very hours Bragg's men were slipping off the mountain by the Summertown road.
This is among the most-cited atmospheric facts of the battle: ABT, Wikipedia, and veteran
postwar accounts (both sides) all carry it — "a brilliant moon, which slipped into the
blackness of a total lunar eclipse, screening the Confederate withdrawal" (Wikipedia,
quoting veteran writings). Confederate troops read it as an omen of doom; Union men read
it as an omen of the enemy's defeat. The draft's §4 p1 describes the overnight withdrawal
"in the darkness" and "by the small hours" with no mention of the eclipse — a missed beat
that is *both* a real tactical screen and a gift for a section whose whole spine is
atmosphere-and-meaning over carnage. This is the single clearest comprehensiveness gap.
**This is a fact-pack blind spot** — it is not in `lookout-mountain-factpack.md` at all,
so the author could not have used it. Add it to the fact pack (§4 geography/§5 sequence
step 7) and offer it to the author for §4 p1.

### MUST-ADD 2 — Grant ordered only a demonstration "if practicable"; Hooker turned it into a real assault
Per Wikipedia and NPS (*Grant at Lookout Mountain*), Grant did **not** want a serious
fight for the mountain — he acceded to Thomas's pressure and ordered Hooker to take the
point only "if his demonstration should develop its practicability." Hooker "ignored this
subtlety" and at 3 a.m. ordered Geary to cross Lookout Creek and assault the mountain
outright. This is load-bearing comprehensiveness: it explains (a) WHY the fight was bigger
than planned, (b) reinforces — from the orders themselves, not just hindsight — Grant's
later "all poetry" dismissal (he never rated the mountain a priority), and (c) adds a
second redemption-arc beat for Hooker (he exceeded a cautious order and pulled it off).
The draft gestures at Grant's low regard only retrospectively via the memoir quote; the
*contemporaneous* order is stronger and absent. **Fact-pack blind spot** — the fact pack
says Grant "designed the three-day breakout" and "did not direct the assault in person,"
but never states the assault exceeded his actual orders. Add to fact pack §2 (Grant) +
§5 sequence step 2; offer to author for §1 h-block ("Hooker Gets the Mountain").

## SHOULD-CONSIDER (enriching, not blocking)

- **S1 — Time anchors for the Cravens fight.** Sources put the Cravens engagement
  intensifying ~11:30 a.m.–noon and effectively decided by ~2–3 p.m. The draft uses "late
  morning," "around one in the afternoon," "about two o'clock" — adequate, but a single
  clean "by mid-afternoon it was effectively over" beat would sharpen the timeline.
- **S2 — "Magnificent skirmish."** A war reporter with Grant's army called it "more like a
  magnificent skirmish than a battle" (per ABT/secondary). A second period deflation
  alongside Grant's "all poetry" would strengthen §3 — but Grant's line already carries
  the load, so this is optional color, NOT required. (Verify wording before use.)
- **S3 — John Kiggins / Medal of Honor friendly-fire save.** Sgt. John A. Kiggins (a
  color-bearer who waved his flag to stop Union friendly fire in the fog) earned the Medal
  of Honor at Lookout Mountain. A vivid human-scale vignette that fits the fog-confusion
  theme, but a war section need not name every MoH recipient. SHOULD-CONSIDER for a
  caption or aside, not a gap.
- **S4 — Walthall's brigade nearly overrun / picket post captured "without firing a
  shot."** ABT notes Geary's men captured an entire Confederate picket post bloodlessly in
  the fog. This concretizes the "dissolved into surrender" theme the draft already states
  abstractly in the Cravens Meanwhile — a strong specific the author could fold in.
- **S5 — Bragg's name for the moved division.** Draft says Bragg "pulled a division off the
  mountain" (correct). Sources name it as Walker's division (the fact pack says Walker;
  ABT's map page says Walker, History.com/some say it differently). Adding the name is
  optional precision; verify against the fact-checker's reconciliation before naming.
- **S6 — Hooker's later delay mechanism at Missionary Ridge.** The draft correctly flags
  Hooker arrived late on Nov 25 (the "drawn battle" minority view). The *reason* — a burned
  bridge over Chattanooga Creek that cost him hours — is the specific behind the delay.
  Optional; the hand-off rule says don't tell Missionary Ridge's story, so leave it terse.

## FACT-PACK BLIND SPOTS (for the reconciler to feed back upstream)

1. **The total lunar eclipse (Nov 24→25 night)** — not in the fact pack at all. It is
   well-attested (Wikipedia, ABT, veteran accounts) and is a signature atmospheric+tactical
   fact. The fact pack's §4 (fog) and §5 step 7 (withdrawal) are exactly where it belongs.
   This is the highest-value blind spot.
2. **Grant's "if practicable" order / Hooker exceeding it** — the fact pack frames Grant as
   the breakout's architect who simply "did not direct the assault in person," omitting that
   the assault went beyond Grant's cautious orders (Thomas pushed, Grant conceded a
   demonstration, Hooker assaulted outright). NPS *Grant at Lookout Mountain* + Wikipedia.
3. **"Magnificent skirmish" period quote** — the fact pack has Grant's "all poetry" and the
   name's myth-making but not this second contemporaneous deflation. Minor.
4. **Medal of Honor / Kiggins friendly-fire vignette** — not in the fact pack; a human-cost
   detail (§7) the pack could optionally carry.
5. **The bloodless picket-post capture** (ABT) — a concrete instance of the "dissolved into
   surrender" theme the fact pack asserts only in aggregate (~1,064 captured). Worth adding
   to §7 as a usable specific.

None of these undermine a claim already in the draft; they are additive. The draft contains
**no comprehensiveness over-reach or fabricated coverage** — every beat it asserts maps to a
real, sourced fact, and the load-bearing corrections (palisade un-stormed, summit abandoned,
mostly-prisoners, name-coined-in-real-time) are all correct.

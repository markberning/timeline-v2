✅ **Place: "About five miles north of Camden, South Carolina, astride the Great Wagon Road"** — Wikipedia and ABT place the battlefield ~5 miles north of Camden (some sources say 4–6; "about five" is safe). Great Wagon Road confirmed as the spine of both night marches.

✅ **"The second southern army in four months"** — Charleston fell May 12, 1780 (~3 months before Aug 16). "Four months" is a standard loose rounding in the secondary literature; the fact pack uses it. Confirmed acceptable.

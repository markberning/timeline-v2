- **Note B — French force at the Forks.** Draft: "around 500 men." Wikipedia/*Fort Duquesne* and the fact pack give ~500; contemporary reports and some secondaries give 600 (Contrecœur's demand, 17 Apr) to 700 (Trent's report). The draft's hedged "around 500" sits at the low end of a genuinely contested figure and is defensible; not an error. ⚠️ minor — consider "500 or more" for safety, but optional.

Sources have Contrecœur's force arriving/demanding on **17 April** with the surrender on **18 April**. The surrender date (the load-bearing fact) is correct. The "arrived" on the 18th is a one-day compression; harmless but could read "arrived on the 17th, and on the 18th Ward surrendered." ⚠️ trivial.
- **Note D — Logstown distance.** Draft "about 14 miles below the Forks" matches the Logstown Wikipedia article. (The *Tanacharison* Wikipedia article inconsistently says ~20 miles — an internal Wikipedia discrepancy; the dedicated Logstown source supports 14, so the draft is on the better source.) ✅

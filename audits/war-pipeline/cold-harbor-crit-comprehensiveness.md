## SHOULD-ADD (enrichment, non-blocking)

1. **Lee's own assessment / the Confederate experience of June 3.** The draft is told almost
   entirely from the Union side of the trench (which is defensible — it is a Union slaughter).
   But a single sentence from inside Lee's works — that to many defenders it barely registered
   as a major attack (the same Rhea point the draft already uses for casualties) could be
   surfaced as *Confederate* perspective, not just as a casualty-accounting footnote. This
   would close a mild perspective gap: the reader currently never stands behind the rampart.
   SHOULD-ADD, optional.

2. **The enslaved laborers digging the Confederate works — given more than a clause.** The
   draft mentions, in the closing section, that "enslaved laborers were being forced to dig
   the Confederate works." This is the single most concrete on-the-Cold-Harbor-ground link
   to the cause, and it is currently one subordinate clause. It could carry a touch more
   weight where the "works" section describes the digging on June 2 — the trench the Union
   charged was, in part, dug by enslaved hands. SHOULD-ADD (also flagged in the framing gate
   as a strengthener, not a fix).

3. **Sheridan and the cavalry race gets one line.** The May 31 seizure of the crossroads is
   covered, but the dramatic "cavalry tearing across the countryside for a tavern that
   couldn't cook a meal" is set up in §1 and then resolved very briefly. Minor; the battle's
   weight is rightly on June 3. No change needed.

4. **Meade.** Listed in the fact pack as Army of the Potomac commander but absent from the
   draft. This is a defensible authorial choice — the draft establishes Grant as the
   decision-maker riding with the army, which is the load-bearing relationship — but a reader
   who knows the command structure may notice Meade's absence. SHOULD-ADD a half-clause if
   space allows; not required.

## Fact-pack blind spots (independent of the draft)

1. **Confederate corps/division command above the Breckinridge/Hoke level is thin.** The fact
   pack lists Anderson (First Corps), A.P. Hill (Third), Early (Second), Hampton (cavalry),
   but the draft (correctly, per the `[VERIFY]` discipline) names only Breckinridge at the
   point of contact. This is the right call — but note the fact pack itself never resolved the
   Hoke/Kershaw-opposite-which-Union-corps question, so the draft's terrain-not-unit
   description of Smith's ravine is the *only* safe path and the author took it. No defect;
   flagging that the blind spot is real and was correctly routed around.

2. **The "men refused to renew the assault" story** was dropped by both pack and draft. The
   omission is correct (sourcing varies), but a reader familiar with the battle may expect it.
   Leaving it out is the conservative, defensible choice. No action.

3. **Whole-battle Confederate casualty figure is genuinely unsettled** (ABT ~4,595 vs Wiki
   ~5,287). The draft prints a range, which is the only honest move. Not a blind spot in the
   draft — flagging that the underlying uncertainty is real and correctly surfaced as a range.

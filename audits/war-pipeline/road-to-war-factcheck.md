**F-71 — John Brown's raid: "October 16, 1859"**

**F-72 — "twenty-one men — sixteen white, five Black"**
Draft: "Brown led a band of twenty-one men — sixteen white, five Black"
❌ **WRONG** — This is an error. The composition of Brown's raiders is contested between sources, but the Wikipedia article on John Brown's raid gives the primary figure as **22 raiders** (with varying racial counts depending on how local enslaved people who assisted are counted). The Wikipedia article on John Brown's raiders lists **22 participants: 17 white, 5 Black** as one count. The NPS article gives "21 men besides Brown himself" = 22 total, with a racial breakdown of 16 white and 5 Black (plus some local enslaved assistance). The most authoritative breakdown per NPS and multiple sources is: **21 raiders besides Brown** (22 counting Brown), with 16 white and 5 Black among the core raiding party.

The draft says "21 men — sixteen white, five Black." This totals 21 including Brown (i.e., Brown + 20 others), which is inconsistent with the NPS's "21 men besides Brown." The fact pack itself flags this discrepancy (Flag #8) and recommends 21 as the NPS figure. However, the racial breakdown "sixteen white, five Black" sums to only 21 total — which means Brown himself is not included in the count if we accept this. Across sources the most defensible reading is: **approximately 21–22 total raiders, with 16 white and 5 Black in the core party that marched on Harpers Ferry.** The draft's figure of "21 men" is defensible if Brown is counted separately as the leader (NPS phrasing: "21 men besides himself"). What IS NOT well-supported is "sixteen white, five Black" summing to 21, since that count appears to exclude Brown. The core people who raided: 22 total including Brown (16 white + 5 Black + Brown himself = 22).

**The clearest error: The draft says "a band of twenty-one men — sixteen white, five Black" which implies the 21 includes all participants. But 16+5=21 only if Brown is excluded. If Brown is the 22nd, the count and composition are internally consistent. The draft's phrasing is ambiguous but the numbers are defensible with one reading. The safest correction: "twenty-one men besides himself — sixteen white, five Black" or "a band of twenty-two — sixteen white, five Black, plus himself."**
→ **SHOULD-FIX** — Clarify whether the 21 includes or excludes Brown to avoid implicit error.

**F-81 — Brown's gallows note exact text**
Draft quotes: *"I John Brown am now quite certain that the crimes of this guilty land will never be purged away but with blood."*
⚠️ PUNCTUATION CONTESTED — The [VERIFY] flag is resolved, but not in the draft's favor. The actual original note (as reproduced at marxists.org and multiple primary source transcriptions) reads: *"Charlestown, Virginia Dec 2, 1859 / I, John Brown, am now quite certain that the crimes of this guilty land: will never be purged away; but with Blood."* Another version at marxists.org transcribes it slightly differently. The Wikipedia article on the raid gives: *"I John Brown am now quite certain that the crimes of this guilty, land: will never be purged away; but with Blood."* The NPS version omits internal punctuation: *"I John Brown am now quite certain that the crimes of this guilty land will never be purged away but with blood."*

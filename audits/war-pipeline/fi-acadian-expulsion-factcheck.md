| Violet sank a day earlier with more than 280 Acadians, all lost | ✅ CONFIRMED | 12 Dec 1758, "all the Acadians on board" lost. (Violet article gives "some 360 aboard"; "over 280" is within the sourced range and matches the fact pack's flagged 280–400 range — not wrong) |
| Ruby, carrying around 310, also sank | ✅ CONFIRMED | ~310 aboard; 213 died (ran aground on Pico Island, Azores) |
| Noël Doiron, wife Marie, five grown children + spouses, 30+ grandchildren — about 120 people of one family; none survived | ✅ CONFIRMED | verbatim match, Duke William article ("120 family members in total") |

⚠️ **"Counting all three wrecks, close to 1,000 Acadians drowned in a single week"** (line 105): Defensible as an aggregate (~360 + ~280–360 + 213 deaths, or ~1,030 aboard the three), and the fact pack explicitly sanctions "Combined, nearly 1,000 Acadians died." But two precision wrinkles: (a) the Ruby ran aground in the **Azores** (Pico Island), not the open North Atlantic off France, and 213 of ~310 died rather than all aboard — so "all three… drowned" is slightly loose for the Ruby; (b) the three sinkings spanned 11–13 December (≈3 days), so "in a single week" is fine. The figure is within the sourced envelope and not a fabrication. **shouldFix** wording (e.g., "close to 900 died" or keep "nearly 1,000" but note the Ruby ran aground); not ship-blocking.

| Overall toll: most cited summary ~5,000 dead; "thousands, and we cannot give the exact number" | ✅ CONFIRMED | ~5,000 summary tally; draft correctly flags uncertainty per the gap list |

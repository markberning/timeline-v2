## Fact-Pack Blind Spots

These items appear in sources but are **not** in the fact pack and are not in the draft — they are not necessarily MUST-ADD, but a revising author should be aware of them.

1. **Sigel's role on Day 1 vs. Day 2 asymmetry.** Wikipedia and ABT both note Sigel's performance was uneven: his rear guard was routed on March 6, and on Day 1 he was not the battle's hero. His Day-2 artillery mastery is partly a redemption arc. The fact pack focuses on Day-2 Sigel but doesn't foreground the Day-1 contrast. Not ship-blocking but the arc is richer if noted.

2. **Curtis's promotion to Maj. Gen. after the battle** (fact pack commander table has it; draft never mentions it). Minor reward-story material; omitting is fine.

3. **Carr wounded three times — specific nature/timeline of wounds** (fact pack §6, Wikipedia — "wounded three times but remained in command"). Pack records it; draft never uses it. This feeds MUST-ADD #2 above.

4. **The 1st Choctaw-Chickasaw and 1st Creek regiments: organized but their back pay arrived too late; they didn't reach the field.** The draft says "other Native units had been organized but didn't reach the field in time" — correct but the reason (pay dispute) is colorful and brief. Low priority.

5. **ABT casualty figure of 2,500 Confederate** vs. the 2,000 in the fact pack and draft. The pack acknowledges the ABT's 2,500 figure but recommends 2,000 (NPS/Wikipedia consensus). Draft uses 2,000. No error; just worth knowing the range if the draft is revised.

---

## Overall Assessment

The draft is structurally sound and covers the core spine completely. The MUST-ADD items are all **one-sentence or subordinate-clause additions** — none require structural revision. The two most important are:

- **#3 (Day-1 artillery imbalance)** — the structural logic of the Day-2 reversal depends on the reader knowing Day 1 was going the other way in firepower.
- **#5 (Pike's treaty stipulation)** — the one gap in the Native American section that breaks the why-they-resisted logic.

## [FIX] — must correct before ship

**FIX-1 — the KEY STATEMENT (Breton cryptogram quote) is silently truncated; add an ellipsis.**
The draft's blockquote reads:
> "Here for the first time nature is treated as a cryptogram. The artist has a code by reason
> of his own sensitive anterior impressions, and can decode nature to reveal the very rhythm
> of life."

The sourced full passage (Wikiquote, citing Matthew Spender, *Arshile Gorky: Goats on the Roof*,
Ridinghouse 2009, p.258) ends:
> "…and can decode nature to reveal the very rhythm of life, **in the discovery of the very
> rhythm of life.**"

The draft drops the trailing "…in the discovery of the very rhythm of life" clause without an
ellipsis. The truncated version ends on a grammatically complete sentence and reads cleanly, and
the dropped tail is a redundant translation-artifact echo — so the *meaning* is faithful. But a
gated verbatim quote may not silently delete words. **Two acceptable corrections (pick one):**
- (a) Mark the cut: end the quote `…to reveal the very rhythm of life …"` (trailing ellipsis), or
- (b) Quote it whole: `…to reveal the very rhythm of life, in the discovery of the very rhythm of life."`

Recommend (a) — cleaner read, honestly marked. Either is fine; the current unmarked truncation is
the only thing standing between this quote and a clean pass. (Attribution line — "March 1945,
introduction to Gorky's first solo show, Julien Levy Gallery" — is correct as written.)

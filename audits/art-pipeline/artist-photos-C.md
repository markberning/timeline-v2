# Abstract Expressionist Artist Portrait Photos — Batch C

Born-verified portrait photos (photo of the ARTIST as a person, NOT their artwork) for
small avatar/hero use. Each URL was test-fetched to `/tmp` (HTTP 200 + valid image bytes
confirmed via `file`) and visually confirmed to show the person. Nothing downloaded into
the repo.

Rights legend: **PD** = public domain / no known copyright restrictions (safe to host
ourselves). **FAIR-USE** = in-copyright; only a small, credited thumbnail from English
Wikipedia is acceptable.

### Caveats for the integrator
- **Krasner (3338×4917)** and **Newman/Gorky (~2900–3700 wide)** are very large originals —
  downscale before shipping as avatars.
- **Frankenthaler (301px short side)** and **Kline (280px)** are near/just under the 300px
  short-side preference; **Still (233px short side)** is clearly under it. All are the
  largest available versions; fine at small avatar/hero scale, not for large crops.
- **dekooning** is square (1749×1749) — best avatar candidate, no awkward crop.
- For **Krasner**, a pre-cropped face version exists
  (`File:Lee_Krasner,_artist,_cropped.jpg`) if a tighter crop is wanted.
- **Rights split:** host the 7 PD images ourselves; for **kline** and **still** keep them
  as small credited fair-use thumbnails and do not enlarge.

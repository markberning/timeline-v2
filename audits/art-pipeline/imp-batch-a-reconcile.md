## 4. THE CRADLE (Morisot) — the biggest visual reconciliation

**FACT (gate 1) — three visual inversions in the painting itself, ALL VERIFIED BY
THE COORDINATOR**:
- F1 (MUST) **Edma is on the LEFT** of the canvas; the **cradle is on the RIGHT**
  (the gauzy netting cascades from a peak on the right and the baby is visible
  through it on the right). The draft's "right two-thirds Edma / left third cradle"
  is **inverted** — fix every spatial reference (looking chapter + 6 Look-Closer
  pointers). The two-thirds/one-third proportion stays; only the L/R swaps.
- F2 (MUST) **Her two arms**: ONE arm bent up with the **hand resting against
  her cheek/chin**; the OTHER arm **extended forward, hand resting on the
  cradle's lower rail**. The draft used "right" twice (right elbow on the rail AND
  right hand at chin) — anatomically impossible. Write it cleanly: "her near arm
  bent up so the hand rests against her cheek; her other arm reaches forward to
  the cradle's lower rail." (Use viewer-frame description, avoid the L/R-anatomy
  trap.)
- F3 (MUST) **Dress colour is dark BLUE**, not "dark grey-black" (coordinator
  verified — a clearly saturated navy/indigo jacket, with a white lace ruffle at
  the collar and a black ribbon at her throat).

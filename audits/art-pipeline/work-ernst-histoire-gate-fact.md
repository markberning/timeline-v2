### KEY-STATEMENT QUOTE (Beyond Painting) — VERIFIED

Both quoted blocks are genuinely Ernst's, from *Beyond Painting* (*Au-delà de la peinture*;
English ed. Robert Motherwell, Wittenborn/Schultz, 1948), verified against WSWS and
corroborating research summaries:

- **Block 1** ("…finding myself one rainy evening in a seaside inn, I was struck by the
  obsession that showed to my excited gaze the floor-boards upon which a thousand
  scrubbings had deepened the grooves… rub with black lead") — **matches the standard
  English text verbatim.**
- **Block 2** ("The drawings thus obtained steadily lost … the character of the material
  being studied — wood — and assumed the aspect of unbelievably clear images") —
  **confirmed verbatim** ("steadily lost the character…of the wood," "assumed the aspect
  of unbelievably clear images").
- The draft's translation-variant disclaimer (`proseMutedStyle` paragraph) is accurate and
  appropriately hedged. The childhood "false mahogany" detail in `ErnFloorboards` is part
  of Ernst's own account (it opens Block 1's source passage) — correct.

## FINDINGS

### [NICE-1] Ellipsis inside Quote Block 1 is editorial, not in the source
Draft Block 1 reads: *"…finding myself one rainy evening…"* with a leading ellipsis, which
is fine (it signals the omitted childhood-memory clause). But the source passage runs
**continuously** — "Beginning with a memory of childhood **in the course of which** a panel
of false mahogany… and finding myself one rainy evening…". The draft's leading "…" correctly
flags the cut, so this is acceptable as-is. **Optional:** no change needed; the ellipsis is
honest. Flagging only so the wirer knows the quote is a faithful *excerpt*, not a contiguous
sentence.

### [NICE-2] Plate width: 42.5 vs 42.7 cm
Const comment (line 55) and several prose spots use **~26 × 42.5 cm**; the NGV record for
*this exact plate* gives **26.0 × 42.7 cm**. The 42.5 figure traces to MoMA's slightly
different copy of the plate (plate sizes vary copy-to-copy). The reader-facing imperial
value **10¾ in wide** rounds identically from both, and the heroAspect (1.63 vs true 1.642)
is within tolerance. **No change required.** If you want exactness on the wired plate,
the NGV plate is 26.0 × 42.7 cm.

### [NICE-3] "Pornic… Brittany" avoided — good
Some sources loosely place Pornic "in Brittany"; it is in fact in Loire-Atlantique
(Pays de la Loire), near Nantes, on the Breton *border*. The draft says only "a fishing
town on the Atlantic coast of France" — **correct and safely non-committal.** No change.

### [NICE-4] "rainy evening" vs "rainy afternoon"
A couple of secondary sources say "rainy afternoon"; Ernst's own quoted text says
"one rainy evening," and the draft follows Ernst ("wet evening," "rainy night" in a title).
The draft is right to follow Ernst's wording. The `ErnFloorboards` header "The rainy night
the floor started drawing" is a house-voice flourish, not a factual claim about night vs
evening — acceptable.

### [NICE-5] Edition paper breakdown — directionally correct, granular detail unverified
The provenance note's "Japan and vellum papers" + "300 (+6 hors-commerce)" + "all but the
hors-commerce signed" all check out at the summary level (NGV + Swann). The finer split
(20 Imperial Japan / 30 Arches / 250 vellum) from the factpack is **not** asserted in the
reader prose — good, since catalogue breakdowns vary slightly. No change.

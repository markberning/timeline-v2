## BIOGRAPHY / SURROUNDING-CAST CLAIMS (all verified)

- **Jasper Johns** — b. Augusta, Georgia, 1930; raised in South Carolina; living. ✅ (Draft const, FlgDream.)
- **Robert Rauschenberg** (1925–2008) — Johns's partner; lived near him (Johns on Pearl Street, Rauschenberg on Fulton). ✅ Draft says "sharing a building on Pearl Street." Sources place Johns *on* Pearl Street and Rauschenberg *nearby on Fulton*, in **adjacent lofts**, not necessarily the literal same building. See NICE-2.
- **Leo Castelli** (1907–1999) — gave Johns his first solo show, **January 1958**. ✅
- **Alfred H. Barr, Jr.** (1902–1981) — MoMA founding director, wanted *Flag*, trustees feared "unpatriotic." ✅
- **Philip Johnson** (1906–2005) — architect; bought *Flag* privately 1958, gave to MoMA 1973 in Barr's honor. ✅
- **Flag variants** — *White Flag* (1955, Met), *Three Flags* (1958, Whitney). ✅ The MoMA *Flag* (1954–55) is the first. ✅

## FINDINGS

### [NICE-1] "No headlines / inconsequential clippings" — keep the soft hedge it already has
The draft says Johns "reportedly avoided headlines and political stories, using
inconsequential clippings and ads." This is the standard account but is not a hard MoMA
catalogue fact — it is a frequently repeated characterization of the visible newsprint.
The draft **already hedges it** ("reportedly," "He reportedly used inconsequential
clippings"), which is exactly right. No change required; flagged only so the hedge is
not removed in a later editing pass. **Action: none (hedge is correct as-is).**

### [NICE-2] "Sharing a building on Pearl Street" — precise sources say *adjacent/nearby* lofts
FlgDream line 123 says Johns was "sharing a building on Pearl Street with… Robert
Rauschenberg." The verified record places **Johns in a loft on Pearl Street and
Rauschenberg nearby on Fulton Street** — adjacent buildings in lower Manhattan, the two
in and out of each other's studios, rather than literally the same building. The
narrative point (they were close neighbors and partners) is unaffected.
**Optional tighten:** "sharing a building on Pearl Street with" → "living a few doors
from… Robert Rauschenberg, in the same lower-Manhattan loft circle," or simply
"living near… Robert Rauschenberg." Low stakes; not a blocker.

### [NICE-3] Dream-quote source pin — optional one-word precision in the ledger only
The draft attributes the looser variant to "a 1960s television interview." Verified
source (greg.org) pins it specifically to the **1966 NET / *USA Artists* interview with
Alan Solomon**. The draft deliberately keeps it loose ("a 1960s television interview"),
which is consistent with the fact pack's instruction *not* to over-specify the
interview/date in the reader. This is correct authorial restraint — **leave the reader
text as-is.** Logged here only so the verified specific (1966 Solomon/NET interview) is
on record for the ledger. **Action: none in draft.**

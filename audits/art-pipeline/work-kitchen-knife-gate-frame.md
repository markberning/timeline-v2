## 2. FRAMING / FAIRNESS

### 2a. The photomontage-"invention" overclaim — HANDLED CORRECTLY

This is the single biggest framing risk for this work, and the draft passes cleanly. It never states invention as settled fact. KnfMaking §"A memoir feud, not a settled fact" does all four required moves:

1. Names the claim as something the reader "will read, often" — i.e., flags it as received wisdom, not the draft's own assertion.
2. Scopes it to a contested **Höch/Hausmann memoir claim** ("The two of them told an origin story…").
3. Cites the **pre-Dada precedent**: "a commercial trick going back to the 1850s, decades before Dada." (Web-verified: cartes-de-visite cut-and-paste from the mid-1850s, Disdéri 1854; combination prints predate Dada by ~three-quarters of a century. The fact pack and draft are correct.)
4. Names the **rival claimants** (Grosz, Heartfield, Baader) and the "no early works survive" caveat.

The landing line — "what they did was take a commercial novelty and make it a serious medium for art and for politics … not a fact anyone can settle" — is the exact scoped framing the gate wants. The const-level comment (lines 20–23) and `medium: 'Photomontage (cut-and-pasted printed paper)'` keep the same discipline.

- **[FIX — minor] One residual flat phrasing in a non-prose surface.** The hook (line 53) and `KnfMaking` blurb (the const `sections` blurb, line 66: "photomontage turned from a commercial trick into a political weapon") are fine. But the `KnfMaking` reader-component opener calls the picture "one of the founding monuments of the form" (line 160) and KnfAfterlife calls it "one of the founding works of political collage" (line 256). These are about the *work's* status as a monument of the medium, NOT about Höch inventing the medium, so they are accurate and acceptable. **No change strictly required**, but if the author wants belt-and-suspenders, "founding monuments of the form" could read "one of the founding monuments of *political photomontage*" to keep the scoping airtight. Optional.

### 2b. Gender framing — LEGITIMATE CONTENT, not tokenizing

Höch's feminism and the men's condescension are handled as substantive, sourced content, not as an identity-token frame:

- The "only woman in Berlin Dada" claim is correctly scoped to **Berlin** Dada ("other Dada cities, Zurich, Paris, New York, had women of their own. In Berlin, there was Höch") — fact pack §8.7 satisfied.
- The condescension is anchored to sourced facts (Grosz/Heartfield opposed her; Hausmann's "never a member of the club"; the threat-to-withdraw), not asserted as a vibe.
- The verbatim "None of these men…" quote carries its translation/excerpt flags (KnfReading) — matches fact pack §4.
- Crucially, the draft does **not** tip into claiming Höch as the sole inventor. The gender argument and the invention argument are kept separate; her feminism is presented as what she *aimed the medium at*, not as a priority claim over the medium. This is the right line.

No blocker. One small note:

- **[FIX — minor] The "kitchen knife = woman's domestic tool" reading is correctly flagged as interpretation** in KnfMaking ("hold it as a reading and not a quote") — good, matches fact pack §4/§8.5. But the **hook** (line 53) and the **KnfFair** close (line 137, "a woman, the one the men nearly kept out, is the one holding the knife") present the knife-as-woman's-tool framing without the hedge. In running prose this is defensible as house-voice framing rather than a factual claim, and KnfMaking later supplies the hedge. Acceptable as-is. If the author wants the hook itself to be unimpeachable, no change is needed — the interpretation is so near-universal it functions as framing, and the gendered title is Höch's own. Leave it; flagging only for awareness.

### 2c. Genius-myth / proportion — CLEAN, in fact INVERTED well

There is no lone-genius inflation. The opposite: the draft repeatedly situates Höch in a circle, credits the collective Berlin Dada contribution, and frames the photomontage breakthrough as collaborative-and-contested rather than a solo stroke. The afterlife section's "the picture that outgrew the men who nearly shut her out" is a fairness/proportion *correction* of the historical record (she was filed as "a footnote … the girlfriend who did collages"), and it is sourced to the real reception history, not invented uplift. Proportion is good — the work gets its due as "the central masterpiece of Berlin Dada photomontage" without claiming it invented the movement or the medium.

### 2d. Anachronism — CLEAN

- "Feminist art" is explicitly flagged as a retrospective frame: "a landmark of feminist art … decades before that question had a name" (KnfAfterlife) — the anachronism is named, not smuggled. Good.
- Einstein "whose theory of relativity had just made him world-famous" is period-accurate (general relativity's 1919 eclipse confirmation made Einstein a global celebrity that very year — the work's exact moment). Correct.
- "Agitprop" is era-appropriate for the early Soviet/Weimar context. Fine.

### 2e. Figure-ID fairness — EXEMPLARY

This is the second-highest risk surface (a dense montage where many IDs are scholarly inference), and the draft handles it better than most. Every speculative head-on-body identification is explicitly marked as a scholarly reading, not a label:

- Hindenburg-on-belly-dancer: "Scholars (Maud Lavin, Smarthistory) read his head as set on a belly-dancer's body … Treat the exact body-pairing as a scholarly reading" (annotation + KnfLooking).
- Lenin/Baader/Radek/Dadaist pairings: "some individual head-on-body matches are scholarly identifications, not labels on the work."
- Niddy Impekoven / Käthe Kollwitz / Pavlova / Pola Negri / Asta Nielsen: "These name-to-figure matches are partly inferential … hold the names loosely."
- The certain core (two camps, Kaiser + wrestler-moustache, suffrage map, self-portrait, "die grosse Welt dada") is consistently separated from the inferential layer.

The const FACT-HANDLING comment (lines 28–32) codifies exactly this certain-vs-scholarly split. This is the correct epistemic discipline; no fix.

## FINDINGS

### [FIX-1] "Black, of course" — Purrmann attribution is thinly sourced; the line itself is fine
Draft (HatMaking) and figures-pack frame the anecdote as: *"As **Hans Purrmann**, a German
painter in Matisse's circle, later recalled it…"* and *"a reported studio anecdote passed
down by a colleague."*
- The anecdote and the "Black, of course" line are **well attested** (SFMOMA, henrimatisse.org,
  arthistoryproject, multiple others) and the draft's "hold it loosely / probably true /
  reported studio story" framing is exactly correct — **no problem with the quote or its
  hedge.**
- BUT the specific **attribution to Hans Purrmann by name** could not be independently
  confirmed: henrimatisse.org (the pack's cited source) says only "the artist allegedly
  replied"; Wikipedia does not carry the anecdote at all and never mentions Purrmann; the
  general web record reports the line *unattributed*. Purrmann *was* in Matisse's circle and
  is a plausible transmitter, but pinning the anecdote to him reads as more precise than the
  sourcing supports.
- **Recommendation (FIX, not blocker):** either (a) soften to "as the story comes down from
  Matisse's circle…" / "studio colleagues reportedly asked…" without naming Purrmann, or
  (b) keep Purrmann but verify the name against a primary citation (e.g. Purrmann's own
  memoir / Barr's *Matisse: His Art and His Public*) before lock. The draft already hedges
  the *truth* of the line correctly; this is only about the **named source**.

### [FIX-2] Haas purchase — "from the Stein holdings" is slightly looser than the record
Draft provenance note (#5) and Afterlife prose say Haas "bought it … from the Stein
holdings." SFMOMA's 2026 material is more specific: she **bought it from Sarah Stein in
1948.** Not wrong, just vaguer than the source allows. **Recommendation:** consider "bought
it from Sarah Stein in 1948" for precision. (Minor; current wording is not inaccurate.)

### [FIX-3] Provenance years carried the fact pack's own ⚠ re-confirm flag — now CLEARED
The fact pack flagged the 1915 / 1935 / 1948 years for gate-1 re-confirmation. This gate
**confirms all three** against SFMOMA's 2026 exhibition material (via Local News Matters
and SFMOMA *From Scandal to Icon*): "In 1915 … Michael and Sarah Stein … brought it from
France to the Bay Area in 1935 … bought it from Sarah Stein in 1948 … came to SFMOMA in
1991." No change needed; the open [VERIFY] in the draft's ledger can be closed.

### [NICE] Amélie middle-name spelling
Sources vary between "Noélie" and "Noellie." The draft uses "Noélie" consistently and it
appears only once (HatMaking). Harmless; no action required.

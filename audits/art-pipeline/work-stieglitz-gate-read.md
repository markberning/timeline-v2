### Reader-commands
Covered above in LOOKING: three [FIX] ("Look at how the camera is doing," "Now go to the top," "the first thing to take in"), one [NICE].

### Meta-narration
- **[FIX] StiLooking ¶5 (line 156): "Hold that contradiction, because it is the key to what the picture actually means, and we come to it last."** This both commands the reader ("Hold that contradiction") AND narrates the article's own structure ("we come to it last") — meta-narration is banned (tell the story, don't narrate what the chapter is about / how it links). Suggested rewrite: *"It is a portrait built out of the language of love, of a man who can't get into gear — and that contradiction is the whole picture."* (drop the "we come to it last" structural pointer entirely; the afterlife section will deliver the payoff without being pre-announced).

- **[NICE] StiBreak ¶3 (line 175): "These 1915 *291* mechanomorphs turn out to be founding documents of New York Dada"** — "turn out to be" is mild but fine; not meta-narration of the article. No change needed; noting only that the "after" half of the break is in-voice and clean.

### Honesty-labels
The factpack forces several source-caution flags (Vest Pocket Kodak as identification not inscription; the quote variants; print-vs-drawing). These are legitimate accuracy content, not the banned "honestly / to be honest / let me level with you" self-labeling — with two borderline cases:

- **[FIX] StiAfterlife ¶2 (line 197): "so it is honest to keep them apart"** and **¶6 (line 208): "The honest answer is that it is both at once"** — both use "honest"/"the honest answer," which is the honesty-label tic (presenting the next clause as the candid/truthful one). The content is fine; the framing word is the problem. Suggested rewrites: line 197 → *"so the two clauses are best kept apart."*; line 208 → *"The answer is that it is both at once, and the picture is built to be both."* (Also note "The honest line:" appears in the factpack §8 but NOT in the draft prose — good; the draft already avoided it once.)

- **[NICE] StiMachine ¶2 (line 126)** — "not a label written inside the picture, so take it as 'based on an ad for the Kodak,' not as a signed fact on the sheet" — this is accuracy-flagging, acceptable, but flirts with the honesty-label register. Keep the fact, consider shortening (see Storytelling [NICE]).

- **[NICE] StiLooking ¶3 (line 150): blackletter gloss "(the dense, spiky, old-fashioned lettering you see on diplomas and beer labels)"** — "you see on diplomas and beer labels" is a nice concretizing touch, not condescending. Keep. (StiMachine line 123 and the annotation line 68 also gloss blackletter/Gothic; the term is defined three times across the work. **[FIX]-adjacent:** the *meaning* is fine but defining "Gothic/blackletter" fully in StiMachine, again in StiLooking, and again in the annotation is mild over-glossing across sections.

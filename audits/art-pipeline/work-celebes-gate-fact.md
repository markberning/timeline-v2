## FINDINGS

### [FIX-1] Acquisition hedge is over-cautious — the sources are clear it was *purchased*
The draft hedges in three places that "the sources split on whether it was purchased or
presented, so the safe statement is that it entered the Tate from Penrose":
- PART A `acquired`: "Entered the Tate from Roland Penrose, 1975 (T01988)" + provenance
  note + the const top comment.
- PART A provenance[3] note: "The sources split on whether it was purchased or presented…"
- PART B CelAfterlife: "(The Tate's own records and the secondary sources do not fully
  agree on whether Penrose sold it or gave it…)"

**Web finding:** the evidence is NOT split. The Tate record lists the acquisition as
**"Purchased 1975."** Wikipedia, ArtUK, and — decisively — **the Elephant Trust's own
About page** all state Penrose **SOLD** Celebes to the Tate in 1975, and that the
**proceeds of that sale funded the founding of the Elephant Trust** (1975). A sale that
funds a trust cannot have been a gift. The factpack's original "purchased vs presented"
caution (§1) is resolved by the live record in favor of *purchased*.

This is a FIX, not a BLOCKER: the draft's hedge is not false, just needlessly weak, and
it slightly misstates the state of the evidence ("sources split"). Recommended corrected
text:
- `acquired`: `'Purchased by the Tate from Roland Penrose, 1975 (T01988)'`
- provenance[3] note → e.g. *"Purchased by the Tate from Roland Penrose in 1975
  (accession T01988); the proceeds funded the Elephant Trust, which Penrose endowed with
  the sale. On view."*
- CelAfterlife parenthetical → drop the "do not fully agree on whether Penrose sold it or
  gave it" hedge; state plainly that Penrose sold it to the Tate in 1975 and used the
  proceeds to found the Elephant Trust. (Optional NICE: the Elephant Trust detail is a
  genuinely good, verifiable house-voice beat.)

### [FIX-2] Penrose acquisition year — "c. 1938" can be firmed to 1938
The draft hedges the Penrose date as "c. 1938 … the precise year is uncertain." Multiple
sources state **Penrose bought it in 1938**, when Éluard sold off much of his collection
(Wikipedia; corroborating accounts). The Elephant Trust page is looser ("late 1930s"),
so a soft hedge is defensible — but the standard figure is a firm 1938. Recommended:
drop "the precise year is uncertain" and state 1938 (a "c." prefix is acceptable but the
"uncertain" qualifier overstates the doubt). Minor; leaving as-is is not an error.

### [NICE-1] Éluard acquisition year
Draft says "c. 1921 … the exact year is not firmly documented." Sources say Éluard bought
it the year it was completed/first exhibited (1921). The hedge is fine and harmless; could
firm to 1921 but not required.

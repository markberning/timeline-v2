## [BLOCKER]

### B1 — Death date conflation: "thrown" date is wrong (off by one day → implies death on the 18th)

**Exact draft text** (Part B, `CtyAfterlife`, ~line 229):
> "On **17 August 1916**, during a cavalry training exercise, he was **thrown from his horse and trampled**. He died the next day, in a military hospital, at the age of **thirty-three**."

**Problem:** Boccioni was thrown on **16 August 1916** and **died the next day, 17 August 1916**. The draft puts the *throw* on 17 August and death "the next day," which dates the death to **18 August** — wrong. (The fact pack itself states "Died 17 August 1916 … the day after he was thrown" — section 6 — so this is a draft-introduced error, not a fact-pack error.)

**Correction (either form is correct):**
- "On **16 August 1916**, during a cavalry training exercise, he was thrown from his horse and trampled. He died the next day, 17 August 1916, in a military hospital, at the age of thirty-three." OR
- "On **17 August 1916** he died in a Verona military hospital, the day after he was thrown from his horse and trampled during a cavalry training exercise, at the age of thirty-three."

**Source:** Wikipedia, *Umberto Boccioni* — "thrown from his horse on 16 August 1916 during a cavalry training exercise … died the following day, 17 August 1916, aged thirty-three, at Verona Military Hospital." Corroborated by Met essay / multiple. Fact pack §6.

2. **NOT "first published in *Le Figaro*."** ✔ PASS. `CtyManifestos` (line 110) explicitly handles it: "that famous *Le Figaro* front page was not the manifesto's first appearance. The Part A `manifestos` blurb (line 51) says "a press release on the front page of a Paris newspaper" — does NOT claim "first published," so it's clean too.

3. **"First major Futurist painting" must stay Futurism-scoped.** ✔ PASS. `CtyDivisionism` (line 209) scopes it explicitly: "this is **Boccioni's first major Futurist painting** … Not the first modern painting, not the first painting of motion in all of art, just the first big one of *this* movement." Part A comment + hook ("Boccioni's first all-out Futurist canvas") also scoped. Verified the claim is supported (MoMA / Wikipedia / Smarthistory call it his first major Futurist work).

4. **No invented Busoni purchase price.** ✔ PASS. 5. **MoMA accession; canvas ~199×301 cm → imperial; 1916 horse-fall death stated as fact not prophecy.** ✔ PASS on all three:

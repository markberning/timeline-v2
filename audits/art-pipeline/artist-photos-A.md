# Artist portrait photos — batch A

Born-verified portrait images for the artists page (avatar / hero use). Every URL
below was test-fetched to `/tmp` and returned HTTP 200 with real image bytes, and
each was visually confirmed to depict the artist (a person), not their artwork.

Fetch note: `upload.wikimedia.org` now rejects a bare/short User-Agent with HTTP
403. A descriptive UA is required, e.g.
`StuffHappenedResearchBot/1.0 (https://stuffhappened.com; contact: cc2@eusd.org)`.
All URLs verified 200 with that UA.

Rights legend: **PD** = public domain (Commons `wikipedia/commons/...`).
**fair-use** = small in-copyright thumbnail on English Wikipedia
(`wikipedia/en/...`) — flagged where used.

### Summary of caveats
- **8 of 10 are public domain.** Two have **no free photo on Commons** and use a
  small English-Wikipedia fair-use thumbnail: **leger** and **balla** (both died
  in the 1950s, so lifetime photos are still in copyright). For balla, a large
  PD self-portrait painting (`GBallaArt.jpg`) is offered as an alternative.
- **marquet** is a PD **self-portrait painting** (no PD photo found) — acceptable
  per the brief's "self-portrait headshot OK" clause.
- **russolo** (200px GIF) and **hausmann** (245px) are below the ~300px short-side
  preference but are the best free portraits available; both are public domain.
- All other files are ≥300px on the short side.

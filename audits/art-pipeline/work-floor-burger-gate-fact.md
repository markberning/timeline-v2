## Special-care items (all CONFIRMED)

1. **rights:'in-copyright' — CORRECT, not pd-us.** Work 1962, Oldenburg d. 2022.
   Firmly in U.S. copyright. Draft hero credit + `rights:'in-copyright'` + "shown
   small under fair use" all correct. NO pd-us anywhere. ✓

2. **Medium = AGO's exact line.** Draft `medium:` = "Canvas filled with foam rubber
   and cardboard boxes, painted with acrylic" — matches the AGO record verbatim
   (AGO full line ends "...painted with acrylic paint"; the trailing "paint" is
   harmless to drop in the const field). Prose correctly scopes it: acrylic on
   cotton-duck canvas, fill = foam rubber + cardboard cartons, and explicitly warns
   off "latex"/"vinyl." Identified as a SOFT SCULPTURE throughout. ✓

3. **Dimensions 132.1 × 226.1 cm → ft/in.** Arithmetic checks: 132.1 cm = 52.0 in =
   4 ft 4 in (height); 226.1 cm = 89.0 in = 7 ft 5 in (across). Draft `dimensions:`
   = "4 ft 4 in × 7 ft 5 in" and prose "about seven feet across … about four feet
   four inches tall (132 by 226 centimeters)" all correct. **Good catch by the
   author:** the draft AVOIDS the fact pack's loose "≈7 ft in diameter" framing and
   correctly treats the piece as landscape (wide, not a single diameter). ✓

4. **AGO acc. 66/29, credit "Purchase, 1967" — use 1967.** Draft uses
   `acquired: 'Purchase, 1967 (acc. 66/29)'` and the prose explains the 66/ prefix =
   1966 fiscal cycle but the gallery states 1967 (finalized 27 Jan 1967). Exactly as
   instructed. No "1966" anywhere. ✓

5. **Title variants both legit.** Draft const comment + prose treat *Giant Hamburger*
   (original) and *Floor Burger* (current AGO standing title) as both correct,
   neither "wrong." Confirmed (AGO title = *Floor Burger*; "Giant Hamburger"/"Giant
   Burger" = original exhibition title). ✓

6. **The Oldenburg quote — AUTHORITATIVE Walker wording.** Verified verbatim on the
   Walker page: **"I am for an art that is political-erotical-mystical, that does
   something OTHER THAN sit on its ass in a museum."** "**other than**," NOT "more
   than." Sourced to the 1961 statement *I am for an art…*, catalogue of
   *Environments, Situations, Spaces*, **Martha Jackson Gallery**, New York. Draft
   quotes it exactly, flags the "more than" misquote, attributes to Walker, and notes
   Oldenburg later resisted "manifesto" (Walker confirms: "literary effort … not a
   sort of cry," "said for the sound of them"). Fully correct. ✓

7. **Soft-sculpture innovation scoped (one of a set).** Draft explicitly: "one of a
   set rather than the lone first example," "Oldenburg did not invent the very idea
   single-handedly," made alongside *Floor Cake* and *Floor Cone* for the 1962 Green
   Gallery Store. Confirmed (companion set verified across Substack/AGO/MoMA framing).
   Never calls it "the first soft sculpture" full stop. ✓

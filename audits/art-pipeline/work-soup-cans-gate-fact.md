## [FIX] — small precision corrections (not ship-blocking, but should be applied)

**[FIX-1] Installment term: "roughly a year" → "about ten months."**
Draft (PART A provenance + the AfterlifeSection prose + the cover note) says the $1,000 was "paid in installments over about a year (roughly $100 a month)." The documented term is **$100 a month for ten months** (Revolver Gallery; Artsy). So "roughly $100 a month" is right, but "over about a year" overstates it.
- Provenance note (PART A line ~63): change `paid in installments over about a year (roughly $100 a month)` → `paid in ten monthly installments of $100`.
- AfterlifeSection prose (`SupAfterlife`): change `paid in installments (by the usual account, around a hundred dollars a month over roughly a year)` → `paid in installments (around a hundred dollars a month for about ten months)`.
- Draft header/cover note: same one-line fix where "$1,000, a token sum in installments" is elaborated.

(The $1,000 total, the token-sum framing, the "paid in installments" core, and "~$100/month" are all correct — only the duration is off.)

## [NICE] — optional polish

**[NICE-1] The "twenty years" quote drops the leading "Because."**
The published Swenson answer begins **"Because I used to drink it. I used to have the same lunch every day, for twenty years, I guess, the same thing over and over again."** The draft quotes it as `"I used to drink it. I used to have the same lunch every day…"` — i.e. it starts the quotation mid-answer at "I used to drink it," dropping the opening "Because." This is a legitimate way to excerpt a verbatim quote (you are starting the quote one word in), and the rest is word-perfect, so it is not an error. If you want it flush with the source, either restore "Because" or signal the truncation — but as written it is acceptable and reads cleanly. No change required.

**[NICE-2] "around fifteen million dollars" — keep the hedge, which the draft already does.**
The ~$15M MoMA figure is "widely reported," not an official disclosed price; the draft correctly hedges it ("widely reported at around fifteen million dollars"). Good as is. No change.

**[NICE-3] National Gallery loan year is correct as stated.**
Draft says Blum placed the set on long-term loan to the National Gallery of Art "from 1987 (the year of Warhol's death)." Verified: the loan began **20 February 1987, two days before Warhol died** (22 Feb 1987). The draft's wording is accurate. No change.

**[NICE-4] "Synthetic polymer paint" = the acrylic.**
Sources describe the paint as "commercial-grade acrylic"; MoMA's catalog term is "synthetic polymer paint," which is the museum's name for that acrylic. Draft uses MoMA's term consistently (medium line and prose). Consistent and correct. No change.

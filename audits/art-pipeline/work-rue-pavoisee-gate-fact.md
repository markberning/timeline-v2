The draft's "from the windows of hotel bedrooms" and "two painters chasing the same subjects out the same windows" are accurate. CLEAN.

## Monet precedent — CORRECT and notably careful

- Draft (looking + break): Monet "had painted his own famous flag-decked Paris streets back in **1878**" / "Monet's flag-decked street of 1878, the obvious ancestor." Named **generically**, no Montorgueil-vs-Saint-Denis choice — exactly the fact pack §8 instruction.
- **Verified factual nuance the draft gets right:** Monet's 1878 flag paintings (*La Rue Montorgueil* / *Rue Saint-Denis*) commemorate the **fête of 30 June 1878** (Universal Exhibition / "paix et travail"), NOT the 14 July. The draft **never** claims Monet painted a 14-July street — it only calls them "flag-decked Paris streets" / "flag streets" on a "national festival." So no date/occasion error is introduced. (histoire-image.org corroborates the lineage: the rue-pavoisée theme is *"cher à Monet."*) CLEAN.
- Source: https://www.musee-orsay.fr/en/artworks/la-rue-montorgueil-paris-fete-du-30-juin-1878-10896

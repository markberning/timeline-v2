# Artist portrait photos — Batch B (Surrealists)

Born-verified PORTRAIT PHOTOGRAPHS (the artist as a person, not their artwork) for
small avatar / hero use on an artists page. Every URL below was test-fetched to
`/tmp/artist-photos-B/` and returned **HTTP 200 with valid image bytes** (verified
with `file` — dimensions confirmed). Nothing downloaded into the repo.

Rights flag per entry: **PD** = public domain / CC0 (use freely). **CC BY-SA** =
free but attribution + share-alike required. **FAIR-USE** = in-copyright; only a
small, credited thumbnail off en.wikipedia is acceptable.

Note on the Wikimedia 429s: the commons CDN rate-limited 3 fetches on the first
pass; all 3 returned clean 200 + JPEG bytes on a paced retry. The URLs are good.

- **≥300px short side:** Ernst, Dalí, Magritte, Miró, Oppenheim. Under 300 (avatar-only):
  Carrington 294, de Chirico 290, Tanguy 252, Masson 196.

  artist has at least a fair-use thumb — but Carrington and Masson have NO Commons
  photo of the person at all (only sculptures / a poster), so they fall back to

## [FIX] — 1

**[FIX-1] Medium omits "watercolor."** Multiple catalogue records (incl. lumenlearning/SUNY and others tracing the museum record) give the medium as **"collage / photomontage with watercolor"** or "photomontage and collage with watercolor," not photomontage alone. The draft const has `medium: 'Photomontage (cut-and-pasted printed paper)'` and PART B's KnfMaking leans hard on "no painting in it at all… not one brushstroke." The "no paint at all" rhetorical point is the spine of the section and is *substantially* true (the work is overwhelmingly cut paper), but if the museum record includes watercolor, the absolute claim "there is no painting in it at all… Not one brushstroke" is technically overstated.
- Lowest-risk correction (keeps the rhetorical break, removes the false-absolute): change `medium` to
  `medium: 'Photomontage and collage (cut paper, with watercolor)'`
  and soften the PART B absolute from "there is no painting in it at all. Not one brushstroke, not one invented figure." to:
  "there is almost no painting in it at all — no invented figures, effectively no brushwork; every face, wheel, letter, and scrap of map was cut from something already printed."
- This is a FIX (not BLOCKER) because the watercolor is minor/incidental and "photomontage" is the canonical medium term every source leads with; but the word "Not one brushstroke" is a stated, checkable fact that the source record contradicts.

## [NICE] — 3

**[NICE-1] Title preposition variant.** The draft uses "…Cultural Epoch **of** Germany"; Smarthistory/Khan/Britannica use "…**in** Germany." Both are legitimate translations of the one German title (which the draft carries correctly: *…Bierbauchkulturepoche Deutschlands*). No change required — flagging only so a later editor does not "correct" one into the other. The German anchor is right.

**[NICE-2] "Around 1957" is an inference from the inventory number, not a sourced acquisition date.** Search did not surface an independent statement that the work was acquired in 1957; the year is read off the "57" in NG 57/61. The draft already hedges this correctly ("the number points to," "not documented… so none is asserted"). Optionally soften "entered the Nationalgalerie around 1957" → "entered the Nationalgalerie (its inventory number points to the late 1950s)" to avoid stating a year as fact. Acceptable as-is given the existing hedge.

**[NICE-3] Quote provenance is "undated notes," not a published manifesto.** The "None of these men…" statement is commonly transmitted as coming from undated notes among Höch's papers (per several biographical sources), not a dated published essay. The draft's framing ("Reflecting on the Dada men… Höch wrote") is fine and the translation flag is present; no change strictly needed, but the cite in the const comment ("NMWA/Lavin") is the right anchor — keep it, do not upgrade to a dated source.

## paolozzi · Eduardo Paolozzi (1924–2005)
- **Status: NOT FOUND.** No public-domain or freely-licensed portrait photo of Paolozzi
  the person exists on Wikimedia Commons or English Wikipedia. The Commons category and
  both the en-wiki and Wikidata (P18 = `NewtonBlakePaolozzi1.jpg`) entries use his
  **Newton sculpture**, not a photo of him. The en-wiki article infobox is also a
  sculpture, with no fair-use portrait file attached.
- Portrait photographs of him exist only behind rights-managed agencies — Getty Images,
  Alamy, and the National Portrait Gallery (https://www.npg.org.uk/collections/search/person/mp05645/sir-eduardo-luigi-paolozzi).
  None are directly hot-loadable / free / stable for avatar use, so none is recommended here.
- **If a portrait is required:** license one from NPG/Getty, or commission/obtain a CC-licensed
  photo. Flagging for a human decision.

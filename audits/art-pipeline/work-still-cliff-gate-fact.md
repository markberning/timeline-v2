## Special-care checklist (brief's six items) — all clear

1. **Rights `in-copyright` + fair-use heroCredit — CORRECT, not pd-us.** Still d. 1980; 1957 work is far short of any US PD threshold and the estate/CSM actively manages reproduction rights. `rights: 'in-copyright'` and the "shown small under fair use" heroCredit are right. No pd-us error.

2. **Medium / dims / location / credit / accession — all verified against Buffalo AKG.**
   - Medium "Oil on canvas" ✓ (Buffalo AKG).
   - Dimensions: Buffalo AKG gives 113 × 159 in (287 × 403.9 cm). 113 in = **9 ft 5 in** ✓; 159 in = **13 ft 3 in** ✓. Draft's `9 ft 5 in × 13 ft 3 in` and `9′5″ × 13′3″` stat are exact. Imperial-first per house rule. ✓
   - `heroAspect: 1.40` — 159/113 = 1.407, and the wired file is 374/267 = 1.401. Both round to 1.40; landscape. ✓ (Factpack's "≈1.41" comment is the same number rounded up; draft's 1.40 is fine and matches the file.)
   - Location "Buffalo AKG Art Museum, Buffalo, New York" (formerly Albright-Knox, renamed 2023) ✓.
   - Credit line "Gift of Seymour H. Knox, Jr., 1959" ✓ (Buffalo AKG verbatim).
   - Accession **K1959:26** ✓ (Buffalo AKG).

3. **DATE-CODE title, NOT a "PH" number — handled correctly.** Draft never calls *1957-D No. 1* a PH work; the const comment and the StlIndependent prose both explicitly distinguish: "PH" = PHotograph inventory number (order works were photographed), vs the date-code (year + letter + number) title. Web-confirmed: SFMOMA/Met render works as e.g. "PH-206 (1938-N-No. 1)", "1947-H No. 1 (PH-265)" — the two systems coexist and are distinct, exactly as drafted. ✓

4. **"Life-lines" NOT attributed to Still — clear.** The term does not appear anywhere in the draft. The rifts are described as "torn / flame-like / jagged color-edges," all well-attested vocabulary. The 2019 film association is avoided. ✓

5. **Quote NOT conflated; one verbatim with source — clear.** The KEY STATEMENT is quoted verbatim: *"I never wanted color to be color. I never wanted texture to be texture, or images to become shapes. I wanted them all to fuse into a living spirit."* — attributed "Clyfford Still (Clyfford Still Museum Archives)." Web-verified (The Art Story, Artnet "7 Quotes", reproduced from CSM Archives). The draft then **explicitly keeps the "life and death merging in fearful union" line separate** ("is a separate statement, made about other work… so we keep them apart"). No splice. The titles quote ("without titles of any kind… no allusions to interfere with or assist the spectator") is also verbatim (The Art Story). ✓

6. **Palette matches the wired Buffalo AKG canvas — confirmed by eye.** Downloaded + viewed the wired file: black-dominant field, jagged **yellow/beige** verticals, small **white** flecks, landscape ≈1.40. Matches Buffalo AKG's own description ("sharp contrasting colors… jagged sections of yellow and beige… against a dense black surface") and the draft's described palette. It is the right painting (K1959:26), not a red/orange Still. ✓

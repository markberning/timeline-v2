# School icon prompts (for ChatGPT / DALL·E)

Same pipeline as the thinker + work + civ emblems. **Save each as `<id>.png`** (the id is
the left column) into a folder — `~/Downloads/school-icons/` is fine. Then tell me and I'll
trim/key/tint and wire them into the school list + school hubs.

Each emblem is a COLLECTIVE symbol for the school, chosen to be distinct from any single
member's thinker emblem.

## Style (paste once, then append "… : <the symbol>" per line — matches the existing emblems)
> A minimalist flat ICON, a single solid **black silhouette on a plain solid white background**.
> Bold simple shapes, clean smooth vector look, centered with generous margin. One symbol only.
> No text, letters, or numbers. No gradient, no shading, no 3D, no drop shadow, no outline, no scene.

## Prompts — one per school

- `pre` — Pre-Socratics: a single sprouting seed with two small leaves (the first roots of philosophy, the search for what everything is made of).
- `plat` — Platonism: one perfect regular geometric solid in outline, like a dodecahedron (the eternal Forms behind the world).
- `arist` — Aristotelians: a magnifying glass held over a single leaf (the close study of the natural world).
- `stoa` — Stoics: a solid rock with small waves breaking around its base (steadfast calm against fortune).
- `schol` — Scholastics: a tall lectern holding an open book (the medieval disputation).
- `rat` — Rationalists: a human head in clean profile with a small radiant sun set inside it (truth reached by reason alone).
- `emp` — Empiricists: an open upturned hand with a few short lines entering the palm (all knowledge arriving through the senses).
- `ideal` — German Idealists: a human head in clean profile containing a small globe (the world built up by mind and Spirit).
- `util` — Utilitarians: a balance scale with several small identical figures resting on the pans (weighing the greatest happiness of the greatest number).
- `exist` — Existentialists: a single solitary standing human figure, alone (the individual, free and responsible, thrown into existence).
- `indep` — Independent: one lone five-pointed star set off by itself (the giants who founded no school and joined none).

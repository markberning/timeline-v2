## Differentiation from the thinker read and the Enquiry (the nesting gate)
- The **thinker read** is built around the *Enquiry* presentation of causation/induction (billiard
  balls, fork, custom) because that is the leaner, famous version. The Enquiry DROPS the self
  and softens the is/ought material.
- This **work read** is the *Treatise* on its own terms: the earlier, bigger, more systematic and
  more radical book. It therefore weights the two things the Enquiry abandoned and this ledger is
  asked to emphasize: **the bundle theory of the self** (Book I.IV.6, plus Hume's own Appendix
  confession that it does not hang together) and **the is/ought gap** (Book III.I.1). Causation is
  carried at *Treatise* altitude (Book I.III: constant conjunction, the inference, necessity as a
  determination of the mind) and pointed to the Enquiry/thinker read rather than re-walking the
  billiard balls beat by beat.

## Potted-summary / apocryphal traps actively handled
- **"Fell dead-born = ignored forever."** ❌ The read frames Q12 as Hume's own 1776 verdict on the
  book's FIRST reception, and states posterity reached back past the Enquiries to treat the
  *Treatise* as his masterwork (pack Trap 4).
- **"Slave of the passions = throw out your brain / nihilism."** ❌ The read gives the two-part real
  claim: reason alone never motivates; any reason-vs-passion fight is really passion-vs-passion with
  reason only supplying facts. Wants set the destination, reason finds the route (pack §2.8, §9.7).
- **"Hume's guillotine" as Hume's term.** ❌ Not used as his phrase; the is/ought passage is read
  exactly as Hume wrote it (a NEW relation needs to be "observ'd and explain'd," a reason "should be
  given"), i.e. a demand to show the bridge, not a flat "you can never get ought from is." The
  20th-c label is only mentioned as a later label if at all (pack §2.7 author note, Trap 2).
- **The bundle theory as a finished doctrine.** ❌ The read carries Hume's OWN Appendix confession
  (Q10) that he could not make it consistent — the second loaded gun he handed his critics (after
  the missing shade of blue). This is the work read's distinctive depth over the thinker read's
  shorter treatment.
- **"Hume was an atheist."** Not in scope for this work read (the *Treatise* is Books I–III on
  understanding/passions/morals; the religion material lives in the Enquiry/Dialogues and the
  thinker read). Not asserted here.

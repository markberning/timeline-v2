## HONEST FRAMING (the read's load-bearing move — stated in-prose, hook ¶2 + Ch6 ¶1)
Existentialism as a NAMED, organized movement is 20th-century (Heidegger, Sartre, Camus, de
Beauvoir) — PAST v1's Thales→Nietzsche scope. The read centers the NINETEENTH-CENTURY ROOTS
(Kierkegaard, Nietzsche, Dostoevsky) and forward-points to the 20th-c. flowering (Ch6) as
"where the story goes next, not covered here" — explicitly labeled a forward-pointer, not a
treatment. The word "existentialism" is later (Sartre); every use of it for the 19th-c. figures
flags it as the retrospective label it is (hook ¶3, Ch2 ¶1, Ch6 ¶1).

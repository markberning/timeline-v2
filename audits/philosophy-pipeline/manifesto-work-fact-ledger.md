### Paraphrases (no quote marks — intentional)
- "two hostile camps directly facing each other" — paraphrase of ch01 "more and more
  splitting up into two great hostile camps, into two great classes directly facing each
  other — Bourgeoisie and Proletariat." Stated without quote marks; safe.
- The 10 measures (progressive income tax, abolition of inheritance, centralized credit
  in a state bank, free public education, abolition of child factory labour, abolition of
  property in land, centralization of means of production in the state) — all paraphrased
  from the ch02 numbered list (items 1, 2, 3, 5, 10). VERIFIED present in ch02; reported,
  not quoted.
- Section III rivals (feudal/clerical socialism; petty-bourgeois socialism; the
  Critical-Utopians honored-but-faulted-on-method) — reported from ch03; "Feudal
  Socialism" header VERIFIED present. Substance paraphrased.

## FINDINGS (numbered, by severity)

### 1. BLOCKER — altered quote: "every one doing his own business" (Ch3, block 6)
**Location:** Ch3 ("Three classes, a useful lie, and four virtues"), block 6.
**Text:** `…that arrangement, "every one doing his own business, and not being a busybody," is justice.`
**Problem:** The pack's VERIFIED string (Q-I, 433a–b) is **"doing one's own business, and not being a
busybody"** — it has no "every one." The draft has fused/altered the verified Jowett wording: the
words **"every one"** are inside the quotation marks but are not in the pack-verified Q-I text. (The
cross-ref `plato-fact-pack.md` §3 has yet a third variant — "every one doing his own **work**" — which
is *not* the WORK pack's authoritative wording and cannot license this either.) An altered string inside
quote marks is a quotation-integrity failure regardless of how small the change.
**Fix:** Quote the verified Q-I wording exactly: `"doing one's own business, and not being a busybody."`
Recast the sentence so the fragment is grammatical, e.g. `…that arrangement — justice as "doing one's
own business, and not being a busybody" — is the structural idea…`. Do NOT quote "every one…" unless the
coordinator fetches and verifies that exact Jowett string (the 433d "each one of us… in whom each part is
doing its own business" region exists, but is not what the pack verified).

### 2. BLOCKER — unsupported fact: the Leontius anecdote (Ch4, block 3)
**Location:** Ch4 ("The soul has the same three parts as the city"), block 3.
**Text:** The whole "A man named Leontius is walking past a place of execution where corpses lie
exposed… take your fill, then, you wretches, of the lovely sight" passage.
**Problem:** The Leontius story (Republic 439e–440a) is a real Republic passage, but it **appears
nowhere in the work fact pack** — not in §1, §2, or §6. The pack establishes the tripartite soul and the
spirited part via Q-J only; it never names Leontius, the place of execution, the covered eyes, or the
snarl at his own eyes. The pack is explicitly "the ONLY facts/quotes the draft may assert," so a named
character + scene + reported speech that is not in the pack is an unsupported assertion, exactly the class
of fact this gate blocks. (The snarl is rendered without quote marks, so it is not an *altered* quote —
but the entire anecdote is un-sourced against the pack.)
**Fix:** Either (a) the coordinator adds a VERIFIED Leontius entry to the pack (fetch Jowett 439e–440a,
match the wording, and if any of it is to be quoted, quote it verbatim), then the draft cites it; or
(b) cut the Leontius illustration and prove the spirited part from pack-supported material only (Q-J's
reason/spirit/appetite division + the reason-as-ruler / spirit-as-"subject and ally" lines). Do not ship
the named anecdote on memory.

### 3. MINOR — number not in pack: "the twelfth day" (Ch8, block 3)
**Location:** Ch8 ("Banishing the poets, and the choice of lives"), block 3.
**Text:** `…on the twelfth day, laid on his funeral pyre, he revives…`
**Problem:** Paraphrase (not in quote marks), so not a quote violation — but the only timeline figure the
pack verifies for Er is **"ten days afterwards, when the bodies of the dead were taken up"** (Q-Y, 614b,
which the pack truncates with an ellipsis *before* any "twelfth day"). "The twelfth day" is the correct
standard Jowett reading (bodies taken up at ten days, revival on the pyre at twelve) but it is a specific
number the pack does not carry, and the draft also drops the "ten days" detail the pack *does* carry.
**Fix:** Prefer the pack-supported figure — e.g. "ten days after the battle, his body uncorrupted, he was
laid on the funeral pyre and came back to life" — or have the coordinator extend the Q-Y verification to
include the "twelfth day" clause before asserting it.

### 4. MINOR — quoted phrases drawn from the pack's summary prose, not its VERIFIED ledger
**Locations / strings:**
- Ch1 b4: `"like a wild beast"` (attributed to "Plato's narrator").
- Ch8 b2: `"the great leader of tragedy"` (Homer) and `"the ancient quarrel between philosophy and poetry."`
**Problem:** All three are inside quotation marks in the draft, but each appears in the work pack only in
its §1 argument-arc *summary prose* (the pack author's own gloss — "the sophist, bursting in 'like a wild
beast'"; Homer "the great leader of tragedy") and NOT in the §2/§6 VERIFIED-quotations ledger, which is the
pack's discipline-bearing list ("only §2/§6 strings are fetched-and-matched Jowett wording"). They are very
likely authentic Jowett (336b; 595/607b), but the gate cannot certify a quotation that the pack did not
verify as a quotation.
**Fix:** Either the coordinator promotes each to a VERIFIED §2 entry (fetch + match the exact Jowett line),
or the draft drops the quote marks and paraphrases (e.g. *Thrasymachus lunges in like a wild beast*; *Homer,
the greatest of the tragedians*; *the old quarrel between philosophy and poetry*).

### 5. MINOR — Popper biography not in pack (Ch7, block 6)
**Location:** Ch7 ("Five regimes, and the most miserable man alive"), block 6.
**Text:** `…Karl Popper — an Austrian who had fled Nazi Europe — published…`
**Problem:** Historically accurate (Popper was Vienna-born and emigrated before WWII), but the pack
(TRAP 2) supplies only "Karl Popper, *The Open Society and Its Enemies* Vol. I (1945)" — no nationality,
no emigration biography. Per the pack-traceability rule, the "Austrian who had fled Nazi Europe" gloss is
an out-of-pack assertion.
**Fix:** Trim to pack-supported content ("the philosopher Karl Popper, in *The Open Society and Its
Enemies* (1945)…"), or have the coordinator add a one-line VERIFIED Popper-bio note to the pack.

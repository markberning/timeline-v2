## Voice / landmine handling
- **No em-dashes in narration** (brief rule 4): used periods/commas/colons/parentheses only;

- **No reader-coaching / no throat-clearing / no editorial "we must"** (rules 1–3, 7): swept.

  afterlife chapter and glossed (reality as mind-structured / self-unfolding Spirit), per brief.

  apocryphal "trivial"); pack §3.C blacklist #7 enforced.
- **Thesis–antithesis–synthesis**: NOT invoked (this read does not touch it).

  Reason*, not the *Groundwork*; not quoted here at all).

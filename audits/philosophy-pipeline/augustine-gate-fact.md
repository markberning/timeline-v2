## Bottom line

**FIX-THEN-SHIP** — one MUST-FIX: the *Confessions* VI grief quote (F1) is a real
misquote inside quotation marks ("clave unto her was torn" → Pusey reads "clave to
her, was racked"). It must be corrected to the exact Pusey wording **`"my heart, which
clave to her, was racked, and wounded, and bleeding."`** (or dropped to paraphrase),
and the fact pack §1 / ledger line corrected at the same time since the error
originates there. Every other quote ships verbatim from a verified PD translation,
every date/place/sequence checks out, the paraphrase-only lines (si fallor sum, noli
foras ire) are correctly never quote-marked, and the contested doctrines
(predestination, original sin, Donatist coercion) are named straight without
laundering or anachronism. Fix F1 and it's clean.

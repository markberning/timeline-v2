## Position-summary discipline (potted-summary traps)
- **"Hume was an atheist."** NOT asserted. The *Enquiry* itself never states atheism;
  the fork forbids it (God's existence is a matter of fact, settled only by evidence; the
  honest verdict is suspension). The read handles this exactly as the thinker page does.
- **Induction conclusion** stated precisely: nothing in *reason or experience* justifies
  the expectation that the future resembles the past; Hume does NOT say the sunrise is
  unlikely or that one should stop trusting it — custom, not proof, carries daily life.
- **Causation** stated precisely: we observe constant conjunction, never the necessary
  connection; the idea of necessity is traced to the mind's habit of expectation, then
  projected onto the world. Not "Hume denied causation."
- **Miracles** stated precisely: Hume does NOT say miracles are impossible; he builds an
  evidential scale (§10) on which testimony for a law-violation essentially never clears
  the bar set by uniform experience.
- **Fork** stated precisely as a sorting test, not "Hume invented analytic/synthetic"
  (that is Kant's later, different distinction — not claimed here).

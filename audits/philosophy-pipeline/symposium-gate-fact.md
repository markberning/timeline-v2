## BLOCKERS

**B1 — Miscount of the love-speeches in the hook (and Ch1), self-contradicting the break block.**
- Location: `hook[0]` ("seven speeches about love, and then a drunk gatecrasher"); `hook[0]`
  earlier ("seven men… each give a speech in praise of Eros"); Ch1 block 4 ("Seven speeches,
  one after another"). Compare break block para 1, which correctly says "first five speeches"
  (Phaedrus, Pausanias, Eryximachus, Aristophanes, Agathon) and closes "Five praises of Eros."
- Problem: The dialogue has SIX encomia in praise of Eros — Phaedrus, Pausanias, Eryximachus,
  Aristophanes, Agathon, then Socrates (reporting Diotima) — followed by Alcibiades, whose
  seventh speech praises SOCRATES, not Love. The hook presents "seven speeches about love" PLUS
  "a drunk gatecrasher," which counts eight speakers (5 + Socrates as a 6th love-speech is not
  even reached, then 7 named love-speeches, then Alcibiades). It also contradicts the break
  block, which builds on five-pre-Socrates speeches (5 + Socrates = six love-speeches). A reader
  who counts the chapters (Phaedrus…Alcibiades = 7 speakers, 6 of them about love) will catch the
  mismatch. The fact pack itself is loose here (it says "seven speeches in praise of Eros" in
  some places but lists Alcibiades as the 7th speaker who praises Socrates) — but the draft must
  not inherit the looseness as a reader-facing claim.
- Fix: Make the count consistent and correct. Either "six speeches in praise of Love, and then a
  drunk gatecrasher who breaks the rules" (Alcibiades = the 7th speaker, praising Socrates), or
  "seven speeches — six about Love, the last about Socrates." Recommended hook line: "seven
  speakers; the first six praise Love, and then a drunk gatecrasher praises Socrates instead."
  Mirror the fix in Ch1 block 4 ("Seven speeches, one after another" → seven speeches, the last
  one not about Love at all / six in praise of Love then Alcibiades). Keep the break block as-is
  (its "five before Socrates" framing is correct).

## MINOR

**M2 — "only as a term of reproach" is an altered quotation (a word dropped from inside the marks).**
- Location: Ch3 block 3 — the "man-woman" "(a word, he notes, that survives now \"only as a term
  of reproach\")".
- Problem: Jowett (pack line 286) reads "the word 'Androgynous' is **only preserved as a term of
  reproach**." The draft's quoted fragment "only as a term of reproach" silently drops "preserved,"
  so the quotation marks enclose wording that is not Jowett's verbatim.
- Fix: Either restore the verbatim — "only preserved as a term of reproach" — or move the word out
  of quotes: survives "only … as a term of reproach" / paraphrase it ("survives now only as an
  insult").

**M3 — "seriously enamoured of my beauty" is quoted but is NOT in the §2/§6 VERIFIED ledger.**
- Location: Ch7 block 3 — Socrates was "\"seriously enamoured of my beauty\"".
- Problem: This phrase appears in the pack only in §1 prose (line 193) inside quote marks; it is
  NOT among the VERIFIED quotations (§2 Q-M / §6 ledger). Q-M's verified text is "he was so
  superior to my solicitations, so contemptuous and derisive and disdainful of my beauty…".
  Presenting an unverified-in-ledger phrase inside quote marks is exactly the risk the gate guards
  against — and Jowett's actual wording at the relevant locus uses the third person ("he fancied
  that I was seriously enamoured of **his** beauty"), so the pronoun in the draft's first-person
  quote may not match the source.
- Fix: Have the coordinator/fetcher pin the exact Jowett wording + pronoun before ship. Until
  confirmed, render as paraphrase (no quotes): "certain that Socrates was smitten with his beauty."
  The surrounding Q-M quotes ("so superior to my solicitations…", "I arose as from the couch of a
  father or an elder brother") are VERIFIED and fine.

**M4 — "love the body rather than the soul" is quoted but is NOT in the §2/§6 VERIFIED ledger.**
- Location: Ch2 block 4 — people who "\"love the body rather than the soul.\"".
- Problem: The pack's quoted form (line 118, §1 prose only) is "the body rather than the soul" —
  it is not in the §2/§6 VERIFIED ledger. The draft additionally inserts "love" inside the marks
  ("love the body rather than the soul"), compounding the un-verified status with an inserted word.
- Fix: Drop the word from the quote and/or confirm against Jowett before ship: render the common
  love as "drawn to the body rather than the soul" (paraphrase), or quote only the verified-pending
  fragment "the body rather than the soul" after the fetcher confirms it.

**M5 — Phaedrus "eldest of the gods" quote elides "which is an honour to him" without an ellipsis.**
- Location: Ch2 block 1 — "\"He is the eldest of the gods,\" Phaedrus says, \"and a proof of his
  claim to this honour is, that of his parents there is no memorial…\"".
- Problem: Jowett (Q-B) reads "For he is the eldest of the gods, **which is an honour to him**; and
  a proof of his claim to this honour is…". The draft splits the line into two quoted chunks across
  the attribution, which silently omits "which is an honour to him" between them. The two chunks
  read as contiguous; they are not.
- Fix: This is borderline (the two-chunk split arguably signals the elision), but to be clean either
  quote the bridge in full or insert an ellipsis: "\"He is the eldest of the gods,\" Phaedrus says,
  \"… and a proof of his claim to this honour is…\"". Low severity — the wording inside each chunk
  is verbatim Jowett.

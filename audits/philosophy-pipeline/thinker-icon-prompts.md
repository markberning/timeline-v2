# Thinker icon prompts (for ChatGPT / DALL·E)

Generate each as its own image in ChatGPT. **Save each as `<id>.png`** (the id is the
left column) into a folder — `~/Downloads/thinker-icons/` is fine. Then tell me, and I'll
trim/key them (white → transparent), tint each to its school color, and wire them into the
Thinkers list, the thinker hubs, and the argument-map nodes — same pipeline as the civ emblems.

## Style (already baked into every prompt below — matches the civ emblems)
> A minimalist flat ICON, a single solid **black silhouette on a plain solid white background**.
> Bold simple shapes, clean smooth vector look, centered with generous margin. One symbol only.
> No text, letters, or numbers. No gradient, no shading, no 3D, no drop shadow, no outline, no scene.

Tip: paste the style line once, then just append "… : <the symbol>" for each. Or copy a full line below.

## Prompts — one per thinker

**Pre-Socratics**
- `thales` — Thales: a single falling water droplet.
- `pythagoras` — Pythagoras: a tetractys (an upward-pointing triangle built from ten dots, in rows of 1, 2, 3, 4).
- `heraclitus` — Heraclitus: a single tongue of flame.
- `parmenides` — Parmenides: one perfect, solid, smooth sphere.

**Socrates (Independent)**
- `socrates` — Socrates: a simple footed drinking cup (the cup of hemlock).

**Platonism**
- `plato` — Plato: the mouth of a cave opening onto a bright radiant light beyond.
- `plotinus` — Plotinus: concentric rings rippling outward from one central dot (emanation from the One).
- `augustine` — Augustine: a heart wrapped in flame (the Augustinian flaming heart).

**Aristotelians**
- `aristotle` — Aristotle: a branching tree, trunk dividing into ordered limbs (the classification of nature).
- `avicenna` — Avicenna: a crescent moon cradling a single small star.
- `averroes` — Averroes: two equal overlapping circles meeting in the middle (reason and faith).
- `maimonides` — Maimonides: a six-pointed Star of David.

**Scholastics**
- `anselm` — Anselm: a tall, slender pointed Gothic arch.
- `aquinas` — Aquinas: a radiant sun with clean straight rays.

**Stoics**
- `zeno` — Zeno of Citium: a Doric portico, a roof on a row of columns (the Painted Porch).
- `epictetus` — Epictetus: a broken chain, an open shackle with a snapped link.
- `aurelius` — Marcus Aurelius: a laurel wreath, an open circlet of leaves.

**Rationalists**
- `descartes` — Descartes: a Cartesian coordinate grid, an x and y axis crossing with faint grid lines.
- `spinoza` — Spinoza: a draftsman's compass, open, as if drawing a circle (the geometric method).

**Empiricists**
- `hobbes` — Hobbes: a single crown (the sovereign Leviathan).
- `locke` — Locke: a blank rectangular writing slate / tablet (the blank slate).
- `berkeley` — Berkeley: one open eye (to be is to be perceived).
- `hume` — Hume: two billiard balls, one just touching the next.

**German Idealists**
- `kant` — Kant: a small cluster of stars in the night sky (the starry heavens above).
- `hegel` — Hegel: a single spiral coiling upward and outward (Spirit unfolding through the dialectic).
- `fichte` — Fichte: a circle with an arrow curving back into its own center (the self positing itself).

**Utilitarians**
- `bentham` — Bentham: a balanced set of scales, two equal pans level (the calculus of happiness).
- `mill` — Mill: a single torch held aloft, burning (liberty).
- `taylor` — Harriet Taylor: two identical interlocking rings, perfectly equal (equality / partnership).

**Existentialist**
- `kierkegaard` — Kierkegaard: a lone figure mid-leap across a gap between two cliff edges (the leap of faith).

**Independent (late)**
- `schopenhauer` — Schopenhauer: a churning whirlpool / spiral of water (the blind, striving will).
- `marx` — Marx: a single clenched fist raised (the point is to change it).
- `nietzsche` — Nietzsche: a single blacksmith's hammer (philosophizing with a hammer).

**3. BLOCKER — Ch1 epigraph wording drifts from the verified Jowett text.** Draft epigraph reads:
> "...the good is not only the author of knowledge to all things known, but of their being and essence, and yet the good is not essence, but far exceeds essence in dignity and power."

The VERIFIED Jowett text (plato-fact-pack §3, Republic VI) is:
> "In like manner **the good may be said to be** not only the author of knowledge to all things known, but of their being and essence, and yet the good is not essence, but far exceeds essence in dignity and power."

The draft opens mid-sentence with an ellipsis ("...the good is not only..."), which silently drops "In like manner the good may be said to be." Truncating a verified quote with a leading ellipsis is acceptable IF the retained words match the source verbatim — and they do here ("the good is not only the author of knowledge..." matches "...the good **may be said to be** not only the author..." — wait: the draft reads "the good **is** not only the author" but Jowett reads "the good **may be said to be** not only the author"). **The draft has compressed "may be said to be not only" into "is not only" — this is a wording alteration inside quotation marks, not a clean truncation.** **Fix:** either (a) restore the exact Jowett wording — "...the good may be said to be not only the author of knowledge to all things known, but of their being and essence..." — or (b) start the ellipsis after the altered clause so only verbatim words remain, e.g. "...the author of knowledge to all things known, but of their being and essence, and yet the good is not essence, but far exceeds essence in dignity and power." Option (b) is cleanest. As written it is a **BLOCKER** (altered quote).

## Verified biography (SEP, Britannica, IEP, Wikipedia, Aeon, Philosophy Now)
- **Born 19 May 1762, Rammenau, Saxony**, eldest son of **poor ribbon-weavers**.
  [SEP: "poor and pious ribbon weavers"; Britannica; Philosophy Now]
- **Patron**: the **Baron von Miltitz** (Ernst Haubold von Miltitz) sponsored his
  education after the (traditional, oft-told) episode of the boy reciting a sermon the
  baron had missed; sent to school and then to the **Pforta** boarding school near
  Naumburg (later Nietzsche's school), and to the universities of **Jena and
  Leipzig**. The patron later died and support ended; Fichte could not finish his
  degree and worked for years as a **private tutor**. [Philosophy Now; SEP; Britannica;
  Wikipedia]. *Framing*: the sermon-recitation is told as the tradition it is ("in the
  tradition that has come down," "the story goes"), not asserted flat.
- **1790 Kant conversion**: while tutoring he took up Kant's philosophy to teach it
  and described it as a "revolution" in his thinking; took from Kant the **primacy of
  the practical** / human freedom. [SEP]
- **1791** traveled to Königsberg to meet Kant (first meeting cool); wrote the
  *Attempt at a Critique of All Revelation* to introduce himself. [SEP; IEP]
- **1792**: *Attempt at a Critique of All Revelation* published **anonymously by
  Kant's own publisher**; "quickly and widely hailed as a work by Kant himself"; Kant
  then **publicly identified Fichte as the author and praised him**, making Fichte
  instantly famous. [SEP, verbatim "hailed as a work by Kant himself"; IEP; Wikipedia]
- **1794**: appointed to the **chair of philosophy at the University of Jena**. [SEP]
- **1798–99 Atheism Controversy (*Atheismusstreit*)**: triggered by his **1798 essay
  "On the Basis/Ground of Our Belief in a Divine Governance of the World"** (God = the
  moral world-order, not a separate personal substance); accusations of atheism; he
  was **forced to resign / dismissed from Jena in 1799**. [SEP; Encyclopedia.com
  "Atheismusstreit"; Wikipedia "Atheism dispute"]
- **Berlin** thereafter (no chair; public lecturing/writing). [SEP; Wikipedia]
- **1806** Napoleon defeats Prussia (Jena–Auerstedt); **French occupation of Berlin**.
  [Wikipedia; standard history]
- **1807–08**: delivered the **Addresses to the German Nation** in French-occupied
  Berlin (lectures winter 1807–1808). [SEP "Addresses to the German Nation (1808)";
  Wikipedia; Wikisource "Addresses to the German Nation"]
- **1810** became **first rector of the new University of Berlin** (later Humboldt
  Univ.). [SEP; Wikipedia]
- **Died 29 January 1814, Berlin**, of **typhus** caught from his wife **Johanna**,
  who contracted a fever nursing soldiers in the wars of liberation; she recovered, he
  did not. [Wikipedia, SEP "fatal infection contracted from his wife, who worked as a
  volunteer nurse"]

## The system (paraphrase, tied to verified sources — no quote marks except above)
- **Wissenschaftslehre = "Science of Knowledge"** (Kroeger's title); Fichte rewrote
  it repeatedly and was never satisfied (well-documented; SEP). [SEP; Kroeger]
- **First principle**: the I/Ego posits itself (the §1 quote). The self is an *act*,
  not a thing; self-positing is the one unconditioned starting point. [Kroeger §1; SEP]
- **Second**: the I posits a **not-I / Non-Ego** against itself (the world as what the
  active self sets opposite itself). **Third**: I and not-I mutually limit each other
  within the I. [SEP "I posits itself; Not-I limits the I; synthesis"; Kroeger
  structure] — PARAPHRASED, not quoted (see quote-discipline note).
- **The check / *Anstoss***: the I meets a limit/impact it does not dictate, from
  which the determinate world unfolds; SEP names the *Anstoß* as "the 'check' essential
  for consciousness." Used to steelman AND to name the standard objection (does the
  check smuggle a thing-in-itself back in?). [SEP]
- **Primacy of the practical**: "the practical power is the innermost root of the I"
  (SEP); the I is fundamentally striving/willing, knowing serves willing; freedom is
  the ground, not a conclusion. [SEP]
- **Endless striving**: the I can never complete the conquest of the not-I (completion
  would dissolve the opposition selfhood needs); infinite striving. [SEP; standard]
- **The philosophy is the man / idealism vs dogmatism**: the choice of philosophy
  reflects the kind of person one is; idealism starts from the free self, dogmatism
  from given things; no proof forces the move (Introduction §5, the verified quote).
  [Kroeger First Introduction; SEP]

## LANDMINE — thesis/antithesis/synthesis (handled per brief §62)
- The **triad of terms IS Fichte's** (the §§1–3 structure of the Science of
  Knowledge: positing / opposing / reconciling = thesis / antithesis / synthesis).
  The read explains it accurately as Fichte's. [Kroeger structure; Wikipedia: "Fichte
  was also the originator of thesis–antithesis–synthesis… often erroneously attributed
  to Hegel"; academia.edu "The Fichtean Shadow"]
- The **popular ascription to Hegel is the Chalybäus misattribution**: Heinrich Moritz
  Chalybäus, *Historical Survey of Speculative Philosophy from Kant to Hegel* (Dresden
  lectures pub. **1837**), recast Hegel's procedure in crisp Fichtean
  thesis/antithesis/synthesis terms; the label stuck via textbooks. Debunked by
  **Gustav Mueller, "The Hegel Legend of 'Thesis-Antithesis-Synthesis'" (1958)** and
  by Walter Kaufmann. **[Hegel](/philosophy/thinker/hegel)** used the words rarely and
  never as his engine; his real motor is **Aufhebung** (sublation: cancel/preserve/
  lift). The read states this and links Hegel. [Mueller 1958; Wikipedia Chalybäus; SEP]

## Nationalist afterlife of the *Addresses* (handled per brief — motive→effect, no lecture)
- Fichte's nationalism in the *Addresses* was **mainly cultural/moral** (shared
  language, education, a spiritual vocation of freedom), NOT ethnic-supremacist; SEP/
  scholars characterize it as "cultural, and grounded in aesthetic, literary, and
  moral principles." [Wikipedia; SEP]
- **Later appropriation**: the **National Socialists** (a century-plus later) claimed
  Fichte as a nationalist forerunner and quarried the *Addresses* for slogans —
  documented effect, named honestly. [Wikipedia]
- **Complication held honestly, not resolved**: an **early unpublished piece (1793)**
  contains antisemitic statements about Jewish civil rights; YET Fichte's **final act
  was resigning the Berlin rectorship in protest** when colleagues refused to punish
  the harassment of Jewish students. The ledger and read present both and do not force
  a clean verdict. [Wikipedia]

## Anecdote / overclaim discipline (framing)
- **Sermon recitation → patronage**: told as the received tradition ("in the tradition
  that has come down," "the story goes"), the documented fact being only that Miltitz
  funded the education. Not asserted as verbatim history.
- **Death cause**: typhus from the wife's nursing is the standard account (SEP gives
  "fatal infection contracted from his wife"); typhus named with "very likely."
- **Atheismusstreit**: his God-as-moral-order position stated as his actual view; the
  "atheism" charge framed as his enemies' reading, which is what it was.
- **Nazi appropriation**: documented effect, not turned into a sermon; the 1793
  antisemitism and the rectorship-resignation are BOTH given and left unresolved.

## FRAMING TRAPS — all six confirmed honored

1. **Title = "after the *Physics*," not "beyond the physical."** Honored repeatedly and
   emphatically (hook ¶2; Ch.1 ¶4 spells out the Latin "after"→"beyond" misreading,
   Andronicus c. 70 BCE, shelving label + study-order hint). ✔
2. **Compilation of 14 treatises, not one composed book.** Honored (throughline closing;
   hook ¶2–3; Ch.1 ¶6; Ch.8 ¶2 "It is, remember, a compilation, not a composed book").
   The draft never writes "as the *Metaphysics* unfolds, Aristotle next proves…" as if
   one argument. ✔
3. **Prime Mover moves as FINAL cause / "as being loved," not by pushing; is "thought
   thinking itself."** Honored, and the load-bearing PRECISION-NOTE-mechanism is hit
   dead-on: Ch.7 ¶3 "does *not* push. It doesn't reach out and shove…sets nothing going
   by force…by being *desired*." ✔
4. **NOT the personal creator-God; no fusion with Aquinas.** Honored cleanly: Ch.7 ¶6
   "*not* the personal God of later religion…did not create the world…answers no
   prayers," and the Aquinas fusion is explicitly dated and attributed as Aquinas's
   thirteenth-century move, not Aristotle's. ✔
5. **Forms critique = serious, respectful disagreement (Plato's student ~20 years).**
   Honored (break ¶2; Ch.3 ¶1 "twenty years…not a silly idea to swat away"; Ch.3 ¶5
   "respectful…not score-settling…truth has to come first"). ✔
6. **BCE/CE, never BC/AD.** Draft uses "70 BCE" (hook ¶2, Ch.1 ¶4). No BC/AD anywhere.
   ✔ (Note: the draft is internally clean here even though the shipped thinker read uses
   "BC" — see Finding 3.)

## FACT-CHECK FINDINGS

### 1. [MINOR] Ch.2 ¶4 — Anaxagoras "Mind" placed as *efficient* cause; pack says it under "source of motion." OK, but verify the gloss isn't overstated.
**Location:** Ch.2, block 4: "Anaxagoras, who said a cosmic **Mind** set the world in
order, had reached, however dimly, for an efficient cause."
**Status:** Traceable. Pack §Book I bullet (lines 99–104) lists "Anaxagoras' Mind" among
predecessors groping at the four causes, and §1 frames the predecessors as half-seeing
the scheme; the thinker-read/§4d associate Mind with the source-of-motion/efficient
cause. This is consistent with the pack's "each having groped at one or more of these
four causes." **No change required** — flagged only to confirm it was checked. Not a
blocker.

### 2. [MINOR] Ch.2 ¶4 — "like untrained fighters who land a good blow now and then by luck" — is this Aristotle's image or the author's?
**Location:** Ch.2, block 4 closing simile.
**Problem:** Aristotle does use a "like untrained men in a fight / they strike random
blows" image of his predecessors (Ross I.4, ~985a). But that exact line was **NOT
fetched into this pack** — it is not among Q-A…Q-R and §5.5 lists Book I beyond the
verified quotes as paraphrase-safe only. The draft does **not** put the simile in quote
marks (it's narrated as the author's paraphrase, "like untrained fighters…"), so it does
**not** violate the quote doctrine. It is a real Aristotelian image rendered as
paraphrase.
**Severity:** MINOR / advisory. It is defensible (the image is genuinely Aristotle's and
the draft paraphrases rather than quotes). If the gate wants zero reliance on
un-fetched-in-pack content, soften to a plainer paraphrase. **Recommended:** leave as-is
(paraphrase of a documented Aristotle image), or have the author confirm against Ross
I.4 if a more cautious posture is preferred. Not ship-blocking.

### 3. [MINOR — cross-pack, informational only; NOT this file's fix] Aquinas "Sixteen centuries later" vs thinker read's "Six centuries later."

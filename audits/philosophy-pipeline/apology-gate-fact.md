## Findings

### 1. MINOR — quote modernizes "minæ" → "minae" *inside* quotation marks (Ch6)
**Location:** Ch6, block 4 (line 181): `"Plato, Crito, Critobulus, and Apollodorus, my
friends here, bid me say thirty minae, and they will be the sureties."`
**Problem:** The VERIFIED Jowett wording (pack Q-N, line 398) is "thirty **minæ**" with the
ligature. The pack's own discipline (§5.6, line 543) is explicit: outside quote-marks you
may modernize the ligature, but **inside a quote you must keep "minæ" or note the
modernization.** The draft silently changes the verified text inside the quote. Same issue
does NOT affect the earlier "five minae" (line 81), which the draft renders as paraphrase
*outside* quotation marks — that one is fine.
**Fix:** Either render `"…bid me say thirty minæ…"` inside the quote, or add a tiny
in-prose note that the ligature is modernized. Trivial; pick one.

### 2. MINOR — several short Jowett phrases are quoted but live only in the pack's §1 arc, not in the §2 VERIFIED ledger
**Locations / phrases (all inside quotation marks in the draft):**
- Ch5 b1 (line 144): `"the conceit that a man knows what he does not know"` — pack carries it
  quoted only at §1 line 163; no Q-tag, no Stephanus locus, not in the §2 ledger or §6 table.
- Ch5 b3 (line 150): `"you will sleep on for the remainder of your lives"` — pack §1 line 173
  (quoted there); not laddered into §2 (Q-L stops at "…reproaching you").
- Ch5 b3 (line 150): `"a bad man is not permitted to injure a better than himself"` — pack §1
  line 170 (quoted there); not in §2.
- Ch5 b4 (line 153): `"will not save his life"` — pack §1 line 181 (quoted there); not in §2.
- Ch5 b6 (line 159): `"make a present of justice"` and `"to you and to God"` — pack §1
  lines 192–193 (quoted there); not in §2.
**Problem:** The gate rule is that every quotation-marked passage be a VERIFIED Jowett quote
*in the pack with a confirmed locus*. These phrases ARE present in the pack and ARE shown in
quote marks there, so they are not invented — but they sit in the §1 argument-arc summary,
which was not the "fetched-and-matched word-for-word" §2 ledger. They were never promoted to
a Q-entry with a Stephanus reference and a VERIFIED tag.
**Fix:** Pack-side (not a draft edit): confirm each against Jowett and add a Q-entry/locus, OR
the coordinator confirms these §1 quotes are equally fetched-verified. Wording in the draft
matches the pack's §1 quoted form exactly, so this is a provenance/ledger gap, not an altered
quote. Keep as MINOR — but it must be closed before ship, because "quoted in §1 prose" is a
weaker warrant than the §2 discipline the rest of the quotes meet.

### 3. MINOR — paraphrase near a quote drifts one notch from the VERIFIED impiety-trap wording (Ch4)
**Location:** Ch4, block 3 (line 128).
**Problem:** The draft renders the impiety-contradiction in its own voice as *spiritual or
divine agencies* (italic) and "spirits or demigods … either gods or the **children** of
gods." The VERIFIED Jowett wording (pack Q-I note, lines 327–328) is "spiritual **and**
divine agencies", "spirits or demigods", and "the **sons** of gods." Critically, the draft
keeps these **outside** quotation marks (italics / em-dashes), so this is NOT a
quote-integrity violation — nothing inside quote marks is altered. It is only worth noting
because the verified phrasing was right there and the paraphrase swaps "and"→"or" and
"sons"→"children."
**Fix:** Optional. If you want the verified ring, restore "spiritual and divine agencies"
and "sons of gods" (or quote them outright). No change strictly required.

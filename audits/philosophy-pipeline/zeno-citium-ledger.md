### Ch 2 — Live in agreement
- Telos: Zeno's ORIGINAL "living consistently" / homologoumenōs (Stobaeus 63B + DL VII.87
  tradition; SEP §2); Cleanthes ADDED "with nature" — pack §5a. Paraphrase-only.
- DL VII.87 "life in agreement with nature" = "the same as a virtuous life" — framed as
  DL's record of the school's teaching, not a Zeno quote — pack §5a, §10.
- "According to nature" = reason/logos (human + cosmic), NOT camping/simple-living — §5a.
- Heraclitean logos inheritance flagged (deepened in ch5) — pack §9.

### Ch 3 — Virtue is the only good
- Virtue (aretē) the only good, vice the only evil; everything else adiaphora/indifferent
  (health, wealth, reputation, life) — pack §5b (DL VII.94ff, doctrinal consensus).
- Preferred (proēgmena) vs dispreferred (apoproēgmena) indifferents — the softening that
  keeps it from being absurd; doctor example — pack §5b.
- The sage; Cynic kernel softened into "preferred indifferents" — pack §5b, §9.
- Potted-summary rescue: not numb; not "wealth doesn't matter at all" — pack §5b, §15.1.

### Ch 4 — The grip of certainty
- Phantasia → synkatathesis (assent) → katalēpsis (graspable impression) → epistēmē;
  the kataleptic impression as criterion of truth; "could not have come from something
  not the case" — pack §5c (Cicero Acad. II.144; doctrine consensus). Paraphrase.
- Cicero's fist-demonstration (open hand=impression, fingers in=assent, fist=comprehension,
  fist clenched by other hand=knowledge, only the wise have it); Zeno coined katalēpsis
  from katalambanein (to grasp) — pack §5c. **Framed as Cicero reporting/reconstructing
  ~200 yrs later, in a dialogue. [VERIFY] on the sourceUrl** — pack §5c, §10, brief.
- Academic Skeptics (Arcesilaus → Carneades) attacked the criterion — pack §5c, §9.
- Passions (pathē) as errors of JUDGMENT: distress/fear/desire/pleasure as false value-
  judgments; correction not suppression; eupatheiai — pack §5d. Forward to Epictetus'
  "disturbed not by things but by opinions" as LATER formulation — pack §5d.

### Ch 5 — The architecture
- Three parts: logic, physics, ethics — pack §4 (DL VII.39–40). Attributed to the SCHOOL
  (whether Zeno or Chrysippus formalized = contested; framed so) — §4.
- Three analogies — animal (bones/flesh/soul), egg (shell/white/yolk), fertile field
  (fence/crop/soil) — pack §4, DL VII.40, framed as DL tradition.
- Heraclitus-derived fire/logos physics; universe as rational living whole — pack §9.
  (Kept at Zeno-altitude; pneuma/cylinder-of-fate are Chrysippean, NOT pinned to Zeno —
  pack §7 framing rule.)

### Ch 6 — The founder and the school
- Cleanthes (c. 330–230 BC, Hymn to Zeus survives, added "with nature", faithful
  transmitter) — pack §7.
- Chrysippus (c. 279–206 BC, 700+ works, propositional logic, systematizer); "if
  Chrysippus had not existed there would have been no Stoa" = DL VII.183 report, NOT
  Zeno's own words — pack §7, §15.5. "Zeno founded, Chrysippus built" — §7.
- Zeno's *Republic* framed at its evidentiary level: lost, known via hostile critics
  (Cassius) + DL; community of wives, no temples/courts/gymnasia, no money, citizenship
  of the virtuous; written response to Plato (consensus, text lost so intent uncertain);
  later school embarrassed, "young & thoughtless" Cynic-phase apologia (accurate or
  damage-control = unknowable) — pack §6. Not absorbed into mature system, not erased.
- Roman Stoa: Seneca, Epictetus, Marcus Aurelius — Meditations exists because Zeno opened
  a school in a colonnade — pack §9, §10.4. (Marcus = surviving first-person Stoic voice,
  but ~450 yrs later, system is Chrysippean as much as Zenonian — §10.4.)
- Why inner freedom outlived its texts — pack §0, §9 (Christian appropriation noted
  lightly), the silence-as-content throughline.

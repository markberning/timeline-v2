## Blacklist-trap handling (confirmation)

- **Cogito source (§9 #1):** Latin "COGITO ERGO SUM" appears only as Veitch's
  parenthetical gloss (*Discourse* 1637) and as a real axiom in the *Principles*
  (1644). The *Meditations* form is given as "I am, I exist" with NO "therefore."
  Draft Ch1 walks all three loci explicitly. HANDLED.
- **Hobbes (§9 #2):** Draft quotes "every man, against every man" and "solitary,
  poore, nasty, brutish, and short"; explicitly flags "war of all against all" as a
  later gloss, never quoting it as his phrase. HANDLED.
- **Locke (§9 #3):** Draft quotes "white paper" and "empty cabinet"; states *tabula
  rasa* appears nowhere in the *Essay* and was pinned on Locke later. Never quoted as
  his words. HANDLED. Also "consent of the people" (Locke) vs. "consent of the
  governed" (Jefferson) per §9 #13. HANDLED.
- **Berkeley steelman (§9 #4):** Draft explicitly preserves tables/chairs/trees,
  rejects only mind-independent matter; runs the steelman ("Locke's most rigorous
  student"), names and answers the "bumped into the table" cartoon. HANDLED.
- **Leibniz "best of all possible worlds" (§9 #5):** Not quoted as Leibniz's; given
  as Voltaire's *Candide* line; the silence is marked in the key-passage block.
  HANDLED.
- **Hume not an atheist (§9 #6):** Draft Ch7 explicitly refuses the label and explains
  why. HANDLED.
- **"I never asserted so absurd a proposition…" (§9 #7):** ABSENT from draft entirely.
- **"Dogmatic slumber" (§9 #8):** Mentioned only as Kant's later move (next era),
  attributed to Kant, NOT quoted, NOT given as Hume's words. HANDLED.
- **Descartes "proved God" (§9 #9):** Draft gives the argument then the Arnauld/
  Cartesian-circle objection; success denied. HANDLED.
- **Stove vs. dreams (§9 #10):** Draft uses the heated-room as Descartes' own account
  and does not assert the Baillet dreams. HANDLED.
- **Herem cause unknown (§9 #11):** Draft states the cause is unrecorded; invents
  nothing. HANDLED.
- **No "God is dead" (§9 #12):** Absent.
- **Elisabeth as thinker (§9 #15):** Draft gives her the central interaction-problem
  objection with full force in Ch1. HANDLED.

## Findings

### 1. MINOR — "longest single treatment of any virtue" vs. friendship as "single longest stretch"
**Location:** Ch5 block 1 (line 156): Book V justice called "the longest single treatment of any virtue." Ch7 block 1 (line 206): friendship called "the single longest stretch of the *Ethics*."
**Problem:** The two superlatives sit close enough to read as competing. The pack frames friendship (VIII–IX, two books) as "the single longest treatment in the NE," and Book V as "a whole Book... the most complex virtue" — it does NOT itself assert Book V is the longest single-virtue treatment.
**Assessment:** Reconcilable and almost certainly intended — justice = longest treatment *within one Book*; friendship = longest *overall* (two Books). Both claims are defensible and the draft does distinguish them ("any virtue" / "single book" vs. "stretch of the *Ethics*"). Not a fact error.
**Fix (optional polish, not ship-blocking):** Tighten Ch5 to "the longest treatment of any single virtue — a whole Book" so the friendship superlative two chapters later reads as unmistakably the broader one. Leave as-is is acceptable.

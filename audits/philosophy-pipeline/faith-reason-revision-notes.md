**E4 (MUST-FIX 4, also comprehensiveness SC-1) — Hildegard of Bingen.** Ch 5, one
focused paragraph. Used only pack §1/§4 material: given as a child to a religious

**E5 (SHOULD-FIX 2) — schools as agents not victims.** Ch 4 close. "would have no idea

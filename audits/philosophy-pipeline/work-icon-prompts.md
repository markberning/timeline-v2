# Work icon prompts (for ChatGPT / DALL·E)

Same pipeline as the thinker + civ emblems. **Save each as `<id>.png`** (the id is the
left column) into a folder — `~/Downloads/work-icons/` is fine. Then tell me, and I'll
trim/key them (white → transparent), tint each to its author's school color, and wire
them into the works lists + work hubs (replacing the date glyph).

Each emblem names the WORK'S central idea or signature image, and is deliberately chosen
to be DISTINCT from its author's thinker emblem (so Plato's four works don't all repeat
the cave).

## Style (paste once, then append "… : <the symbol>" per line — matches the existing emblems)
> A minimalist flat ICON, a single solid **black silhouette on a plain solid white background**.
> Bold simple shapes, clean smooth vector look, centered with generous margin. One symbol only.
> No text, letters, or numbers. No gradient, no shading, no 3D, no drop shadow, no outline, no scene.

## Prompts — one per work

**Plato**
- `republic` — a city wall rising in three ascending tiers (the three classes of the just city).
- `symposium` — one perfect sphere split cleanly into two equal halves (Aristophanes' divided beings, seeking their other half).
- `phaedo` — a single graceful swan (the philosopher's swan song before death).
- `apology` — a single fly (Socrates, the gadfly of Athens).

**Aristotle**
- `politics` — a walled city gate with an open archway (the polis; man as the political animal).

**The Stoics**
- `discourses` — a simple oil lamp (Epictetus's famous lamp).
- `meditations-ma` — a small square fortress tower (the Stoic inner citadel of the mind).

**Augustine**
- `cityofgod` — a walled city resting on a cloud (the heavenly City of God above the earthly one).

**Aquinas**
- `summa` — a large circular Gothic rose window (the vast ordered structure of the Summa).

**Descartes**
- `discourse` — an open draftsman's compass (method and certainty, geometry as the model).
- `principles` — a single squared cornerstone block (the first principles everything is built on).

**Spinoza**
- `ethics` — a triangle with faint internal construction lines (ethics proved like a geometry theorem).

**Leibniz**
- `monadology` — one perfectly smooth orb with no windows or openings (a single monad).

**Hobbes**
- `leviathan` — a giant crowned figure rising above a row of tiny houses (the Leviathan frontispiece).

**Locke**
- `essay` — a single blank sheet of paper with a quill pen resting on it (the mind as white paper).

**Hume**
- `treatise` — a loose bundle of short sticks tied at the middle (the self as a bundle of perceptions).
- `enquiry` — a two-pronged fork (Hume's Fork: relations of ideas vs matters of fact).

**Kant**
- `cpr` — a pair of round spectacles (the mind's own lenses, which shape everything seen through them).
- `groundwork` — a ring of identical small figures joined hand to hand in a circle (the kingdom of ends).

**Hegel**
- `phenomenology` — a tall straight ladder standing upright (the ladder of consciousness climbing to Absolute Knowing).

**Schopenhauer**
- `wwr` — a parted curtain drawn back from the center (the veil of representation lifted off the blind will).

**Mill**
- `liberty` — a birdcage with its little door swung wide open (individual liberty).
- `utilitarianism` — a rising sun above a row of small identical figures (the greatest happiness of the greatest number).

**Kierkegaard**
- `feartrembling` — a single hand raising a knife on a bare mountaintop (Abraham on Moriah; the leap of faith).

**Marx**
- `manifesto` — a pair of broken chain links snapping apart (the workers with nothing to lose but their chains).
- `capital` — one large industrial cogwheel (the grinding engine of capital).

**Nietzsche**
- `beyondgood` — a single plain theatrical mask (every deep thing wears a mask; beyond the moral surface).
- `genealogy` — a branching root system spreading downward beneath a line (digging for the buried origins of morality).
- `gayscience` — a lantern held up high in broad daylight (the madman with his lantern, seeking the God who is dead).

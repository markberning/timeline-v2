### Q1 — the closing of the Enneads, "flight of the Alone to the Alone" (VI.9.11)
> "Suppose him to fall again from the Vision, he will call up the virtue within him and, seeing
> himself all glorious again, he will take his upward flight once more, through virtue to the Divine
> Mind, through the Wisdom There to the Supreme. And this is the life of the Gods, and of Godlike
> men, a life without love of the world, a flight of the Alone to the Alone."
- **Citation:** Enn. VI.9.11 (the final sentences of the treatise, and of the whole Enneads as Porphyry ordered them).
- **Translation:** Stephen MacKenna, Vol. 1 (1917). US-PD.
- **Verified URL:** https://en.wikisource.org/wiki/Page:Plotinus_-_The_Enneads_Vol._1_-_tr._Stephen_MacKenna_(1917).djvu/172 (verbatim match)

### Q4 — Porphyry on the refused portrait (Life of Plotinus §1)
> "Is it not enough to carry about this image in which nature has enclosed us? Do you really think I
> must also consent to leave, as a desirable spectacle to posterity, an image of the image?"
- Preceding context (also verified, MacKenna): "Plotinus, the philosopher our contemporary, seemed
  ashamed of being in the body... He showed, too, an unconquerable reluctance to sit to a painter or
  a sculptor, and when Amelius persisted in urging him to allow of a portrait being made he asked
  him..." [then Q4]. Porphyry then notes Amelius secretly had the artist **Carterius** attend the
  open conferences and produce a likeness from memory, without Plotinus's knowledge.
- **Citation:** Porphyry, *Life of Plotinus* (*Vita Plotini*) §1.
- **Translation:** MacKenna, Vol. 1 (1917) front matter. US-PD.
- **Verified URL:** https://en.wikisource.org/wiki/Life_of_Plotinus (verbatim match, §1)
- FRAME: this is **Porphyry's testimony**, the source for the refused-portrait story; report it as such.

### Q5 — Porphyry on the four unions (Life of Plotinus §23)
> "...for the Term, the one end, of his life was to become Uniate, to approach to the God over all:
> and four times, during the period I passed with him, he achieved this Term... To this God, I also
> declare, I Porphyry, that in my sixty-eighth year I too was once admitted and entered into Union."
- **Citation:** Porphyry, *Life of Plotinus* §23.
- **Translation:** MacKenna, Vol. 1 (1917). US-PD.
- **Verified URL:** https://en.wikisource.org/wiki/Life_of_Plotinus (verbatim match, §23)
- FRAME: **Porphyry's report** — "four times" is Porphyry's count of unions Plotinus reached while
  Porphyry was with him (Porphyry was with him ~263–268); Porphyry claims one for himself. Frame as
  testimony, not as a flat fact.

### NON-QUOTED / paraphrase-only (no PD English wording pinned, OR a coinage)
- **"emanation"** — the standard scholarly term for the One's overflow; not a translated quote.
  Plotinus's own image is the sun radiating / the spring overflowing (Enn. V.1, V.2). Ship as house
  prose, NOT in quotation marks.
- **"Neoplatonism"** — a modern scholars' label (19th c.); Plotinus called himself a Platonist.
  Always framed as the later coinage (matches platonism.ts ch3).
- **"hypostasis"** — Plotinus's own Greek term for a real level of being; gloss it, do not quote-mark
  an English rendering.
- **VI.9.11 inner phrases** ("Soul restored to Likeness goes to its Like", "It abandons Being to
  become a Beyond-Being") are verified MacKenna wording on the preceding djvu page (/171) if a second
  ascent quote is wanted; otherwise paraphrase.

### Names to gloss on first use (newcomer gate)
- **hypostasis** — a real level/grade of being (not a metaphor; an actual rung reality is built from).
- **the One / the Good** — the single ineffable source, beyond being and beyond thought.
- **Nous (Intellect / Divine Mind)** — the second level; the realm of the Forms thinking themselves.
- **Soul (World-Soul + individual souls)** — the third level; animates the material world.
- **emanation** — reality overflowing DOWN from the One (sun→light), not deliberate creation.
- **matter** — the lowest rung; near-non-being, privation of form/good; whence evil (as a lack).
- **ascent / return** — the soul's climb back UP toward the One; union = the goal.
- **Neoplatonism** — the modern label for this radicalized Platonism.
- **Porphyry** — Plotinus's student, editor, biographer (the source for the man).

## 3. POSITION SUMMARIES — each tied to source (potted-summary trap guarded)

- **The three hypostases** (One → Nous → Soul): the system's spine. One is beyond being/thought
  (Enn. VI.9, V.1); Nous = Being and Thinking identical, the Forms (V.1, V.9); Soul mediates between
  the intelligible and the sensible (IV, V.1). SEP confirms the three-principle structure.
- **Emanation, not creation**: the One does not choose, plan, or will; reality overflows from its
  superabundance the way the sun radiates light without diminishing (Enn. V.1.6, V.2.1). NOT a
  temporal event — the levels depend on one another timelessly. Frame the sun image as Plotinus's own.
- **Matter & evil as privation**: matter is the last, faintest echo of the overflow, near-non-being,
  the total privation of form/good; evil is therefore not a rival substance (the Gnostic/Manichaean
  error) but an *absence* of good (Enn. I.8; II.9 Against the Gnostics). This is the seed Augustine
  later takes (see Augustine read ch3 privation — consistent across pages). NOTE the nuance: Plotinus
  sometimes calls matter itself "the first evil" / "evil in itself," which is a sharper claim than
  Augustine's pure-privation line — flag the difference, don't flatten them into identity.
- **Ascent & union**: the soul climbs back up (purification → turning inward → contemplation of Nous →
  beyond Nous to the One); union is wordless, beyond knowledge (Enn. VI.9). Porphyry reports four
  such unions (§23). Beauty is the first rung of the ladder (Enn. I.6; echoes Plato's *Symposium*).
- **Influence chain (the spine — gated, consistent with other pages):**
  - ANSWERED / inherited from: **Plato** (the two worlds, the Form of the Good "beyond being",
    *Symposium* ladder of beauty, *Timaeus*); Aristotle (Nous thinking itself); the Stoics (sympatheia);
    Ammonius Saccas.
  - ANSWERED BY / shaped: **Porphyry, Iamblichus, Proclus** (the later Neoplatonists);
    **Augustine** (the "books of the Platonists," *Confessions* VII — the privation/immaterial-God
    moves; see Augustine read ch2–3); **Pseudo-Dionysius** (via Proclus) → Christian mysticism;
    the whole later Platonist tradition (platonism.ts is the umbrella).

### Potted-summary / anachronism guards
- Do NOT call the One "God" flat — it is impersonal, not a theistic deity (it does not know, will, or
  love). platonism.ts already flags this; keep consistent.
- "Neoplatonism" is the scholars' name; Plotinus said Platonism.
- Do NOT say Plotinus "influenced Descartes" or read later mind-body framings back in.
- The four unions and the refused portrait are **Porphyry's testimony** — frame, never assert flat.
- Birthplace Lycopolis = Eunapius (later), not Porphyry — frame as "the later tradition."
- Plotinus did not title or order the treatises; *that* was Porphyry. "Enneads" is Porphyry's frame.

## FINDINGS

### Finding 1 — MINOR — "Arian Christians" is not in any fact pack
**Location:** Ch1 block4 — "The Visigoths, Arian Christians themselves, spared those who fled
to them, Christian and pagan alike."
**Problem:** The packs document the Visigoths under Alaric and that they spared those in the
churches (Q-B), but NONE of the three packs states the Visigoths were *Arian* Christians.
Per the WORK rule ("The author writes ONLY from this pack"), this is an out-of-pack claim,
even though it is historically true. It is also load-bearing for Augustine's point (it
explains *why* the barbarians honored the churches), so it should be sourced, not dropped on
a hunch.
**Fix:** Either (a) cut "Arian Christians themselves" to "The Visigoths spared those who fled
to them, Christian and pagan alike," or (b) add the fact to the fact pack with a named source
and a VERIFIED/DOCUMENTED tag, then keep it. Recommend (a) unless the pack is amended — the
sentence reads fine without the editorial detail, and Q-B's force does not depend on it.

### Finding 2 — MINOR — "eight hundred years / since the Gauls" is not in any fact pack
**Location:** hook[1] ("had not fallen to a foreign enemy in eight hundred years") and Ch1
block1 ("no foreign army had taken the city since the Gauls some eight centuries earlier").
**Problem:** The Gallic sack of Rome (390/387 BCE) and the ~800-year gap are real history and
standard context, but they are not in any of the three fact packs. Same WORK-rule issue as
Finding 1: an out-of-pack factual assertion (and a specific number).
**Fix:** Either add to the pack with a named source (the Gallic sack c. 390 BCE → 410 CE is
~800 years; "eight centuries" is right) and tag it, or soften to a pack-safe formulation that
does not assert the specific span, e.g. "Rome had not fallen to a foreign enemy in centuries"
/ "for the first time in living memory and far beyond it." Recommend amending the pack — the
800-year framing is the emotional engine of the hook and is accurate; it just needs a source
line so the WORK read stays pack-disciplined.

### Finding 3 — MINOR — Latin-tag gloss "if I err, I am" vs pack "if I err, I exist"
**Location:** Ch4 block2 — "(The Latin tag is *si fallor, sum*, \"if I err, I am\"...)".
**Problem:** Not a violation — the gloss translates the bare two-word Latin tag and the full
XI.26 English is correctly kept OUT of quotation marks. But for tidy consistency with the
pack's wording (Q-G / augustine-pack §3.J both gloss it "if I err, I exist"), the verb could
match. "sum" = "I am / I exist" — both correct; this is cosmetic only.
**Fix (optional):** change "if I err, I am" → "if I err, I exist" to align with the packs.
No blocker; ship as-is is acceptable.

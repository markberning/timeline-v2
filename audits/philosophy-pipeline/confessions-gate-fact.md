## FINDINGS

### 1 — MINOR — Ch 3, block 1 (line 98): `"a cauldron of unholy loves"` quoted but NOT in the verified-quote ledger
**Location:** Chapter 3 ("Carthage, lust, and nine years a Manichee"), first {p}: *Carthage, he writes, was "a cauldron of unholy loves."*
**Problem:** This is presented as a direct Pusey quotation (`he writes, "…"`), but the phrase appears in the
WORK pack ONLY in its §1 prose summary (Book III, line 124) — where it is itself in quote marks but is
never tagged `VERIFIED` and is absent from both §2 (VERIFIED QUOTATIONS) and the §7 ledger. The brief's
rule is explicit: every quotation-marked passage must be a VERIFIED Pusey quote in the pack with a correct
Book.ch locus. This phrase has no §2 entry, no Book.ch locus, and no confirmed Pusey wording (it is also a
phrase whose common rendering — "cauldron of unholy loves" / "frying-pan / cauldron of shameful loves" —
varies by translation, exactly the Pilkington/Pusey hazard this work is most exposed to). It therefore
fails the quotation-integrity check even though the underlying fact (Carthage as a place of disordered
loves) is sound.
**Fix:** Either (a) demote to paraphrase — drop the quote marks and the "he writes": *Carthage he calls a
cauldron of unholy loves* / *a place of disordered, unholy loves* — or (b) re-fetch the exact Pusey III.1
wording, confirm it, and add it to the pack's §2/§7 with its locus before quoting. Until verified, do not
present it as a quotation. Option (a) is the low-risk fix and changes nothing factual.

*the opening of Genesis, "In the beginning God created the heaven and the earth."* — This is **scripture

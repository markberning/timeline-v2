## Vitals (ch1) — all pack §1
- Born 13 Nov 354, Thagaste (Souk Ahras, Algeria), Numidia. Died 28 Aug 430, Hippo, under Vandal siege.
- Monica (devout Christian, c.331–387, dies at Ostia 387); Patricius (pagan small landowner, baptized deathbed).
- Carthage c.370–71 for rhetoric. Cicero's *Hortensius* c.372–73 = the spark.
- Concubine (UNNAMED in every source — pack §1 forbids supplying a name), ~14–15 yrs, "monogamous union."
- Adeodatus ("given by God"), b. c.372–73, baptized Milan Easter 387, d. c.389–90 aged ~17–18; *De Magistro* (389) dedicated to him.
- Concubine sent away c.385 (Monica-arranged engagement to an heiress); grief quote "my heart, which clave to her, was racked, and wounded, and bleeding" (pack §1, Confessions VI.15); second temporary concubine; engagement then abandoned at conversion.
- Manichee HEARER (auditor) ~9 yrs c.373–382; never "elect." Faustus disillusion (Confessions V) ends it. — pack §1, §6A.
- Rome 383; Milan rhetoric chair 384; Ambrose (c.339–397) + allegorical method. — pack §1.

## The break (brk) — pack §8
- BEFORE: Plotinus (turn inward, ascend to the impersonal One, by intellect) + Manichee (evil = co-equal dark power).
- AFTER: inward turn to a PERSONAL God already within ("You were within, and I without" — Conf X.27 VERIFIED §3.G); the WILL not just intellect is the problem; abstract Neoplatonism repointed at a particular will that cannot save itself.
- "do not go abroad; return within yourself; in the inward man dwells truth" = *noli foras ire* PARAPHRASE (De Vera Religione §39.72) — pack §4A, §10.1; reported speech only, Latin may be cited.

## Ch3 Where evil comes from (PRIVATION — load-bearing)

## Ch4 Time and the inner self
- Conf XI.14 "What, then, is time? If no one ask of me, I know; if I wish to explain to him who asks, I know not." VERIFIED — pack §3.D (New Advent Pusey wording).

- Descartes parallel framed AS a parallel ("the argument Descartes would reach by a different road twelve centuries later"), NOT influence. — pack §4.H, §6.B.

## Ch5 Grace, the will, and Pelagius
- *De Libero Arbitrio* (388–c.395): evil from free will, turning from higher to lower good. — pack §2.4.
- Original sin = heritable biological corruption (diseased-root metaphor — pack uses it §4.C); "developed/gave definitive Western form," NOT "invented" — pack §9.1.
- Enslaved will / will liberated by grace not inherently free — pack §4.C. "mass of perdition" (massa perditionis) PARAPHRASE concept — pack §4.C.
- Pelagius (British monk c.390–418) steelman: God can't command the impossible → free will retained, grace rewards effort/given by merit, sinlessness possible. Julian of Eclanum pushes (no original sin; makes God unjust to infants). — pack §4.D.
- Condemned Council of Carthage 418 + Pope Zosimus. — pack §4.D.
- Augustine's predestination (elect chosen before the world, rest justly damned), he did NOT soften it; hardening vs early De Libero Arbitrio, named; *Retractationes* acknowledges shift. — pack §4.C/D, §5.
- Semipelagian aftermath resolved Second Council of Orange 529 (~100 yrs post-death), broadly in his favor. — pack §4.D.

## Ch6 The two cities and the afterlife
- *City of God* begun 412 completed 426; occasion = sack of Rome by Visigoths 410, pagans blamed Christianity. — pack §2.3.
- CoG XIV.28 "Accordingly, two cities have been formed by two loves: the earthly by the love of self, even to the contempt of God; the heavenly by the love of God, even to the contempt of self. The former, in a word, glories in itself, the latter in the Lord." VERIFIED (Dods) — pack §3.H.
- NOT church-vs-state: two orientations of love, intermixed in history, resolved only at Last Judgment. — pack §2.3, §4.F, §9.4.
- Donatists = rigorist N. African sect (traditor sacraments invalid). Coercion: Luke 14:23 "compel them to come in"; letter to Vincentius "You are of opinion that no one should be compelled to follow righteousness; and yet you read that the householder said to his servants, 'Whomsoever ye shall find, compel them to come in.'" DOCUMENTED — pack §4.E. Dark afterlife (inquisition/forced conversion logic); he argued exile/fines not death; ONE paragraph, no sermon. — pack §4.E, §5.
- Homilies on 1 John VII.8 "Love, and do what you will…" VERIFIED (Browne) — pack §3.I; real line behind the "Love God and do as you please" misquote — pack §9.5.
- Descendants: Anselm (crede ut intelligas / faith seeking understanding, era-2) — pack §6.B; Aquinas (indebted, more optimistic on reason) — pack §6.B; Luther (Augustinian friar, joined Augustinian Hermits 1505, sola gratia/sola fide from anti-Pelagian Augustine) — pack §6.B; Calvin (double predestination = most consistent development; cited Augustine extensively) — pack §6.B; Descartes parallel (structural, not influence) — pack §6.B.

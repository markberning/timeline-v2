## CLARITY LIST (cold-reader: sharp 15-year-old, zero prior knowledge)

Every term the prompt named was checked for an inline gloss on first use.

**CLEAR — glossed on first use (good):**
- Neoplatonism / Platonists / Plotinus — "the great Neoplatonist," *Enneads* named, role explained (Ch2/brk). CLEAR.
- privation — defined three ways with images before the word does any work (Ch3). CLEAR.
- the Manichees — "followers of a third-century Persian prophet named Mani… stark dualism" + the two-powers picture (Ch1). CLEAR.
- concupiscence — **does not appear** in the draft. The fact-pack quote that contains it (the full VIII.7 sentence) is not used; the draft quotes only "Grant me chastity and continency, but not yet." So no undefined "concupiscence" ships. CLEAR (by omission).
- City of God vs City of Man — defined by love, misreading explicitly killed (Ch6). CLEAR.
- dualism, hearer/auditor, concubine, rhetoric, allegorical, distentio, the elect, massa perditionis — all glossed inline. CLEAR.
- Every figure gets a one-line who-they-are: Cicero ("great Roman orator and philosophical popularizer"), Ambrose ("formidable bishop of Milan"), Faustus, Pelagius ("a British monk"), Julian of Eclanum, Anselm, Aquinas, Luther ("literally, an Augustinian friar"), Calvin, Descartes. CLEAR.

**NEEDS-GLOSS (1 item — SHOULD-FIX):**
- **"predestination"** (Ch5) — the *concept* is fully built in the surrounding prose
  (God chooses the elect before the world began, etc.), so a reader is not lost. But the
  bolded term itself is dropped as a one-word payoff (`finally up to God's free choice…
  — **predestination**`) without a from-the-word gloss. The idea is clear; the *label*
  arrives slightly ahead of an explicit "predestination = …" beat. Minor. The mechanism
  is explained, so this is NEEDS-GLOSS not LOST.

**No LOST items. No silently-skipped argument steps** — the privation argument is walked
in five numbered moves; the grace→predestination chain is walked link by link
("if the will cannot save itself and grace is what saves, and grace is given to some and
not others, then…"). Nothing reads as mysticism.

**Influence-chain integrity (special duty): PASS.** No silent missing upstream links.
Where the read leans on Neoplatonism/Plotinus it gives the reader Plotinus first (Ch2,
brk) before using him. Manichaeism is built before privation refutes it. Forward refs
(Aquinas/Luther/Calvin "built on Augustine") are correctly forward and fine. The
Descartes parallel is explicitly *not* claimed as a debt — matches the scholarship
(Descartes acknowledged the resemblance to Colvius/Arnauld, claimed independent
discovery).

**On "illumination":** the prompt lists it as a major idea to three-part-test. The draft
does NOT present Augustine's divine-illumination epistemology (the mind sees eternal
truths by a light from God, the *De Magistro* "inner teacher" doctrine). The fact-pack
touches *De Magistro* only as a paternal monument (§2.6) and does not build illumination
as a worked idea. Given the draft is a "walk the whole system" read and already runs six
chapters, this is a **judgment call**: I rate it **SHOULD-CONSIDER, not MUST-ADD.** The
inward-turn throughline already carries the epistemic payload ("the soul is where the
image of God is stamped… going deeper you get closer to truth"); illumination is the
technical name for that mechanism and a purist would want one paragraph on it (ideally in
Ch4 beside time/the self, or as a beat in the brk). But its absence creates **no silent
gap** — nothing downstream leans on an un-introduced illumination doctrine. Flagging for
the author's call rather than blocking.

## VOICE-RESTRAINT LIST (locked corpus rule — reject-and-rewrite class)

**This is the blocking issue.** The draft talks AT the reader continuously. Below is the
catalog by category. All are MUST-FIX under the locked rule; they are mechanical to strip
and do not alter a single argument.

### Second-person imperatives / reader-commands ("Watch," "Picture," "Read that again," "Feel," "Notice," "Hold that")
- Hook: "Start with the young man…" (imperative open)
- Brk: "Steelman what came before…"; "Read it against Plato and **feel** the floor turn over."; "Now **watch** him find them."
- Ch1: "**Hold** the shape of that…"; "**Do not let** anyone supply one…"; "**Read that sequence at full strength** before you let any halo near it."; "**Hold** him; he matters…"; "And you must **understand** *why* it gripped him…"
- Ch2: "**Read it slowly**, because it is usually quoted as a wink…"; "**Treat that scene with care** in both directions."; "The first thing we should **watch** him do…"
- Ch3: "**Walk it move by move**… skim it and it will sound like a word trick"; "**Recall** the trap."; "Here is the move, in five steps, **slow**."; "Now **feel** what that one move buys him…"; "**Go back** to the killer question… and **watch** it dissolve."; "**Keep this chapter in your pocket**…"; "**Get** privation and you have the key…"; "put it **at full force**."
- Ch4: "**Start with** that epigraph…"; "**Make it concrete** with his own example…"; "Now **go down** one more level…"; "If that rings a bell, **it should**…"; "**Stand back and see** what this chapter has done."
- Ch5: "**Watch** the hardening happen, and **watch** him know it's happening."; "**Begin with** the early position…"; "**Hold onto it**…"; "state it plainly"; "**Follow that to its end**… and you should **see** the whole of it."
- Ch6: "you must **read it carefully**…"; "Now **kill the misreading**…"; "named plainly and then **left**…"; "**Set against that**…"

### Meta-narration / throat-clearing ("here is the part people miss")
- Ch1: "Now the part the pious editions used to skip, and that the honest story cannot."; "Because here is **how it was broken up**, and it is the central wart of the early life."
- Ch2: "and it is the **intellectual hinge of his whole life**"
- Ch3: "**This is the chapter to slow down for**, because it contains the single idea you most need to actually *get*…"; "Now feel what that one move buys him, because **this is why it is load-bearing and not just clever**."; "**And here is the move that earns him real respect**…"; "But **notice what a real philosopher does**…"
- Ch5: "**This is the chapter where many readers part company** with Augustine…"; "And **here is the wart that is *his own***…"
- Ch6: "**And now the darkest wart**…"; "Because **here is the size of him**, stated at the right scope…"

### Self-labeled asides ("the dinner-table version," "a small honesty note")
- Ch3: "**The plain-language version, to carry to dinner**:" — the self-labeled aside, banned form.
- Ch3: "(Plato did exactly this to his own Forms; the move is the mark.)" — self-referential craft aside.
- Ch6: "(Say it precisely — the *Latin* West…)" — aside addressed at the act of saying.

### Vague gestures / "(the ambiguity is his, and worth keeping)"-type editorializing-at-reader
- Ch2: "(the ambiguity is his, and worth keeping)"
- Ch1: "the oldest knife in religion" / "the oldest question in the world" — atmospheric gesture (borderline; flag, lower priority)

**BC/AD-arithmetic condescension:** none found. (The "twelve hundred years later / twelve
centuries on" Descartes framing is substantive chronology, not condescension — keep it.)

**Severity note:** Ch3 and Ch4 — the two strongest chapters on substance — are also the
most voice-saturated. The fix is to convert imperatives to declaratives (e.g. "Read that
sequence at full strength" → the sequence, stated; "Now feel what that one move buys him"
→ "That one move buys him this:"; "The plain-language version, to carry to dinner:" →
"Put plainly:"). The content survives the strip intact.

## SUMMARY

On substance this is among the strongest philosophy writing in the corpus: every major
idea passes the worked-example / steelman-first / plain-restatement test, with the
privation chapter (Ch3) as model work — full-strength build, sharpest objection handed to
the reader, dinner-table restatement. Coverage is complete and the influence chain has no
silent gaps. **The one blocking problem is voice:** the draft commands the reader
constantly ("Watch," "Feel," "Hold that," "Read that again," "the plain-language version
to carry to dinner," "here is the part people miss") — the exact reject-and-rewrite class
under the locked corpus rule, concentrated heaviest in the two best chapters. The fix is a
mechanical imperative-to-declarative sweep that leaves every argument intact. Verdict:
**FIX-THEN-SHIP** — strip the voice, optionally gloss "predestination" and add one
illumination paragraph, and it ships.

# Fact ledger — "German Idealism" SCHOOL read (kind = SCHOOL, one tradition's story)

The author's audit trail for the German Idealism school-level "the whole tradition as a
connected arc" read, the analog of the shipped Platonism and Rationalism school reads.
Export const = `IDEALISM`. Written primarily from the verified era-4 fact pack
(`audits/philosophy-pipeline/kant-germans-fact-pack.md`, built + verified 2026-06-13) and
the era-5 fact pack (`audits/philosophy-pipeline/nineteenth-century-fact-pack.md`) for the
afterlife/handoff (Feuerbach, Marx, Kierkegaard). Two quotes that the era-4 pack flagged as
SOFT SPOTS (the Fichte first-principle wording; the Hegel Baillie "cows" line) were resolved
by fetching the PD source directly on **2026-06-15** and matching word-for-word; see below.
No other new web research; every other quote is lifted from a `VERIFIED` pack entry with its
translation and sourceUrl carried over. Where a pack tags a claim `PARAPHRASE-ONLY`, the read
paraphrases and never sets it in quotation marks.

**Scope of THIS read vs the era read.** The era-4 read ("Kant and the Germans") tells the
whole sweep 1781–1851 across Kant + Hegel + Schopenhauer at era altitude, with Fichte and
Schelling named as bridge-walkers. THIS school read is the *tradition's own story*: German
Idealism as one connected relay (Kant's stuck question → Fichte → Schelling → Hegel → the
afterlife), narrated as the handoffs BETWEEN minds, NOT a re-walk of each thinker's full
system (those belong to the thinker reads). Differentiation from the era read and from the
thinker reads is the nesting-gate requirement: the era read is the survey, the thinker reads
are the deep system-walks, and this is the family relay.

## Landmines + traps honored (era-4 pack §2, §9; era-5 pack)
1. **"Idealism" = "having ideals"** — DEFINED explicitly on first use (Ch1 + the break block):
   reality as mind-structured, root *idea* not *ideal*; not a claim the world is perfect (pack
   §2 clarity landmine 2, §9 trap 11). Every newcomer term glossed: *a priori* (implicitly via
   Kant's "à priori" in the quote + context), *transcendental* vs *transcendent* (Ch1, pack
   landmine 1), *categories*, *phenomena*, *thing-in-itself / noumenon*, *Geist*, *Aufhebung*,
   *the Absolute*, *posit*, *determinate negation*, *recognition*.
2. **"Thesis–antithesis–synthesis" as Hegel's** — staged AS the Chalybäus misattribution (Ch4):
   terms are Fichte's (thetic/antithetic/synthetic); pinned onto Hegel by Heinrich Moritz
   Chalybäus in an 1837 textbook; debunked by Gustav Mueller 1958 ("The Hegel Legend of
   Thesis-Antithesis-Synthesis"). Then the REAL motor given: *Aufhebung* (cancel + preserve +
   lift up) and *determinate negation*, with the Being→Nothing→Becoming opening triad as the
   worked case (era-4 pack §9 trap 1, §11.5). Marx as a propagator of the slogan named (pack
   §2/§9).
3. **Kant's race-hierarchy writings** — named honestly at school altitude (Ch4: "teleological
   and Eurocentric ... casts the non-European world as an earlier, lower stage, a serious
   limitation"). This is applied to HEGEL's history-schema (the school-relevant surface, pack
   §2 framing trap) rather than a Kant-life lecture; the brief says include race-hierarchy only
   where it fits the altitude, and a school relay read's natural surface is the Eurocentric
   teleology of the Hegelian philosophy of history. Kant's own *Observations* (1764)
   race-hierarchy material + the Kleingeld debate (era-4 pack §11.8) is NOT separately
   lectured, consistent with the brief's "only where it fits the altitude."
4. **"Hegel justified the Prussian state / whatever is, is right"** — read via *wirklich*;
   conservative-reading worry acknowledged, not validated (pack §9 trap 4).
5. **"Standing Hegel on his head" as a Marx quote** — avoided; verified "turned right side up"
   used instead (era-5 pack §321).
6. **"Nietzsche was Schopenhauer's student"** — avoided; framed as Nietzsche read Schopenhauer
   at 21 and was overwhelmed (intellectual influence, not student-teacher; era-4 pack §9 trap 7,
   §6 item 7).
7. **"Marx as prophet of 20th-c state communism"** — NOT asserted; Ch5 keeps Marx as
   philosopher (the inversion of Hegel + Thesis XI), the political afterlife noted once in Ch6
   as "shaped a century of history, for better and for catastrophic worse," not as Marx's own
   doctrine (era-5 pack §372 big trap).
8. **Schopenhauer's pessimism as a metaphysical position, not a mood** — Ch6 calls it a
   doctrine (Will = blind striving = inner nature of all), not a personal complaint (era-4 pack
   §2 trap).

## Lineage relay honored (the spine of this read)
Kant (thing-in-itself wall) → Fichte (drop it; self-positing I + not-I + recognition) →
Schelling (one Absolute, nature = visible spirit; break with Hegel over the "cows" intuition)
→ Hegel (Geist developing through history; Aufhebung; master/slave; real-is-rational; owl of
Minerva). Afterlife: Hegel → Right vs Left/Young Hegelians → Feuerbach (projection inversion)
→ Marx (turned right side up; Thesis XI) and Kierkegaard (the individual vs the System).
Counter-current: Schopenhauer (Will = thing-in-itself; contempt for Hegel) → Nietzsche.
Later: British Idealism (Green, Bradley) → analytic backlash (Moore, Russell). All chains are
era-4/era-5 pack §6 influence-skeleton items except the British-Idealism/analytic tail
([VERIFY], textbook-standard).

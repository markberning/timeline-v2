## Framing / legend-handling audit (pipeline traps)
- **Apocryphal "I know that I know nothing"** — explicitly debunked in ch3, never placed in quotes attributed to Socrates. (§3/Q2)
- **Diogenes Laertius material** — the "sculptor father / young Socrates carving statues" and the Xanthippe water-dousing are framed as later legend ("traced to the biographer Diogenes Laertius six centuries on"). (§5 LATE-LEGEND)
- **Cicero "called philosophy down from the heavens"** — framed as a later (3-centuries-on) summary, not a Socrates quote. (§6)
- **Sophists steelmanned** — stated at full strength ("serious, learned, formidable thinkers, not charlatans") before the corrosive idea is named. (§8 / framing)
- **Anachronism on the trial** — explicitly refused ("the modern reflex… is an anachronism"); both layers (real charges + political subtext under the amnesty) held visible. (§6)
- **Fidelity seam** — middle-dialogue material (immortal soul in *Phaedo*, tripartite soul) is attributed to Plato, not asserted of the historical Socrates. (§0 rule)
- **Aristotle *Metaphysics* 987b1** — used as paraphrase only (inductive arguments + universal definitions), no quote in quote-marks. (§4A `PARAPHRASE-ONLY`)
- **Authored comparisons flagged** — the doctor/skill analogy (ch4) and the gadfly/horse gloss are author illustrations built on documented positions, not attributed quotations.

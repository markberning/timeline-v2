## GLOBAL RULES FOR ALL GORYEO KOREA PROMPTS — read before drawing any map in this file

**CRITICAL RULES — read carefully before drawing:**

1. **Label cleanly.** Spell every label correctly. A long river MAY be labeled more than once along its course (normal cartography) as long as each instance is correct and the repeats are well separated — not touching, stacked, or adjacent. Do NOT duplicate a city/site/region label, do NOT place two copies of a label next to each other, and do NOT repeat an annotation/caption.
2. **Spell every label exactly as written in this prompt.** Do not invent words. Do not abbreviate. Do not generate any word that is not in the prompt text.
3. **Include the exact site name on every label.** The site name is the word or phrase before the first parenthesis or em-dash. Never drop a site name.
4. **North is at the top of the map. East is to the right.** Place every location according to its real-world geography in this orientation. Do not rotate or tilt the map.
5. **All water is light blue.** Seas, bays, lakes, and rivers are rendered in light blue. Do not render water as tan, cream, or white. Land is beige/tan. River floodplains are soft green.
6. **Do not invent any labels, annotations, sites, or features that are not in this prompt.** If it is not in the prompt, do not draw it.
7. **Geography correct & readable.** Place features in their correct real-world locations. Faint modern country/region outlines or labels as orientation aids are *welcome*. Real geography defects = invented/misspelled place names, wrong locations, or labels contradicting the prompt — NOT the presence of reference outlines.
8. **No compass words as labels.** Never print the words North, South, East, West (or the letters N/S/E/W) anywhere on the image.
9. **No ornamental glyphs.** No decorative star, four-point sparkle, twinkle, compass-rose flourish, or corner ornament.

---

The chapter title "Chapter N: <Title>" should appear at the top of the map image, centered, in a clean serif font, inside a header bar that spans the full width of the image. The header bar is the ONLY bordered element of the image. Draw NO border, outline, frame, rectangle, or hairline anywhere else — the map and its water must bleed off all four outer edges. Never enclose the map in a rectangle. Render the chapter title **exactly once**, only inside that single full-width header bar.

**GORYEO KOREA ORIENTATION RULES:**

1. North is at the top. East is to the right.
2. The Korean Peninsula is a roughly rectangular north-south landmass jutting south from the Asian continent, with the Yellow Sea on the WEST, the East Sea (Sea of Japan) on the EAST, and the Korea Strait at the SOUTH separating Korea from the Japanese island of Kyushu.
3. The Yalu River and Tumen River form the NORTHERN border. The Yalu flows southwest into the Yellow Sea (the northwestern frontier toward Manchuria); the Tumen flows northeast into the East Sea.
4. Gaegyeong (modern Kaesong), the Goryeo capital, is in the CENTER-WEST of the peninsula near the Yellow Sea coast, just north of the Han River. Pyongyang (the Western Capital) is to the NORTHWEST on the Taedong River. Gyeongju is in the SOUTHEAST (the old Silla capital). Naju is in the far SOUTHWEST. Andong is in the SOUTHEAST-CENTER interior.
5. Ganghwa Island sits just WEST of Gaegyeong at the mouth of the Han River where it meets the Yellow Sea. Jindo is a small island off the SOUTHWEST tip of the peninsula. Jeju Island is the large island roughly 60 miles SOUTH of the southern Korean coast.
6. The northern neighbor is era-specific and is given per chapter (the Khitan Liao empire, then the Jurchen Jin empire, then the Mongol Yuan empire, then Ming China) — always across the Yalu to the NORTH and NORTHWEST. China proper is to the WEST across the Yellow Sea. Japan is to the EAST across the East Sea and SOUTHEAST (Kyushu) across the Korea Strait.

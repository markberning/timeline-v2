# War map prompts — Fort Stedman (modern battle-map house style)

These are **battle maps** drawn in a **clean modern textbook style** — the Hal
Jespersen / National Park Service look: legible on a phone, beside prose, with
real terrain, troop blocks, and bold arrows. Generated by
`scripts/generate-war-maps.mjs` (parses `## Map <slug>` sections, writes
`public/war-img/<slug>.png`). House style chosen 2026-05-22 (replaced the old
flat-tan schematic). Label style = the locked no-box rule (canonical preamble
copied from `wilsons-creek.md` rule 4, 2026-06-01).

The opener for this battle already carries a `{locator}` establishing map, so
these sections are TACTICAL ONLY (no campaign/operational/locator map here).

## Style — every battle map MUST obey these

1. **CLEAN MODERN CARTOGRAPHY** — pale parchment-white background (#f3eee2). Flat even fills only — NO paper texture, NO relief shading, NO 3-D, NO drop shadows, NO glow, NO noise.
2. **Terrain = simple flat shapes in muted color**, only what the caption names: woods = soft sage-green; a hill/ridge/plateau = pale tan; a mountain range = a pale-tan band; water (river/creek/sea) = a flat light-blue band or fill; a road/trail = a thin gray line; a town, city, fort, church, house = a small gray block or dot.
3. **Forces use side colors.** Union (North) = BLUE (#1d4ed8). Confederate (South) = RUST RED (#b44d3b). A force/line = crisp solid rectangular unit blocks; a movement = a bold smooth tapered arrow. No other strong colors. BLUE/RUST/RED name the INK, never text. Never render a color name as a label.
4. **LABELS MUST BE LARGE, BOLD, AND EASY TO READ ON A PHONE** — this is critical. Use a HEAVY modern sans-serif at a GENEROUS size (think a clean textbook map seen on a small screen) in DARK, near-black ink, placed DIRECTLY ON THE MAP. Give each label only a thin soft cream halo hugging the letters (a narrow outline the colour of the pale background) for separation — do NOT draw a filled white box, pill, sticker, or rounded rectangle behind the text. Prefer FEWER, BIGGER labels over many small ones. NO label may be small, thin, faint, or gray. Use SOLID, FILLED glyphs — NEVER outlined, hollow, or stroked-only letterforms; hollow/outlined text is illegible and is the exact failure to avoid. Position each label over open, pale ground (not on top of arrows, water, or dark fills) so it reads cleanly without a box. At most SIX labels per map (more only if the map's own section explicitly lists them). Bare proper names only, spelled EXACTLY as written, upright (not italic or rotated); add NO unlisted label and print NO color words.
5. **North is at the TOP, east to the RIGHT.** A defending line FACES the attack.
6. **NO title bar, NO caption text, NO border/frame, NO compass rose, NO scale bar, NO legend, NO N/S/E/W letters, NO ornament.** The map bleeds off all four edges.

## Map fort-stedman-breakthrough

About 4:15 a.m., March 25, 1865 — Gordon's pre-dawn rush takes Fort Stedman and the batteries beside it. A zoomed-in tactical view, NOT the whole siege.

Terrain and works:
- Draw TWO parallel siege lines running north–south down the map, very close together (the prose: only about 150 yards apart). The Confederate trench line is on the LEFT (west); the Union trench line is on the RIGHT (east). Render each as a thin gray earthwork line.
- On the Union (right) line, place a small gray fort block at the CENTER labeled "Fort Stedman".
- Just NORTH (above) Fort Stedman on the Union line, place a small gray battery block labeled "Battery X".
- Just SOUTH (below) Fort Stedman on the Union line, place a small gray battery block labeled "Batteries XI and XII".
- Below those, still on the Union line toward the lower edge, place a small gray fort block labeled "Fort Haskell".

The Confederates attack from the WEST, facing EAST: draw a row of solid rust-red unit blocks along the Confederate (left) trench line. Show the assault as ONE bold rust-red tapered arrow, labeled "Gordon", driving EAST across the narrow no-man's-land and striking into Fort Stedman, with two short rust-red arrows splitting off from it — one north into Battery X and one south into Batteries XI and XII — to show the captured ground spreading along the Union line. The arrow clearly crosses from the Confederate line into the Union fort.

The Union holds on the flank: at Fort Haskell, draw a few solid blue unit blocks facing NORTH up the trench, blunting the southward spread (the garrison that did not give way).

Six labels only: Fort Stedman, Battery X, Batteries XI and XII, Fort Haskell, Gordon, City Point. Place "City Point" near the upper-RIGHT edge as the distant objective (a small gray dot at the edge with a short rust-red arrow stub pointing off the map toward it from the lodgement). Nothing else.

## Map fort-stedman-ring

Mid-morning, around 7:45 a.m., March 25, 1865 — Parke and Hartranft wall off the lodgement in a ring of guns. A zoomed-in tactical view, NOT the whole siege.

Terrain and works:
- Same setting: the Union trench line runs north–south on the RIGHT, the Confederate trench line on the LEFT.
- At the center of the Union line place the captured cluster as small gray blocks: "Fort Stedman" in the middle, "Battery X" just north of it, "Batteries XI and XII" just south of it. Inside and around that cluster draw a packed mass of solid rust-red unit blocks (the Confederate penetration, jammed in place).
- Behind (east of) the captured forts, draw a pale-tan ridge band running north–south, with a thin gray rail line in front of it (leave the rail unlabeled).

The Union seals the breach: draw a long curving wall of solid blue unit blocks wrapping the rust-red mass on THREE sides — north, east (along the ridge), and south — a tightening semicircle. Place a row of blue artillery blocks ON the pale-tan ridge behind the forts. Show fire converging inward with three short bold blue arrows striking the rust-red lodgement from the north, the east, and the south (the crossfire from three sides).

Label the Union effort with TWO commander labels: "Parke" near the upper-right (directing the seal) and "Hartranft" near the right-center behind the ridge (the division building the ring).

Six labels only: Fort Stedman, Battery X, Batteries XI and XII, Parke, Hartranft, Fort Haskell. Keep "Fort Haskell" as the small gray fort block holding at the lower end of the Union line. Nothing else.

## Map fort-stedman-recapture

A little before 8:00 a.m., March 25, 1865 — Hartranft's assault retakes the forts and the Confederates run back across the open ground. A zoomed-in tactical view, NOT the whole siege.

Terrain and works:
- Same orientation: Confederate trench line on the LEFT (west), Union trench line on the RIGHT (east), with the narrow no-man's-land between them.
- On the Union line at center, the recaptured cluster as small gray blocks: "Fort Stedman" in the middle, "Battery X" north, "Batteries XI and XII" south.

The Union counterassault: draw the curving wall of solid blue unit blocks from the north, east, and south, and show ONE bold blue tapered arrow, labeled "Hartranft", driving WEST into Fort Stedman and the batteries (the general assault that retook them). Add two short blue arrows pressing in from north and south to show the ring closing.

The Confederate retreat: draw ONE bold rust-red tapered arrow running EAST-to-WEST, from the captured forts back across the open no-man's-land to the Confederate trench line, labeled "Gordon" (the survivors he pulled back). Show it crossing the open ground under fire.

Five labels only: Fort Stedman, Battery X, Batteries XI and XII, Hartranft, Gordon. Nothing else.

# War map prompts — Perryville (modern battle-map house style)

Battle maps in the clean modern textbook style (Hal Jespersen / NPS look). Generated by
`scripts/generate-war-maps.mjs` (parses `## Map <slug>` sections -> `public/war-img/<slug>.png`).

## Style — every battle map MUST obey these

1. **CLEAN MODERN CARTOGRAPHY** — pale parchment-white background (#f3eee2). Flat even fills only — NO paper texture, NO relief shading, NO 3-D, NO drop shadows, NO glow, NO noise.
2. **Terrain = simple flat shapes in muted color**, only what the caption names: woods = soft sage-green; a hill/ridge/plateau = pale tan; a mountain range = a pale-tan band; water (river/creek/sea) = a flat light-blue band or fill; a road/trail = a thin gray line; a town, city, fort, church, house = a small gray block or dot.
3. **Forces use side colors.** Union (North) = BLUE (#1d4ed8). Confederate (South) = RUST RED (#b44d3b). A force/line = crisp solid rectangular unit blocks; a movement = a bold smooth tapered arrow. No other strong colors. BLUE/RUST/RED name the INK, never text. Never render a color name as a label.
4. **LABELS MUST BE LARGE, BOLD, AND EASY TO READ ON A PHONE** — this is critical. Use a HEAVY modern sans-serif at a GENEROUS size (think a clean textbook map seen on a small screen), DARK ink, each label sitting on a crisp solid white pill/halo so it stays sharp against the map. Prefer FEWER, BIGGER labels over many small ones. NO label may be small, thin, faint, or hard to read. Each label must read like a printed STICKER — BOLD near-black text inside a SOLID WHITE rounded box — NEVER thin, gray, or faint text floating directly on the terrain (that is the failure to avoid). Use SOLID, FILLED glyphs — NEVER outlined, hollow, or stroked-only letterforms; OUTLINED text is illegible and is the exact failure to avoid. At most SIX labels per map. Bare proper names only, spelled EXACTLY as written; add NO unlisted label and print NO color words.
5. **North is at the TOP, east to the RIGHT.** A defending line FACES the attack.
6. **NO title bar, NO caption text, NO border/frame, NO compass rose, NO scale bar, NO legend, NO N/S/E/W letters, NO ornament.** The map bleeds off all four edges.

## Map perryville-overview

Early October 1862 — Bragg's invasion converges on Perryville in a drought: Buell's feint toward Frankfort splits the Confederate army northeast while the rest of both armies grope toward the shrinking water west of town. A campaign-scale approach map, north up.

Draw a small gray town block near the center labeled "Perryville". Just west of it draw a thin light-blue creek running roughly north-to-south labeled "Doctor's Creek", and a second light-blue stream curling along the western/northwestern side of the town labeled "Chaplin River" — both drawn thin and broken to read as drought-shrunk water. In the upper-right (northeast) corner place a small gray city block labeled "Frankfort". Draw a bold tapered BLUE arrow climbing from the lower-center up toward Frankfort in the northeast — Buell's feint that drew the Confederates north — and a separate cluster of BLUE unit blocks swinging west toward Perryville from the lower-left/south. Draw a bold tapered RUST-RED arrow pulling north-northeast away from Perryville toward Frankfort (the bulk of Bragg's army chasing the feint), and a smaller cluster of RUST-RED unit blocks holding at Perryville along the creek. Scatter a few pale-tan rolling hills (the Chaplin Hills) between Perryville and Frankfort to show the broken country.

Six labels only: Perryville, Doctor's Creek, Chaplin River, Frankfort, Buell, Bragg.

## Map perryville

October 8, 1862 — the Confederate en-echelon attack rolls south out of the north and northeast, crumpling the Union left back from Open Knob, while most of Buell's army sits idle to the southwest in the acoustic shadow. A tactical battle map, north up.

Draw a thin light-blue stream down the LEFT and lower edge labeled "Doctor's Creek"; draw a second thin light-blue stream curling across the TOP-LEFT (northwest) labeled "Chaplin River". In the upper-center-left place a pale-tan hill labeled "Open Knob" — this is the Union far LEFT (north). Across it and trailing south draw a row of BLUE unit blocks (the embattled Union left). Draw a thin gray road slanting from the town off toward the southwest labeled "Springfield Pike". In the upper-center, where two thin gray roads meet just north of town, mark the junction "Dixville Crossroads". From the NORTH and NORTHEAST send three bold tapered RUST-RED arrows striking one after another down the line (the en-echelon attack), the heaviest tip slamming into Open Knob and bending the blue blocks there backward to the south. To the SOUTHWEST, well away from the fighting, draw a large idle cluster of BLUE unit blocks sitting still (Buell's unused army in the acoustic shadow). Add a small gray town block at lower-center for Perryville itself if room remains, unlabeled.

Six labels only: Open Knob, Dixville Crossroads, Springfield Pike, Doctor's Creek, Chaplin River, Buell.

# War map prompts — Gaines' Mill (modern battle-map house style)

Battle maps in the clean modern textbook style (Hal Jespersen / NPS look). Generated by
`scripts/generate-war-maps.mjs` (parses `## Map <slug>` sections -> `public/war-img/<slug>.png`).

## Style — every battle map MUST obey these

1. **CLEAN MODERN CARTOGRAPHY** — pale parchment-white background (#f3eee2). Flat even fills only — NO paper texture, NO relief shading, NO 3-D, NO drop shadows, NO glow, NO noise.
2. **Terrain = simple flat shapes in muted color**, only what the caption names: woods = soft sage-green; a hill/ridge/plateau = pale tan; a mountain range = a pale-tan band; water (river/creek/sea) = a flat light-blue band or fill; a road/trail = a thin gray line; a town, city, fort, church, house = a small gray block or dot.
3. **Forces use side colors.** Union (North) = BLUE (#1d4ed8). Confederate (South) = RUST RED (#b44d3b). A force/line = crisp solid rectangular unit blocks; a movement = a bold smooth tapered arrow. No other strong colors. BLUE/RUST/RED name the INK, never text. Never render a color name as a label.
4. **LABELS MUST BE LARGE, BOLD, AND EASY TO READ ON A PHONE** — this is critical. Use a HEAVY modern sans-serif at a GENEROUS size (think a clean textbook map seen on a small screen), DARK ink, each label sitting on a crisp solid white pill/halo so it stays sharp against the map. Prefer FEWER, BIGGER labels over many small ones. NO label may be small, thin, faint, or hard to read. Each label must read like a printed STICKER — BOLD near-black text inside a SOLID WHITE rounded box — NEVER thin, gray, or faint text floating directly on the terrain (that is the failure to avoid). Use SOLID, FILLED glyphs — NEVER outlined, hollow, or stroked-only letterforms; OUTLINED text is illegible and is the exact failure to avoid. At most SIX labels per map. Bare proper names only, spelled EXACTLY as written; add NO unlisted label and print NO color words.
5. **North is at the TOP, east to the RIGHT.** A defending line FACES the attack.
6. **NO title bar, NO caption text, NO border/frame, NO compass rose, NO scale bar, NO legend, NO N/S/E/W letters, NO ornament.** The map bleeds off all four edges.

---

**LABEL PLACEMENT — applies to EVERY label on EVERY map (critical):** Each label, together with its box/halo, must sit FULLY INSIDE the frame with a clear margin on all four sides. NO label may touch, be clipped by, or run off any edge — pull every edge-hugging name inward toward open ground. No two labels may overlap or touch each other. When a label cannot sit in clear open space right beside the place or arrow it names without colliding with another label, an arrow, troop blocks, or the edge, move it to the nearest open area and draw a THIN GRAY LEADER LINE from the label to its dot/marker so the link stays obvious. River and water labels must be SOLID and STRONG (a deep, readable blue), never pale, faint, or washed-out; if blue-on-water would be low-contrast, set the river name on open ground beside the water with a halo instead.

## Map gaines-mill-sevendays

June 26–27, 1862 — Lee's plan: crush Porter's isolated corps north of the Chickahominy before McClellan realizes the Confederate force south of the river is a bluff. A strategic overview, north up.

Place a gray city block in the lower-LEFT corner labeled "Richmond". Draw the Chickahominy River as a flat light-blue band running roughly west-to-east across the lower third of the frame, labeled "Chickahominy R.". South of the river, draw blue unit blocks (McClellan's main army) clustered near Richmond — do NOT label them. North of the river, place a small gray village dot in the upper-LEFT area labeled "Mechanicsville" (where Lee opened the Seven Days on June 26). Further northeast, draw an isolated cluster of solid BLUE blocks labeled "Porter" — this is the exposed V Corps on the north bank. Draw a thin gray line from Mechanicsville northeast to Porter's position to show the road axis. Draw two bold rust-red converging arrows aimed at "Porter": one sweeping from the west (from the direction of Richmond, crossing the river) and one curling down from the north (Jackson's approach from the Shenandoah); label the combined force with a single rust-red block west of Porter labeled "Lee". Add a thin gray line south of Porter running to the Chickahominy labeled "Grapevine Bridge" — Porter's only escape route back across the river.

Six labels only: Richmond, Chickahominy R., Mechanicsville, Porter, Lee, Grapevine Bridge.


**LABEL PLACEMENT (text-readability sweep 2026-06-01):** Every label, with its box/halo, must sit FULLY INSIDE the frame with a clear margin on all four sides — none may touch, be clipped by, or run off any edge. Render "Chickahominy R." in SOLID strong blue (not pale or faint), set beside the water on open pale ground with a halo so it reads cleanly. No two labels may overlap or touch.
## Map gaines-mill

June 27, 1862 — The battle: Porter's three-tier defense behind Boatswain's Swamp holds all afternoon until Hood's Texas Brigade punches through at dusk. A tactical map, north up.

Draw the Chickahominy River as a flat light-blue band across the BOTTOM edge of the frame, labeled "Chickahominy R." — do not draw bridges. Above it, fill the center-right with a soft pale-tan plateau. On the NORTH face of the plateau draw a curving sage-green boggy ravine sweeping in a long semicircle (concave side facing north/west), labeled "Boatswain's Swamp". Place a small gray house block on the plateau behind (south of) the swamp labeled "Watt House". Place a small gray crossroads dot at the TOP-LEFT of the frame labeled "Old Cold Harbor" — this is the northern crossroads where Confederate forces arrived expecting Porter's open flank and found a solid line instead; draw a pale-tan low rise south of it to suggest Turkey Hill on Porter's far-left. Add a small gray mill dot on the north edge of the swamp labeled "Gaines' Mill".

The Union (Porter) holds the plateau: a curved arc of solid BLUE blocks along the swamp's south rim, facing north/west. The Confederates assault from the north and west: rust-red blocks with three lighter rust-red arrows crossing the swamp toward the arc (the piecemeal afternoon assaults — D.H. Hill from Old Cold Harbor, A.P. Hill and Ewell from the west), all stopped at the arc. Then draw ONE heavy rust-red arrow punching through the arc at its center-right, labeled "Hood" — the dusk breakthrough. Show a BLUE withdrawal arrow pointing south from the plateau toward the Chickahominy.

Six labels only: Chickahominy R., Boatswain's Swamp, Watt House, Old Cold Harbor, Gaines' Mill, Hood.

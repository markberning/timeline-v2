# War map prompts — Missionary Ridge (modern battle-map house style)

Battle maps in the clean modern textbook style (Hal Jespersen / NPS look). Generated by
`scripts/generate-war-maps.mjs` (parses `## Map <slug>` sections -> `public/war-img/<slug>.png`).

## Style — every battle map MUST obey these

1. **CLEAN MODERN CARTOGRAPHY** — pale parchment-white background (#f3eee2). Flat even fills only — NO paper texture, NO relief shading, NO 3-D, NO drop shadows, NO glow, NO noise.
2. **Terrain = simple flat shapes in muted color**, only what the caption names: woods = soft sage-green; a hill/ridge/plateau = pale tan; a mountain range = a pale-tan band; water (river/creek/sea) = a flat light-blue band or fill; a road/trail = a thin gray line; a town, city, fort, church, house = a small gray block or dot.
3. **Forces use side colors.** Union (North) = BLUE (#1d4ed8). Confederate (South) = RUST RED (#b44d3b). A force/line = crisp solid rectangular unit blocks; a movement = a bold smooth tapered arrow. No other strong colors. BLUE/RUST/RED name the INK, never text. Never render a color name as a label.
4. **LABELS MUST BE LARGE, BOLD, AND EASY TO READ ON A PHONE** — this is critical. Use a HEAVY modern sans-serif at a GENEROUS size (think a clean textbook map seen on a small screen), DARK ink, each label sitting on a crisp solid white pill/halo so it stays sharp against the map. Prefer FEWER, BIGGER labels over many small ones. NO label may be small, thin, faint, or hard to read. Each label must read like a printed STICKER — BOLD near-black text inside a SOLID WHITE rounded box — NEVER thin, gray, or faint text floating directly on the terrain (that is the failure to avoid). Use SOLID, FILLED glyphs — NEVER outlined, hollow, or stroked-only letterforms; OUTLINED text is illegible and is the exact failure to avoid. At most SIX labels per map. Bare proper names only, spelled EXACTLY as written; add NO unlisted label and print NO color words.
5. **North is at the TOP, east to the RIGHT.** A defending line FACES the attack.
6. **NO title bar, NO caption text, NO border/frame, NO compass rose, NO scale bar, NO legend, NO N/S/E/W letters, NO ornament.** The map bleeds off all four edges.

---

**LABEL PLACEMENT — applies to EVERY label on EVERY map (critical):** Each label, together with its box/halo, must sit FULLY INSIDE the frame with a clear margin on all four sides. NO label may touch, be clipped by, or run off any edge — pull every edge-hugging name inward toward open ground. No two labels may overlap or touch each other. When a label cannot sit in clear open space right beside the place or arrow it names without colliding with another label, an arrow, troop blocks, or the edge, move it to the nearest open area and draw a THIN GRAY LEADER LINE from the label to its dot/marker so the link stays obvious. River and water labels must be SOLID and STRONG (a deep, readable blue), never pale, faint, or washed-out; if blue-on-water would be low-contrast, set the river name on open ground beside the water with a halo instead.

## Map missionary-ridge-overview

Late November 1863 — the ground at Chattanooga: the trapped Union city in the west, the long Confederate ridge to the east, and the two flanks where Grant means to win. A strategic overview, north up.

Draw the Tennessee River as a flat light-blue band curling across the upper-LEFT (northwest), and on its south bank place a small gray city block labeled "Chattanooga" filling the WEST (left) of the map. Down the entire EAST (right) side draw Missionary Ridge as a long, narrow pale-tan high-ridge band running north–south, the dominant feature — Confederate rust-red unit blocks strung along its crest facing west. In the open valley floor between the city and the ridge, west-of-center, mark a small detached pale-tan hill with a single blue block on it labeled "Orchard Knob" (Grant's vantage). At the NORTH (top) end of the ridge mark a raised pale-tan knob labeled "Tunnel Hill" with a blue block facing a rust-red block (Sherman vs Cleburne). At the SOUTH (bottom) end of the ridge mark a notch/pass through it labeled "Rossville Gap" with a blue block pressing up toward it (Hooker). Keep arrows minimal here — this is the lay of the land, not the assault.

Six labels only: Chattanooga, Missionary Ridge, Orchard Knob, Tunnel Hill, Rossville Gap.

## Map missionary-ridge

November 25, 1863 — the Army of the Cumberland charges from Orchard Knob, overruns the rifle pits at the base, then surges spontaneously straight up the slope and breaks Bragg's center on the crest. The decisive center assault, north up.

Draw Missionary Ridge as a tall pale-tan high-ridge band filling the RIGHT (east) two-thirds of the map, its crest along the right edge. Along the BASE of the ridge (its western foot, running top-to-bottom through mid-field) draw a thin line of rust-red unit blocks labeled "Rifle Pits". Along the CREST at the top of the ridge (right edge) draw a second thin line of rust-red blocks labeled "Bragg's Center". On the valley floor at the LEFT place a small pale-tan detached hill with blue blocks massed on and around it labeled "Orchard Knob". From Orchard Knob draw a broad front of bold tapered BLUE arrows sweeping EAST across the open valley into the Rifle Pits at the base; then, continuing from the base, draw multiple steep BLUE arrows bending sharply UP the slope and over the crest, punching through Bragg's Center line near the middle and splitting it (show the rust-red crest line broken/scattered where the blue arrows crest). The motion reads: west-to-east across the flat, then a hard turn straight up the ridge.

Six labels only: Chattanooga, Orchard Knob, Rifle Pits, Bragg's Center, Missionary Ridge.

## Map missionary-ridge-flanks

November 25, 1863 — the two flanks: Sherman stalled at Tunnel Hill in the north against Cleburne, Hooker coming up through Rossville Gap in the south, and Bragg's broken army streaming east toward Dalton. The flanks and the rout, north up.

Draw Missionary Ridge as a long narrow pale-tan high-ridge band running north–south through the CENTER of the map, top to bottom. At the NORTH (top) end draw a raised pale-tan knob labeled "Tunnel Hill" held by a solid rust-red block labeled "Cleburne"; from the west, draw bold BLUE arrows striking it and STOPPING short (blunted, not breaking through) labeled "Sherman". At the SOUTH (bottom) end draw a notch cutting through the ridge labeled "Rossville Gap" with a bold BLUE arrow labeled "Hooker" driving up through it onto the ridge's southern shoulder. From the broken CENTER and southern stretch of the ridge, draw several bold tapered RUST-RED retreat arrows streaming EAST off the back (right) slope of the ridge and converging toward the lower-right corner, the longest arrow tip labeled "Dalton".

Six labels only: Tunnel Hill, Cleburne, Sherman, Rossville Gap, Hooker, Dalton.

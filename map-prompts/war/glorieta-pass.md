# War map prompts — Glorieta Pass (modern battle-map house style)

Battle maps in the clean modern textbook style (Hal Jespersen / NPS look). Generated by
`scripts/generate-war-maps.mjs` (parses `## Map <slug>` sections → `public/war-img/<slug>.png`).

## Style — every battle map MUST obey these

1. **CLEAN MODERN CARTOGRAPHY** — pale parchment-white background (#f3eee2). Flat even fills only — NO paper texture, NO relief shading, NO 3-D, NO drop shadows, NO glow, NO noise.
2. **Terrain = simple flat shapes in muted color**, only what the caption names: a mountain/mesa/ridge = pale tan rounded shapes; a road or trail = a thin gray line; a ranch, town, or fort = a small gray block or dot.
3. **Forces use side colors.** Union (North) = BLUE (#1d4ed8). Confederate (South) = RUST RED (#b44d3b). A force = crisp solid rectangular unit blocks; a movement = a bold smooth tapered arrow. No other strong colors. BLUE/RUST/RED name the INK, never text. Never render a color name as a label.
4. **At most SIX labels**, clean sans-serif, dark ink, thin white halo. Bare proper names only. Spell EXACTLY as written. Add NO unlisted label, print NO color words.
5. **North is at the TOP, east to the RIGHT.** A defending line FACES the attack.
6. **NO title bar, NO caption text, NO border/frame, NO compass rose, NO scale bar, NO legend, NO N/S/E/W letters, NO ornament.** The map bleeds off all four edges.

## Map glorieta-pass

March 28, 1862 — Glorieta Pass. The Texans win the field while a Union raiding party slips over the mesa and burns their supply train in the rear. A tactical overview, north up.

Draw the Santa Fe Trail as a thin gray road winding roughly east–west across the middle of the frame, threading between pale-tan mountain shapes (the Sangre de Cristo Mountains), labeled "Santa Fe Trail". At the WEST end of the trail, draw a small gray block labeled "Johnson's Ranch" (the Confederate supply camp, at the mouth of Apache Canyon). In the CENTER, draw a small gray block labeled "Pigeon's Ranch" (the main battlefield). Off to the upper-RIGHT (northeast), draw a small gray block labeled "Fort Union" (the Union depot). Draw a broad pale-tan mesa across the SOUTH (lower) part of the frame, labeled "Glorieta Mesa".

The main battle: rust-red Confederate unit blocks pushing EAST along the trail from the Apache Canyon side toward Pigeon's Ranch (a bold rust-red arrow), with blue Union unit blocks giving ground at Pigeon's Ranch.

The decisive stroke: a bold BLUE arrow that leaves the Pigeon's Ranch area, swings SOUTH up and over Glorieta Mesa, then curves back down to the WEST and strikes "Johnson's Ranch" in the Confederate rear — labeled "Chivington".

Six labels only: Santa Fe Trail, Johnson's Ranch, Pigeon's Ranch, Fort Union, Glorieta Mesa, Chivington. Nothing else.

# War map prompts — Wilson's Creek (modern battle-map house style)

Battle maps in the clean modern textbook style (Hal Jespersen / NPS look). Generated by
`scripts/generate-war-maps.mjs` (parses `## Map <slug>` sections → `public/war-img/<slug>.png`).

## Style — every battle map MUST obey these

1. **CLEAN MODERN CARTOGRAPHY** — pale parchment-white background (#f3eee2). Flat even fills only — NO paper texture, NO relief shading, NO 3-D, NO drop shadows, NO glow, NO noise.
2. **Terrain = simple flat shapes in muted color**, only what the caption names: woods = soft sage-green; a hill/ridge/plateau = pale tan; a mountain range = a pale-tan band; water (river/creek/sea) = a flat light-blue band or fill; a road/trail = a thin gray line; a town, city, fort, church, house = a small gray block or dot.
3. **Forces use side colors.** Union (North) = BLUE (#1d4ed8). Confederate (South) = RUST RED (#b44d3b). A force/line = crisp solid rectangular unit blocks; a movement = a bold smooth tapered arrow. No other strong colors. BLUE/RUST/RED name the INK, never text. Never render a color name as a label.
4. **LABELS MUST BE LARGE, BOLD, AND EASY TO READ ON A PHONE** — this is critical. Use a HEAVY modern sans-serif at a GENEROUS size (think a clean textbook map seen on a small screen), DARK ink, each label sitting on a crisp solid white pill/halo so it stays sharp against the map. Prefer FEWER, BIGGER labels over many small ones. NO label may be small, thin, faint, or hard to read. Each label must read like a printed STICKER — BOLD near-black text inside a SOLID WHITE rounded box — NEVER thin, gray, or faint text floating directly on the terrain (that is the failure to avoid). Use SOLID, FILLED glyphs — NEVER outlined, hollow, or stroked-only letterforms; OUTLINED text is illegible and is the exact failure to avoid. At most SIX labels per map. Bare proper names only, spelled EXACTLY as written; add NO unlisted label and print NO color words.
5. **North is at the TOP, east to the RIGHT.** A defending line FACES the attack.
6. **NO title bar, NO caption text, NO border/frame, NO compass rose, NO scale bar, NO legend, NO N/S/E/W letters, NO ornament.** The map bleeds off all four edges.

## Map wilsons-creek-plan

The night march, August 9–10, 1861 — Lyon splits his small army outside Springfield and sends the halves on a converging dawn attack. A strategic overview, north up.

Place a small gray town block labeled "Springfield" in the upper-right (northeast) corner. Draw the "Wire Road" as a thin gray road running from Springfield diagonally down to the lower-left (the southwest). Draw "Wilson's Creek" as a flat light-blue band running north–south in the lower-left third of the frame, with the Wire Road crossing it; cluster a few solid rust-red unit blocks along the creek to mark the Confederate camp.

From Springfield, draw Lyon's main column as a bold BLUE arrow that runs southwest down toward the creek and curls in to strike the camp from the north, labeled "Lyon". Draw Sigel's column as a second bold blue arrow that branches off near Springfield, loops in a wide clockwise sweep south and around the bottom of the frame, and comes up to strike the camp from the south, labeled "Sigel".

Six labels only: Springfield, Wire Road, Wilson's Creek, Lyon, Sigel. Nothing else.

## Map wilsons-creek

Dawn, August 10, 1861 — the fight for Bloody Hill. Lyon's main column drives onto the plateau; Price counterattacks uphill. A tactical close-up, north up.

Draw "Wilson's Creek" as a flat light-blue band running roughly north–south down the left of the frame. Draw the "Wire Road" as a thin gray road crossing the creek at a ford and running up toward the upper-right. Place a broad pale-tan plateau labeled "Bloody Hill" on the creek's east bank in the center of the frame, ringed with soft sage-green brush.

Set a row of solid BLUE Union unit blocks along the crest of Bloody Hill facing south. Draw Lyon's attack as a bold blue arrow coming down from the north onto the hilltop, labeled "Lyon". Cluster solid rust-red Confederate blocks south and below the hill and draw bold rust-red arrows surging UPHILL into the blue line; label the rust force "Price".

Six labels only: Wilson's Creek, Wire Road, Bloody Hill, Lyon, Price. Nothing else.

## Map wilsons-creek-sigel

Mid-morning, August 10, 1861 — Sigel's column is overrun south of the creek. His flanking force is shattered by McCulloch's counterattack at the Sharp farm. A tactical close-up, north up.

Draw "Wilson's Creek" as a flat light-blue band running east–west across the upper part of the frame. Draw the "Wire Road" as a thin gray road crossing the creek and running down toward the lower-left. Mark a small gray house block labeled "Sharp Farm" in the lower-center, just south of the creek.

Show Sigel's blue column as a row of solid BLUE unit blocks strung loosely across the Wire Road near the Sharp farm, labeled "Sigel". Drive a bold rust-red arrow down into the flank of that line from the east, with a row of solid rust-red blocks behind it, labeled "McCulloch". Draw a broken blue arrow breaking away to the rear (toward the lower-left, back up the Wire Road) to show the rout.

Six labels only: Wilson's Creek, Wire Road, Sharp Farm, Sigel, McCulloch. Nothing else.

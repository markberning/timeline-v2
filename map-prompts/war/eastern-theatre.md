# War map prompts — Eastern Theatre (strategic corridor map)

A theatre-level strategic map (not a single battle). Same clean flat-schematic
house style as the battle maps, but it may carry more named places because it is
an overview of a whole theatre. Generated by `scripts/generate-war-maps.mjs`.

## Style — every map MUST obey these (read before drawing)

**CRITICAL MAP RULES:**

1. **This is a clean, flat, schematic strategic map — NOT a topographic or terrain map.** Draw NO contour lines, NO hill shading, NO relief, NO textured ground, NO forest stippling, NO elevation tinting. The base is a single flat pale tan (#e7ddc8) land area and, where water is shown, a single flat light blue.
2. **Use side colors.** Union (United States / North) markers and arrows are BLUE (#1d4ed8). Confederate (South) markers and arrows are RUST RED (#b44d3b). A movement is a bold tapered arrow in that side's color. The words BLUE, RUST, and RED name the ink to use — they are instructions to you, NEVER text to print. Never render a color name as a label.
3. **Few, legible labels.** Each label is a bare proper name only, in a clean sans-serif, dark ink, with a thin white halo. State names are in gray. This strategic map may carry up to ~12 named places — no more. Never write a sentence on the map.
4. **No road network, no creeks, no railroads, no county lines** beyond what is named below.
5. **Spell every label EXACTLY as written below. Invent no words, no extra labels, no annotations.**
6. **North is at the top. East is to the right.** Place everything in correct real-world orientation.
7. **NO title bar, NO chapter number, NO caption text on the image.** Render no header bar.
8. **NO frame.** Draw no border, box, rectangle, or hairline. The map bleeds off all four edges.
9. **No ornaments, no compass rose, no scale bar, no legend, no north arrow, no inset.** Do not print the words North/South/East/West or the letters N/S/E/W. Do not draw a decorative star-of-the-compass or sparkle.

## Map eastern-theatre-corridor

A clean schematic strategic map of the Eastern Theatre of the American Civil War — the contested corridor between the two capitals, 1861–1865.

Show three soft horizontal state bands, each labeled in gray small-caps: "PENNSYLVANIA" across the top, "MARYLAND" in a thin band below it, and "VIRGINIA" filling the lower two-thirds of the map.

Place the two capitals on a near-vertical axis down the middle:
- "Washington" as a small BLUE five-point star, on the Maryland–Virginia border (upper-middle).
- "Richmond" as a small RUST five-point star, in central Virginia, lower down.
Draw one thin dashed gray line connecting Washington and Richmond, with a small bracket labeled "110 miles" beside it — the contested corridor.

Place small dark battle-site dots, each with a bare name label:
- "Gettysburg" — up in Pennsylvania, near the top.
- "Antietam" — in Maryland, west of Washington.
- "Fredericksburg" and "Chancellorsville" — in Virginia, between the two capitals.
- "Petersburg" — just south of Richmond.
- "Appomattox" — to the lower-left (southwest Virginia).

Show Lee's two invasions of the North as two bold RUST arrows sweeping up out of Virginia, crossing the Potomac: one curving north to Antietam, the longer one reaching all the way to Gettysburg.

Twelve labels only: PENNSYLVANIA, MARYLAND, VIRGINIA, Washington, Richmond, Gettysburg, Antietam, Fredericksburg, Chancellorsville, Petersburg, Appomattox, 110 miles. Nothing else.

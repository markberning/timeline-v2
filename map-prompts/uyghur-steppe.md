## GLOBAL RULES FOR ALL UYGHUR & QARA-KHITAI STEPPE PROMPTS — read before drawing any map in this file

**CRITICAL RULES — read carefully before drawing:**

1. **Label cleanly.** Spell every label correctly. A long river MAY be labeled more than once along its course (normal cartography) as long as each instance is correct and the repeats are well separated — not touching, stacked, or adjacent. Do NOT duplicate a city/site/region label, do NOT place two copies of a label next to each other, and do NOT repeat an annotation/caption.
2. **Spell every label exactly as written in this prompt.** Do not invent words. Do not abbreviate. Do not generate any word that is not in the prompt text.
3. **Include the exact site name on every label.** The site name is the word or phrase before the first parenthesis or em-dash. Never drop a site name. The parenthetical after a site name is placement guidance for you — it is NOT label text and must NOT be drawn.
4. **North is at the top of the map. East is to the right.** Place every location according to its real-world geography in this orientation. Do not rotate or tilt the map.
5. **All water is light blue.** Seas, lakes, and rivers are rendered in light blue. Do not render water as tan, cream, or white. Land is beige/tan; desert is pale yellow; steppe grassland is soft green; high mountains are gray-brown.
6. **Do not invent any labels, annotations, sites, or features that are not in this prompt.** If it is not in the prompt, do not draw it.
7. **Geography correct & readable.** Place features in their correct real-world locations. Faint modern country/region outlines or labels as orientation aids are *welcome*. Real geography defects = invented/misspelled place names, wrong locations, or labels contradicting the prompt — NOT the presence of reference outlines.
8. **No compass words as labels.** Never print the words North, South, East, West (or the letters N/S/E/W) anywhere on the image.
9. **No ornamental glyphs.** No decorative star, four-point sparkle, twinkle, compass-rose flourish, or corner ornament.

The chapter title "Chapter N: <Title>" should appear at the top of the map image, centered, in a clean serif font, inside a header bar that spans the full width of the image. The header bar is the ONLY bordered element of the image. Draw NO border, outline, frame, rectangle, or hairline anywhere else — the map and its terrain must bleed off all four outer edges. Never enclose the map in a rectangle. Render the chapter title **exactly once**, only inside that single full-width header bar.

**UYGHUR & QARA-KHITAI STEPPE ORIENTATION RULES:**

1. North is at the top. East is to the right. This is the Inner Asian theatre — Mongolia, the Tarim Basin, and Central Asia — not a single country.
2. The **Mongolian Plateau** is the open grassland in the NORTH-CENTER. The **Orkhon** River valley, with Ötüken and Ordu-Baliq, sits in the heart of it (in modern Mongolia). The **Yenisei Kyrgyz** homeland is the forest-steppe directly NORTH of the Orkhon, in southern Siberia.
3. **China** lies to the SOUTHEAST: the North China plain with Chang'an and Luoyang, behind the line of the Great Wall and the Ordos loop of the Yellow River.
4. The **Tarim Basin** is the great oval desert (the Taklamakan) in the CENTER-SOUTH. Its oasis towns ring the desert: **Turfan** and **Beshbalik** on the NORTHEAST rim, **Kashgar** on the WEST rim, **Dunhuang** at the EAST entrance. The **Gansu Corridor** runs SOUTHEAST from the Tarim's eastern end toward China, with **Ganzhou** along it.
5. The **Tian Shan** is the high mountain range running west-to-east that separates the Tarim Basin (to its south) from the Ili and Chu river valleys and **Lake Balkhash** (to its north).
6. **Transoxiana** is in the SOUTHWEST: the land between the **Syr Darya** and **Amu Darya** rivers, with **Samarkand** and **Bukhara**. **Balasagun** is in the Chu valley north of the Tian Shan. **Khwarazm** is at the Amu Darya delta by the Aral Sea, in the far WEST. Khurasan and the route to Baghdad lie off the SOUTHWEST edge.
7. The northern neighbor on the steppe is era-specific and given per chapter (the Second Türk Khaganate, then the Yenisei Kyrgyz, then the rising Mongols). China to the southeast is era-specific (Tang, then Song/Jin). Always keep the Orkhon NORTH-CENTER, the Tarim CENTER-SOUTH, Transoxiana SOUTHWEST, China SOUTHEAST.

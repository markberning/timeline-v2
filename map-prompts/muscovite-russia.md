## GLOBAL RULES FOR ALL MUSCOVITE RUSSIA PROMPTS — read before drawing any map in this file

**CRITICAL RULES — read carefully before drawing:**

1. **Label cleanly.** Spell every label correctly. A long river or coast MAY be labeled more than once along its course (normal cartography) as long as each instance is correct and the repeats are well separated — not touching, stacked, or adjacent. Do NOT duplicate a city/site/region label, do NOT place two copies of a label next to each other, and do NOT repeat an annotation/caption.
2. **Spell every label exactly as written in this prompt.** Do not invent words. Do not abbreviate. Do not generate any word that is not in the prompt text.
3. **Include the exact site name on every label.** The site name is the word or phrase before the first parenthesis or em-dash. Never drop a site name. The parenthetical after a site name is placement guidance for you — it is NOT label text and must NOT be drawn.
4. **North is at the top of the map. East is to the right.** Place every location according to its real-world geography in this orientation. Do not rotate or tilt the map.
5. **All water is light blue.** The seas, gulfs, lakes, and rivers are rendered in light blue. Do not render water as tan, cream, or white. Land is beige/tan; the dense northern forest is soft green; the open southern steppe grassland is pale yellow-green; high ground is gray-brown.
6. **Do not invent any labels, annotations, sites, or features that are not in this prompt.** If it is not in the prompt, do not draw it.
7. **Geography correct & readable.** Place features in their correct real-world locations. Faint modern country/region outlines or labels as orientation aids are *welcome*. Real geography defects = invented/misspelled place names, wrong locations, or labels contradicting the prompt — NOT the presence of reference outlines.
8. **No compass words as labels.** Never print the words North, South, East, West (or the letters N/S/E/W) anywhere on the image.
9. **No ornamental glyphs.** No decorative star, four-point sparkle, twinkle, compass-rose flourish, or corner ornament.

The chapter title "Chapter N: <Title>" should appear at the top of the map image, centered, in a clean serif font, inside a header bar that spans the full width of the image. The header bar is the ONLY bordered element of the image. Draw NO border, outline, frame, rectangle, or hairline anywhere else — the map and its water must bleed off all four outer edges. Never enclose the map in a rectangle. Render the chapter title **exactly once**, only inside that single full-width header bar.

**MUSCOVITE RUSSIA ORIENTATION RULES:**

1. North is at the top. East is to the right. This is the East European / Russian theatre: the forested Russian heartland in the center, the open steppe spreading south, the seas ringing the edges.
2. **Moscow** sits at the CENTER of the map, in the northeastern forest on the **Moskva River**. The river-veined forest country around it holds **Vladimir**, **Suzdal**, and **Tver** (upriver on the upper Volga, northwest of Moscow).
3. The **Volga River** is the great river of the map: it rises northwest of Moscow, swings east and then makes a large bend southeast, runs down to **Kazan** and far down to **Astrakhan** at its mouth on the **Caspian Sea** in the far southeast. The **Oka River** runs south and east of Moscow and joins the Volga; **Kolomna** sits where the Moskva meets the Oka. The **Ugra River** is a small tributary southwest of Moscow. The **Don River** runs south through the steppe toward the **Sea of Azov** (Kulikovo Field lies near its upper reaches).
4. To the NORTHWEST: **Novgorod** on the **Volkhov River** beside **Lake Ilmen**, with **Pskov** further west and **Lake Peipus** on the western frontier. To the SOUTHWEST: the **Dnieper River** running down to the steppe, with **Kiev** on it; **Smolensk** sits on the upper Dnieper west of Moscow.
5. To the NORTH: the **White Sea** with **Arkhangelsk** at its shore. To the NORTHWEST coast: the **Baltic Sea** and the **Gulf of Finland**, with **Narva** on its eastern shore and the future site of **Saint Petersburg** at the mouth of the **Neva River**.
6. To the SOUTH: the open **steppe** grassland, the **Crimea** peninsula on the **Black Sea**, and **Sarai** on the lower Volga (the capital of the Golden Horde). To the WEST lies **Lithuania** / **Poland** (the Polish-Lithuanian Commonwealth). To the EAST, beyond the **Ural Mountains**, lies **Siberia**.
7. Always keep Moscow at the center, the Volga sweeping from the northwest down to the southeast and the Caspian, the steppe and Crimea SOUTH, the White Sea NORTH, the Baltic NORTHWEST, Lithuania/Poland WEST, and Siberia/the Urals EAST.

## GLOBAL RULES FOR ALL ISLAMIC PERSIA PROMPTS — read before drawing any map in this file

**CRITICAL RULES — read carefully before drawing:**

1. **Label cleanly.** Spell every label correctly. A long river or coast MAY be labeled more than once along its course (normal cartography) as long as each instance is correct and the repeats are well separated — not touching, stacked, or adjacent. Do NOT duplicate a city/site/region label, do NOT place two copies of a label next to each other, and do NOT repeat an annotation/caption.
2. **Spell every label exactly as written in this prompt.** Do not invent words. Do not abbreviate. Do not generate any word that is not in the prompt text.
3. **Include the exact site name on every label.** The site name is the word or phrase before the first parenthesis or em-dash. Never drop a site name. The parenthetical after a site name is placement guidance for you — it is NOT label text and must NOT be drawn.
4. **North is at the top of the map. East is to the right.** Place every location according to its real-world geography in this orientation. Do not rotate or tilt the map.
5. **All water is light blue.** The Caspian Sea, the Persian Gulf, the Aral Sea, the Mediterranean, and rivers are rendered in light blue. Do not render water as tan, cream, or white. Land is beige/tan; desert is pale yellow; river valleys and irrigated land are soft green; mountains are gray-brown.
6. **Do not invent any labels, annotations, sites, or features that are not in this prompt.** If it is not in the prompt, do not draw it.
7. **Geography correct & readable.** Place features in their correct real-world locations. Faint modern country/region outlines or labels as orientation aids are *welcome*. Real geography defects = invented/misspelled place names, wrong locations, or labels contradicting the prompt — NOT the presence of reference outlines.
8. **No compass words as labels.** Never print the words North, South, East, West (or the letters N/S/E/W) anywhere on the image.
9. **No ornamental glyphs.** No decorative star, four-point sparkle, twinkle, compass-rose flourish, or corner ornament.

The chapter title "Chapter N: <Title>" should appear at the top of the map image, centered, in a clean serif font, inside a header bar that spans the full width of the image. The header bar is the ONLY bordered element of the image. Draw NO border, outline, frame, rectangle, or hairline anywhere else — the map and its water must bleed off all four outer edges. Never enclose the map in a rectangle. Render the chapter title **exactly once**, only inside that single full-width header bar.

**ISLAMIC PERSIA ORIENTATION RULES:**

1. North is at the top. East is to the right. This is the Iranian plateau and its surroundings: a great tableland walled by mountains, ringed by seas and rivers, sitting between Mesopotamia in the west and Central Asia in the northeast.
2. The **Zagros Mountains** run northwest-to-southeast as a long parallel ridge system down the western side of the plateau, separating the Mesopotamian lowland from the high interior. The **Elburz Mountains** run east-to-west along the northern edge of the plateau, walling off the southern shore of the **Caspian Sea**, the large inland sea in the north.
3. The **Persian Gulf** is the body of water in the south, between the plateau and Arabia. The **Aral Sea** is a smaller inland sea in the far northeast, fed by the Amu Darya.
4. To the WEST lies **Mesopotamia** — the land between the **Tigris** and **Euphrates** rivers (both flowing northwest-to-southeast toward the Persian Gulf) — with **Baghdad** on the Tigris and the ruined **Ctesiphon** just downstream from it. The Mediterranean coast (Syria) is at the far northwest edge.
5. To the NORTHEAST lies **Khurasan** (the great northeastern province) and beyond it **Transoxiana** — "the land beyond the Oxus" — between the **Amu Darya** (the Oxus) and the Syr Darya. The major northeastern cities are **Bukhara** and **Samarkand** in Transoxiana, and **Merv** and **Herat** along the road south.
6. Key plateau cities: **Nishapur** (northeast Khurasan), **Isfahan** (center), **Shiraz** (south), **Rayy** (north-center, near modern Tehran), **Tabriz** (northwest), **Maragheh** (northwest, near Tabriz), **Ghazni** (far east, in modern Afghanistan), and the mountain fortress **Alamut** (in the Elburz, south of the Caspian).
7. To the NORTH and NORTH-NORTHEAST lies the open **steppe** of Central Asia — the grassland off which the Seljuk Turks, the Mongols, and Timur all came down into Persia.
8. To the NORTHWEST lies **Anatolia** (modern Turkey, former Byzantine land), with **Manzikert** in its east and **Ankara** in its center.
9. Always keep the Zagros walling the west, the Elburz and Caspian across the north, the Persian Gulf in the south, Mesopotamia and Baghdad to the west, Khurasan and Transoxiana to the northeast, and Anatolia to the northwest.

## GLOBAL RULES FOR ALL SWAHILI COAST PROMPTS — read before drawing any map in this file

**CRITICAL RULES — read carefully before drawing:**

1. **Label cleanly.** Spell every label correctly. A long river or coast MAY be labeled more than once along its course (normal cartography) as long as each instance is correct and the repeats are well separated — not touching, stacked, or adjacent. Do NOT duplicate a city/site/region label, do NOT place two copies of a label next to each other, and do NOT repeat an annotation/caption.
2. **Spell every label exactly as written in this prompt.** Do not invent words. Do not abbreviate. Do not generate any word that is not in the prompt text.
3. **Include the exact site name on every label.** The site name is the word or phrase before the first parenthesis or em-dash. Never drop a site name. The parenthetical after a site name is placement guidance for you — it is NOT label text and must NOT be drawn.
4. **North is at the top of the map. East is to the right.** Place every location according to its real-world geography in this orientation. Do not rotate or tilt the map.
5. **All water is light blue.** The Indian Ocean, the Red Sea, the Persian Gulf, channels, and rivers are rendered in light blue. Do not render water as tan, cream, or white. Land is beige/tan; desert is pale yellow; the interior plateau and coastal forest are soft green; high ground is gray-brown.
6. **Do not invent any labels, annotations, sites, or features that are not in this prompt.** If it is not in the prompt, do not draw it.
7. **Geography correct & readable.** Place features in their correct real-world locations. Faint modern country/region outlines or labels as orientation aids are *welcome*. Real geography defects = invented/misspelled place names, wrong locations, or labels contradicting the prompt — NOT the presence of reference outlines.
8. **No compass words as labels.** Never print the words North, South, East, West (or the letters N/S/E/W) anywhere on the image.
9. **No ornamental glyphs.** No decorative star, four-point sparkle, twinkle, compass-rose flourish, or corner ornament.

The chapter title "Chapter N: <Title>" should appear at the top of the map image, centered, in a clean serif font, inside a header bar that spans the full width of the image. The header bar is the ONLY bordered element of the image. Draw NO border, outline, frame, rectangle, or hairline anywhere else — the map and its water must bleed off all four outer edges. Never enclose the map in a rectangle. Render the chapter title **exactly once**, only inside that single full-width header bar.

**SWAHILI COAST ORIENTATION RULES:**

1. North is at the top. East is to the right. This is the western Indian Ocean theatre: the East African coast and its islands on the LEFT (west), the open Indian Ocean filling the RIGHT (east).
2. The **East African coast** runs north-to-south down the left of the map. From north to south the coastal towns are: **Mogadishu** (far north, in modern Somalia), then **Pate** and **Lamu** (the Lamu archipelago), **Malindi**, **Mombasa**, **Gedi** (just inland near Malindi), the islands **Pemba** and **Zanzibar** (just off the coast in modern Tanzania), **Kilwa** (an island off the southern Tanzanian coast), **Songo Mnara** (a small island just south of Kilwa), and **Sofala** (far south, in modern Mozambique).
3. The **Indian Ocean** is the great body of water filling the entire eastern (right) side of the map.
4. The interior to the WEST is the African mainland: soft-green coastal forest near the shore, rising to the high inland **Zimbabwe Plateau** in the SOUTHWEST (the gold source behind Sofala). Do not draw interior cities unless the chapter names them.
5. **Arabia** is in the far NORTHEAST: the Red Sea and the Gulf of Aden in the north, **Aden** at the mouth of the Red Sea, and **Muscat** on the southeast Arabian coast (the Oman coast) further east. The **Persian Gulf** is north of that.
6. **India** lies across the ocean to the EAST: show the southwest Indian coast at the far right edge with **Calicut** when a chapter names it. **Madagascar** is the large island in the SOUTHEAST, across the channel from the southern coast; the **Comoros** are the small islands between the southern coast and Madagascar.
7. The **monsoon** is the wind system over the ocean: when a chapter calls for it, show two labeled curved arrows — one "Northeast monsoon (Dec–Mar)" blowing from the northeast toward Africa, one "Southwest monsoon (Apr–Sep)" blowing from the southwest back toward Arabia and India. Keep arrows over open water, well clear of town labels.
8. Always keep the African coast on the LEFT running north–south, the Indian Ocean filling the RIGHT, Arabia NORTHEAST, India EAST, Madagascar SOUTHEAST, the Zimbabwe Plateau interior SOUTHWEST.

**SPECIFIC REMINDERS for this chapter:**

- **Mogadishu** sits on the EAST-FACING Indian Ocean coast at the top of the African shore (the outer Horn of Africa coast that faces the ocean), NOT on the northern Gulf-of-Aden coast and NOT inland.
- **Sofala** sits far south on the CONTINUOUS mainland African coastline. Do NOT draw an invented peninsula, cape, or island for it — it is a point on the smooth north–south coast.
